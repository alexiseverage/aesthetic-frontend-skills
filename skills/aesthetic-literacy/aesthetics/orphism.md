# Orphism

Orphism organizes color relationships, unequal measures and intersecting forms into visual rhythm. Its best-known vocabulary includes concentric disks and arcs, alongside prismatic planes, striated waves, vertical bands and applied patterns. A rainbow circle alone does not establish the aesthetic.

## Scope

Use for color-composition tools, cultural publishing, original editorial artwork, music-program information and bounded illustrative surfaces where relationships between color and form can carry identity. Keep practical reading and controls stable while the visual anchor expresses rhythm.

The early modernist category is associated most clearly with Robert and Sonia Delaunay. Their Simultanisme extends across painting, poetry, textile, fashion and theater. Selected Kupka and Picabia works contribute different investigations; their affiliations and neighboring influences must remain distinct. A broad exhibition or an artist's name does not classify an entire oeuvre. Later continuations retain their dates. The interface is a contemporary interpretation, not a recovered historical system.

## 7-Dimension Profile

**Palette**: Organize adjacent hues and values to reinforce or interrupt forms. Complementary and clashing colors, neutral intervals and unequal color areas are common. Pale, muted, two-color and monochrome studies are documented alternatives. Choose a coherent branch and role hierarchy; saturated rainbow color and a fixed primary palette are optional. Historical hex values and whole-interface contrast cannot be inferred from reproduction pixels.

**Type**: Original display lettering can participate through variable width, weight, size, color and arrangement, as in the announcement, poem-dress and illustrated-book branch. Paintings often have only secondary signatures. Pair expressive display treatment with plain, readable support text. No single historical font defines the category; do not turn signatures into a UI alphabet.

**Texture**: Paint, ink and material handling can remain visible: scumbling, loaded gouache strokes, translucent watercolor, stencil edges, silk weave and block-print variation. Flatter print versions are also established. Apply restrained texture inside artwork rather than making every surface distressed. Aging and reproduction artifacts are not mandatory design rules.

**Shape**: Unequal repeated measures, interlocking or intersecting forms and directional changes carry the color system. Disks, interrupted arcs and pie-like divisions are a substantial branch, with planes, waves, strips, stepped bands, angular rails and organic later forms as alternatives. Avoid one centered target repeated across every component.

**Motion**: Static compositions can suggest rotation, pulsation or rocking. Sources establish depicted rhythm, not actual animation, easing or UI timing. Intentional stillness and immediate control updates are suitable proposals. Any optional movement must be nonessential, avoid flashing and respect reduced motion. Musical association does not require autoplay, sound or a color-to-note mapping.

**Spatial**: Compose through displacement, unequal scale, overlap, varied band widths and directional tension. Dense fields and sparse studies both occur; formats include tall, oval and circular paintings, a narrow announcement and an unfolded accordion book. Choose an original arrangement and preserve its relationships as the layout stacks. Generic centered symmetry and uniformly spaced modules lose much of the rhythm.

**Cultural markers**: Early-1910s Paris and transnational exchange, Apollinaire's Orpheus naming, color theory, modern light, musical/poetic analogy and Delaunay Simultanisme across everyday design. Kupka and Picabia retain their own concerns. Synchromism's color-harmony theories and neighboring Cubist/Futurist investigations are relations, not universal Orphism rules.

## Non-Negotiables

**Non-negotiables**:

- Color relationships actively organize form or rhythm rather than merely decorate a neutral template.
- Unequal measures, repetition, overlap or directional tension create an intentional composition.
- A grounded historical branch and its boundaries survive translation into an original contemporary surface.

Circles, full saturation, material distress, total abstraction and actual animation are common or variant signals, not universal requirements.

## Connotation

**Mode:** contemporary revival.

Suggest lyrical experimentation, sensory relationships and exchange across art and useful design. Preserve the difference between poetic visual rhythm and a claimed scientific or musical mapping. A new interface may demonstrate color composition without pretending to be a period artifact or an authenticated theory lesson.

## Related / Subsets

- `op-art`: apparent movement and optical contrast overlap, but later illusion-focused precision is a different emphasis. Orphism can retain brushy material, poetic context and applied design without a vibrating optical field.
- `de-stijl`: unequal planar balance overlaps. Its canonical orthogonality is narrower than Orphism's arcs, prismatic fragmentation, overlap and varied materials. Late angular Kupka works require historical context to distinguish them.
- `psychedelic`: color intensity and curves overlap; later countercultural lettering and imagery have another lineage.
- `bauhaus`: geometry and applied design overlap; a general workshop or function-first visual system is not automatically Orphism.
- Delaunay disk/arc, early planar, Kupka wave/vertical, Picabia interwoven-form and Simultané applied-design branches are prose guidance, not additional catalog slugs.
- Cubism, Futurism and Neo-Impressionism are source-supported influences and neighbors. Synchromism is a distinct related inquiry; do not silently merge its name or pitch theories into this entry.

## Frontend / UI Guidance

Give the page one strong original composition with secondary rhythm in section markers, diagrams or display lettering. Use neutral surfaces for labels, inputs and reading. Controls should change meaningful content: a selected composition, color arrangement, program or saved draft, with explicit state and keyboard focus.

For a color studio, a palette selection can change actual artwork fills while a composition selection changes its geometry. Describe these as original contemporary studies. Pair color swatches with names and selection markers. A music-program surface can use abstract artwork without requiring audio. Local demonstrations should state their persistence scope. Preserve art proportions, legible captions and controls at narrow widths.

## CSS Translation

- Proposed application roles: paper `#F3ECD9`, surface `#FFF9E9`, ink `#252638`, muted text `#5A5967`, blue action `#2548A3`, ember secondary `#B33F32` and yellow accent `#E5BD3E`. These are contemporary choices, not sampled historical pigments or a universal palette.
- Use original SVG geometry with explicit color roles, unequal arcs/planes and controlled overlaps. Avoid tracing a historical canvas or depending on a universal centered radial gradient.
- Keep controls primarily rectangular with limited rule weights; reserve curves and pattern for the artwork. Separate essential labels from color intersections and texture.
- Use an asymmetrical artwork/control grid with generous internal margins. Stack on narrow screens while preserving composition proportions and reading order; implementation breakpoints are proposed values.
- Make selection immediate. Stillness is valid; disable any optional nonessential animation under `prefers-reduced-motion`. Do not imply that static references establish CSS timing.

## Typography / Fonts

Use a small original display treatment with variable letter color or width for the main anchor, supported by a system sans for controls and captions. A readable serif such as Georgia can support editorial explanation. These are proposed contemporary choices, not identified period typefaces. Keep essential text as text, avoid uncertain stencil-font claims, and do not reproduce source lettering, inscriptions or signatures.

## Cultural / Ethical Notes

Attribute artists, institutions and distinct contexts. Preserve Sonia Delaunay's independent painting, design and commercial practice. Distinguish association from affiliation and a later continuation from the early movement. Museum reference media need separate rights handling; create original geometry and retain asset provenance. Do not claim a historical pitch mapping, animation or original paint color without evidence. Test control contrast and focus independently of artwork color relationships.

## Anti-Patterns

- Rainbow targets or spinning circles with no purposeful color/form relationship.
- Treating every associated artist, broad-exhibition participant or geometric painting as Orphism.
- Mandatory saturation, circles, flat vectors, dense coverage or nonrepresentational content.
- Backdating later works, or assigning a screenprint an unsupported production date.
- Copying a named painting, book composition, lettering or signature into supposedly original assets.
- Inventing historical fonts, hex values, animation timing, responsive behavior or musical mappings.
- Color-only selection, unreadable text at intersections, flashing patterns or autoplay justified only by musical analogy.

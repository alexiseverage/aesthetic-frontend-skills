# Anatomical Flap Atlases

Printed anatomical diagrams with superimposed paper parts that disclose related structures or alternate states through local hinges.

Evidence basis: [the research profile](../../../knowledge/aesthetics/anatomical-flap-atlases.md) records 30 qualifying unique artifacts, source distribution, claim support and limitations. This is a descriptive catalog category. Exact historical colors, fonts and screen timings are not inferred from appearance; proposed frontend values below are contemporary translations.

## Scope

Printed anatomical diagrams with superimposed paper parts that disclose related structures or alternate states through local hinges. Single sheets and bound atlases share registration and meaningful reveal. An antique anatomy image or generic page turn alone is insufficient.

## 7-Dimension Profile

**Palette**: Light paper and dark print with localized anatomical color; monochrome early sheets, saturated household manikins and restrained specialist sections coexist. Sepia is not universal.

**Type**: Plate numbers, keyed parts and explanatory text. Latin serif, German labels and blackletter vary by work. Modern labels should be readable rather than reproducing tiny print or assuming one illustrator from the author name.

**Texture**: Contour-cut paper, pale reverses, hatch/stipple, thin edges and small local shadows. Creases, stains and missing parts are copy condition rather than required distress.

**Shape**: Nested body/organ-contoured flaps on local hinges, plus circular and rectangular-topped variants. A whole figure is optional; a page may mix interactive and static vignettes.

**Motion**: Actual filmed handling shows reversible upward/downward/lateral folding while surrounding print stays fixed. Some overlays compare alternate positions rather than depth. Handler pace, video crossfades and modern GIF labels do not establish intrinsic historic timing.

**Spatial**: Registered anatomy within a stable explanatory field. Dense text columns, ornate marginal tableaux, isolated foldouts and airy multi-figure plates are variants. Do not discard context on every reveal.

**Cultural markers**: Anatomical publishing, material teaching devices and historical print engineering. Historical audiences, dates and anonymous artists can be uncertain; source anatomy is not current clinical authority.

## Non-Negotiables

**Non-negotiables**:

- A registered anatomical diagram and stable surrounding context.
- A local layered disclosure related to the underlying content.
- A meaningful correspondence between revealed part and explanation.

## Frontend / UI Guidance

Pair an original schematic figure with numbered disclosure controls and matching explanation. Keep a stable outer frame, show which layer is open, allow close/reset and provide text for every revealed part. Native accordion buttons work by keyboard; mobile stacks figure and text without clipping. Reduced motion switches layers immediately.

## CSS Translation

Cream paper, dark rules, restrained coral/bone/blue and local paper-back/edge shadows. Any optional hinge transition should originate at its actual attachment rather than spin the entire figure. Treat its duration as proposed; keep reading order stable.

## Cultural / Ethical Notes

Use original artistic schematics without clinical or procedural claims. Preserve missing/replaced flap distinctions, uncertain dates and anonymous artist credit. Historical sexed figures do not define all current bodies; no diagnostic interpretation is implied.

## Anti-Patterns

- Whole-image crossfade or carousel without registered local layers.
- Unrelated content behind decorative peel effects.
- Claiming edited video pace is historic movement or current medical knowledge.

# Cute Arcade Puzzle Boards

A descriptive name for a game-design vernacular coupling a small, readable puzzle board with an expressive cartoon world. Kickle Cubicle, Eggerland/Lolo, Pengo, Bomberman, Puzzle Boy and Quinty supply different branches. Its printed box and promotional art is as useful as its game screens: polished rounded lettering and oversized characters can frame a disciplined grid without making every element pixel art.

## Scope

Use for playful planning, collections, small commerce flows and game-adjacent tools where position, selection and progress matter. Retain an understandable bounded board, meaningful objects and friendly character-scale hierarchy. Pixel rendering, ice, balloons and one franchise's characters are not universal requirements. Flipull's abstract packaging demonstrates why a puzzle-game label alone is insufficient.

## 7-Dimension Profile

**Palette**: Saturated blue, red, yellow and green separated into actor, floor, obstacle and HUD roles; cream/white outer grounds are common in packaging. Handheld monochrome is a valid hardware branch. Cold cyan ice can sit beside warm golden tilework and red highlights.

**Type**: Short bitmap labels on native game screens contrast with large illustrated display lettering on packages. Kickle's tied balloon letters, Lolo 3's glossy extruded forms and Puzzle Boy's inflated pink title are specific packaging options, not interchangeable mandatory fonts.

**Texture**: Native pixels, repeated floor tiles and small patterned water marks in games; airbrush volume, hard contours, specular streaks and print grain in illustrated art. Keep these layers distinguishable. A blurred screenshot is not an authentic texture treatment.

**Shape**: Orthogonal cells, raised blocks, stepped island edges, compact collectible symbols and round expressive characters. Packaging can use tilted picture windows, bubble titles, toy buildings and comic bursts around the board logic.

**Motion**: Grid-following movement, object displacement, collection and short scene changes are observed. Inspected captures do not establish universal timing or easing. Proposed web feedback can use a short stepped displacement; suppress travel, bounce and ambient loops under reduced motion while preserving state changes.

**Spatial**: One legible task surface bounded by water, masonry or a frame; counters sit in a top strip or narrow side rail. Illustrated promotional layers use exaggerated foreground scale and receding tile planes. Dense rooms and sparse islands are both supported.

**Cultural markers**: Action puzzles, collectible objectives, level/room labels, lives and power indicators, cheerful enemies and illustrated cartridge-era packaging. Treat names and characters as historical references, not reusable production assets.

## Non-Negotiables

**Non-negotiables**: A bounded cell-based task surface; clearly differentiated actor/object/obstacle/state roles; cartoon playfulness in shape and hierarchy. A package-led treatment must remain connected to the board's spatial logic.

## Connotation

**Mode:** nostalgic quotation or contemporary revival. The effect is toy-like invitation and manageable problem solving. Original game interfaces were functional constrained media; today's use should preserve their clarity without imposing a game on an unrelated essential task.

## Frontend / UI Guidance

Make cells useful: selectable destinations, slots, collection items or route steps. Place ordinary form fields and a visible summary beside the board. Label cells, expose selection with shape and text, and provide keyboard focus and a list alternative when spatial navigation is complex. Packaging-inspired headers and original small mascots should support the task rather than obscure it.

## CSS Translation

Proposed application values, not measured historical tokens: paper `#fffdf4`, ink `#172b45`, blue `#125cc8`, red `#c42138`, green `#11864b`, ice `#d8f2ff`, gold `#ffd66b`. Use `2px` dark grid rules, `4px` board-cell corners, `18px` illustrated panel corners and small solid offset shadows. Reserve radial highlights and outlined display type for the package layer. Use a `180ms steps(4,end)` move only as an optional modern translation; reduce motion to immediate state updates.

## Typography / Fonts

Pair a heavy rounded display category with a readable sans for controls and paragraphs. A licensed pixel face may serve short HUD labels. Preserve semantic HTML text; hand-drawn title art needs a text equivalent. Do not force bitmap paragraphs or claim exact historical font identities from visual resemblance.

## Cultural / Ethical Notes

Create original characters, marks, scenery and copy. Do not reproduce Irem, HAL, Sega, Nintendo or Hudson assets. Avoid flashing, forced timers, sound-dependent feedback and color-only object coding. Modern interfaces should not carry over inaccessible historical controls.

## Anti-Patterns

- A stock card grid with a pixel font and unrelated game picture.
- Treating every source as icy, pastel or visibly pixelated.
- Counting regional cover crops and reused illustrations as independent evidence.
- Making a booking or purchase require completing a puzzle.
- Placing tiny text or focus indicators on noisy tile patterns.

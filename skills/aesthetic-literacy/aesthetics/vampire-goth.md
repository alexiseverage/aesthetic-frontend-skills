# Vampire Goth

## Scope

Vampire Goth is treated here as a bounded romantic/aristocratic Gothic presentation, informed by vampire fiction and theatrical dress. Its useful grammar combines dark material depth, formal fitted hierarchy, elongated or pointed silhouettes and concentrated ornamental fastenings. Some visual elements are shared with broader romantic goth, mourning dress and historical revival. They become a coherent vampire interpretation through the full composition and source context, not a checklist of fangs, black fabric or a single red gem.

Claim support is strongest for patterned framing (VA02–05/08–09/11–15/18/21–27/36), elongated drape (VA01–05/07/12–16/23–26/28/36), and concentrated clasp/jewel detail (VA12/17–22/29/32). The gold coat VA02 and blue collar jacket VA08 are meaningful color exceptions. Ivory jabot VA27 functions as a bright inner layer against dark clothing, not an instruction that all surfaces must be black.

## 7-Dimension Profile


**Palette**: Black dominates; burgundy/red contrast appears in VA03/05/15/16/19/21/24/28/29/35. Gold VA02, blue VA08 and ivory VA27/36 prevent a mandatory two-color definition. High for this fashion corpus; CSS colors are proposed estimates.


**Type**: Brand watermarks do not establish a substyle font. Tall serif display and occasional blackletter can suggest Gothic literary associations, but clear body/control typography is a web proposal. Low universal font confidence; avoid imitating a maker logo.


**Texture**: Tonal scrolling brocade, velvet-like dark absorption, porous lace and bright small metal/gem points recur across VA01–05/07–09/11–29/32/35/36. High visual support; actual fiber/gem composition requires source metadata.


**Shape**: Tall collars, fitted centers, pointed openings, broad lapels, long tails/drips and hanging sleeves recur; small center clasps and rows of buttons articulate the opening. VA19/20/32 isolate ornamental edge structure. High within the selected romantic/aristocratic interpretation; not exclusive forms.


**Motion**: Hanging capes, skirts and bead drops imply weight and drape. Actual temporal behavior, choreography and UI motion were not observed. Unknown historical timing; small user-triggered transitions are inferred.


**Spatial**: Quiet central dark fields are framed by brocade/lace sides and concentrated fastenings; mirrored ornament and narrow vertical axes create formal hierarchy. High for object composition; device layout remains a web decision.


**Cultural markers**: Museum context joins literature/cinema and fashion; practitioner interpretation links romantic/Victorian vampire dressing to theatrical garments. It is a visual substyle, not evidence of beliefs, personality or music taste. Moderate classification confidence; retail and fashion-led sampling limits remain.

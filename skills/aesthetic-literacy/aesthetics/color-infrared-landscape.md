# Color-Infrared Landscape

## Scope

A process-based photographic language in which reflected near-infrared information produces selective false-color relationships while recognizable landscape geometry, texture and light remain intact. Red/pink vegetation against cyan, blue, dark water and neutral rock is prominent. Yellow, blue/silver and low-chroma variants also occur.

Use for image-led articles, photo essays, travel/editorial cards and photographic portfolios. Distinguish actual infrared capture, sensor composites and a contemporary visual simulation. Uniform pink filters and thermal heat maps do not establish this identity.

## 7-Dimension Profile

**Palette**: Selective material-dependent separation; red/coral/pink foliage against blue/cyan/neutral regions is common, not universal. Yellow, silver-blue and low-chroma variants are documented. Keep rock, cloud, water and foliage distinguishable.

**Type**: No mandatory typeface follows from the imaging process. Use legible editorial hierarchy; neutral sans labels and a contrasting serif reading face are contemporary options. The image carries the transformation.

**Texture**: Retain fine vegetation, stone, water, atmospheric depth and realistic shadows. Grain, halation and digital sharpness vary by method. Blur and fake film damage are optional, not defining.

**Shape**: Natural shores, canopies, mountains and clouds, sometimes contrasted with roads, architecture or field contours. Coherent perspective and scale survive transformation; flares/fractals are unnecessary.

**Motion**: Inspected references are static. Exposure trails do not establish animation. Prefer stillness and deliberate disclosure/image changes; remove optional transitions for reduced motion. Historical timing is unknown.

**Spatial**: Depth, reflection, framing foliage and leading lines remain useful. Dense vegetation may border open sky, water or snow. Support portrait and horizontal images without destructive cropping or compulsory text overlays.

**Cultural markers**: Near-infrared photography, color-infrared film and scientific imaging, with distinct authors/methods/purposes. This is an imaging tradition, not a unified movement or ecological ideology.

## Non-Negotiables

**Non-negotiables**: Recognizable photographic landscape; selective non-natural color tied to material regions; preserved texture/light/spatial continuity; honest identification of real capture versus simulation. Pink/cyan alone is insufficient.

## Connotation

**Mode:** contemporary interpretation of a photographic process. Familiar places become uncanny, serene, scientific or estranging. A vivid artistic palette must not be presented as ecological measurement.

## Related / Subsets

- **Cyanotype**: blue contact-print chemistry/material, not near-infrared separation.
- **Psychedelic**: overlapping altered-perception connotation; flowing lettering/ornament belong to another grammar.
- **Solarpunk**: possible nature/technology overlap; regenerative futures and their politics are not implied.
- Analog film, modified-camera and sensor-composite branches require context. No independent subset slug is asserted.

## Frontend / UI Guidance

Build a real photo-essay/article, photographic card or portfolio gallery. Give imagery room; put semantic headings, captions and controls on an opaque reading surface. Preserve focal regions/proportions on mobile. Alt text should describe the scene and significant color transformation.

Reading disclosures, save buttons and gallery controls must produce actual states. Do not invent sensor readings, locations or comparisons using unrelated images. Choose controls for the product's purpose.

## CSS Translation

Proposed screen-estimated roles: paper #F4F3EB, ink #153E44, coral #F1A39E, crimson #A92D48, pale cyan #D2EBE7, teal #0E626A. Use paper/ink for reading and reserve vivid color for imagery/accents.

Prefer flat surfaces, fine rules, squared image fields and generous whitespace. Set explicit image dimensions, responsive grids, min-width: 0 and intentional object-fit. CSS hue rotation is a visual effect, never spectral reconstruction. Optional 150ms color transitions are modern choices, disabled under reduced motion.

## Typography / Fonts

Choose legible editorial categories rather than an invented infrared font. System Arial and Georgia italic provide hierarchy with no font download; licensed publication faces are appropriate too. Keep body text around 16px, readable leading and short letterspaced labels.

## Cultural / Ethical Notes

Credit photographers and follow image licenses. Do not copy a living maker's recognizable compositions or call generated scenery authentic capture, a real location, ecological evidence or endorsement. Near-infrared reflection differs from thermal emission. Scientific red-vegetation conventions do not make every artistic palette a health map.

## Anti-Patterns

- Uniform magenta overlays without material/color separation.
- Thermal maps, radar and all false-color images treated as one system.
- Fabricated measurements or unrelated before/after sliders.
- Neon grids or psychedelic lettering replacing photographic space.
- Low-contrast image-overlay text, autonomous panning or image-only navigation.

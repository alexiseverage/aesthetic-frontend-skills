# Witchcore

Witchcore is a contemporary esoteric lifestyle aesthetic of ritual objects, apothecary logic, dark natural palettes, candlelit warmth, and occult-symbol framing. It should feel ceremonial and domestic rather than Halloween novelty.

## Scope

Use Witchcore for ritual journaling, lunar trackers, boutique storefronts, fantasy-adjacent editorial, apothecary catalogs, and wellness-adjacent tools where symbolism is consensual and contextual. Tone symbols down for clinical, emergency, or evidence-sensitive flows.

## 7-Dimension Profile

**Palette**: Black, charcoal, forest green, plum, oxblood, amber candlelight, bone, dried herb greens, and tarnished brass (`#111111`, `#2A1F2D`, `#263B2A`, `#5B1E33`, `#C07A2D`, `#E8DEC8`). Color contrast should feel nocturnal but readable.

**Type**: Apothecary serif labels, engraved occult caps, small chart annotations, and readable sans support copy. Use high-contrast serif display sparingly, title case for ritual headings, and letterspaced caps for talismanic labels.

**Texture**: Smoke, wax, velvet, dried botanicals, parchment, etched star charts, ink bleed, candle glow, and glass jar translucency. CSS textures can use radial shadow pools, subtle noise, botanical line art, and amber glow.

**Shape**: Circles, crescent moons, pentacles, jars, talismans, altar trays, arched cards, and framed correspondences. Radii range from squared apothecary labels to soft jar/card corners (`6–16px`).

**Motion**: Candle flicker, smoke drift, slow reveal, lunar phase progressions, and orbiting celestial marks. Motion is patient (`500–1200ms`) and ritualistic, never bouncy SaaS delight.

**Spatial**: Altar/tabletop framing, cabinet-of-curiosity clustering, ritual grids, inventory drawers, moon-calendar rows, and central object focus. Dense symbolic clusters should still leave a clear primary action.

**Cultural markers**: Tarot, crystals, moon phases, spell bottles, ravens, herbs, candles, grimoires, apothecary labels, altar cloths, correspondences, protective symbols.

## Non-Negotiables

**Non-negotiables**: occult-symbol framing + ritual/apothecary object logic + dark natural palette with candlelight warmth + ceremonial pacing. If these are removed, the result becomes a generic adjacent style rather than Witchcore.

## Frontend / UI Guidance

Frame flows like altar tables, apothecary drawers, moon calendars, correspondence charts, and spellbook sections. Keep a clear primary action inside the dense symbolic field and include reduced-motion alternatives for flicker, smoke, or orbiting marks.

## CSS Translation

- Color roles: `--bg-night`, `--surface-apothecary`, `--accent-candle`, `--accent-plum`, `--accent-forest`, `--text-bone`.
- Borders/dividers: moon phases, talisman rings, apothecary labels, altar trays, and chart correspondences.
- Radius language: arched cards, jar softness, squared labels, and circular ritual marks.
- Effects: candle glow, smoke, wax, parchment, ink bleed, etched star charts, and glass translucency.
- Layout: altar/tabletop framing, cabinet drawers, moon-calendar rows, correspondence grids, and central focal objects.
- Motion: candle flicker, smoke drift, slow reveal, lunar progression, orbiting marks, and reduced-motion static phases.

## Typography / Fonts

Pair apothecary serifs, engraved occult caps, chart annotations, and readable sans support. Keep decorative faces for headings, seals, and talismanic labels; long copy should remain calm and legible.

## Cultural / Ethical Notes

Use esoteric and spiritual symbols respectfully. Avoid flattening living religious or magical practices into decoration, and do not use Witchcore to sell medical, safety, or evidence-sensitive claims without clear boundaries.

## Anti-Patterns

- Halloween novelty with pumpkins, jump scares, or costume-store occult marks.
- Dark academia bookshelves with no ritual/apothecary object logic.
- Cottagecore herb imagery without omen, correspondence, or ceremonial framing.
- Sacred or living-practice symbols used as generic decoration.

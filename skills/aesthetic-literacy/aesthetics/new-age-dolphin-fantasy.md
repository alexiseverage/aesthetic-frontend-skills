# New Age Dolphin Fantasy

## Scope

New Age Dolphin Fantasy is a descriptive visual label for luminous, idealized cetacean worlds where underwater clarity, cosmic imagery, dreamlike illumination or surreal transformation exceeds ordinary marine depiction. It draws on visionary/environmental fantasy and decorative circulation; it is not presented as a historically self-named movement or a synonym for all marine art. Dolphin-led imagery is central, while selected whale/orca works establish a closely related cetacean variant.

The strongest shared relationship is smooth, brightly modeled organic forms suspended within unusually legible depth. Light beams, white caustic webs, bubble chains and small star-like accents direct attention toward an open luminous center. A dark reef or cosmic surround contains dense miniature detail around that center. Above/below-water splits, planets at incompatible scales, impossible simultaneous horizons and rainbow body transformations make the fantasy relationship explicit. Not every work needs a planet or rainbow; those are variants, not universal requirements.

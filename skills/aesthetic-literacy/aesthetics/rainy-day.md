# Rainy Day

Rainy Day makes rainfall, softened distance and the experience of shelter into a contemplative visual system. Sharp water beads, trailing rivulets, disrupted reflections and diffuse light sit against quieter surroundings. It can feel solitary, comforting or melancholy; a cold blue palette and a coffee cup are not sufficient on their own.

## Scope

Use for focus sessions, ambient instruments, reading spaces, personal journals and small reflective editorial experiences. Preserve the relationship between wet surroundings and a stable place to pause. An outdoor composition can suggest shelter through framing, awnings or a protected viewpoint rather than a literal interior.

This entry describes a contemporary mood aesthetic, not a historical art movement, universal response to weather or scientific promise of relaxation. Rain photography is evidence for optical/material grammar; it does not make every photograph of rain an example of a named internet community. “Raincore” and “pluviophile” are related search language whose synonym boundaries remain unsettled.

## 7-Dimension Profile

**Palette**: Overcast gray, gray-green, slate and blue-black recur, often with pale reflected sky or small warm amber lights. Daylight green, ochre, mauve-gray and near-monochrome variants are supported. Restrained surrounding color lets water highlights and the sheltered foreground remain distinct. These are observed relationships, not universal historical hex values.

**Type**: Sparse, readable hierarchy rather than one canonical font. Inspected ambient sites include serif titling, narrow sans labels and ordinary sans controls. A gentle serif display with clear sans utilities is a useful contemporary translation. Droplet lettering, cursive quotes and blurred body text are not requirements.

**Texture**: Reflective beads with light/dark rims, irregular lobed drops, merging trails, condensation, misted distance and water-disturbed pavement. Knit, paper, curtains and ceramic can supply a quieter indoor countertexture. Keep optical effects in bounded surfaces; uniformly frosted cards lose the contrast between wet texture and resting space.

**Shape**: Small uneven bead clusters and long gravity-led trails contrast with window frames, sills, quiet rectangular reading planes and circular ripple rings. Full-window scenes and close surface studies both work. Water has varied scale and spacing; a regular polka-dot field or mandatory teardrop icon is reductive.

**Motion**: Environmental trickling and sparse user-controlled play, level, preset and timer changes appear in live references. Still photographs establish no easing or loop duration. A contemporary application may use slow decorative rain with a visible pause control; reduced motion should retain a static wet surface and immediate functional state updates. No lightning flashes, compulsory audio or animated reading text.

**Spatial**: Focused near surface versus softened distance; framed thresholds; localized light against spacious quiet ground. Warm domestic and darker urban variants differ in density. The interface needs a stable sheltered region for text and controls rather than placing everything directly over active droplets.

**Cultural markers**: Window watching, rainy walks, warm interiors, ordinary streetlight reflections, seasonal observation and ambient focus/reading culture. Umbrellas, mugs, books and autumn leaves are optional contextual objects. Rain has varied regional, practical and emotional meanings; no one climate or mood owns it.

## Non-Negotiables

**Non-negotiables**:

- Rain-specific optical/material cues: droplets, trails, misted distance or disturbed wet reflections.
- A deliberate relationship between weather and a quiet, sheltered or contemplative viewpoint.
- A hierarchy that preserves the contrast between active wet texture and stable resting/reading space.

## Connotation

**Mode:** contemporary interpretation.

Intimate, patient and observant; comfort and melancholy can coexist. This is neither a reconstruction of a specific early website nor a claim that rain improves everyone's wellbeing. Generic blue-gray “cozy” themes fatigue quickly when weather is only a background image.

## Related / Subsets

- `glassmorphism` organizes translucent floating UI panes; Rainy Day organizes a weather/shelter relationship and can use fully opaque controls.
- `cottagecore` centers idealized rural/domestic life; Rainy Day also supports ordinary cities, bare panes and abstract water surfaces.
- `scandi-noir` needs regional crime/social unease; damp surfaces alone do not establish its narrative or geography.
- `shin-hanga` includes specific Japanese print practices and weather compositions. Do not treat it as a synonym, origin claim or interchangeable rain decoration.
- Warm window-side, cool daylight, rain-at-night and puddle studies are prose variants, not additional canonical slugs. “Pluviophile” names an affinity for rain and need not prescribe a single visual style.

## Frontend / UI Guidance

Compose a useful small task beside or within a framed rain surface: a focus timer, reading selection or observation journal. Let controls share the material logic through sheltered opaque surfaces, waterline progress, modest outlines and quiet state changes. Keep weather presets, duration, start/pause/resume/reset and completion honest when using a timer. Audio requires its own explicit control and must never be implied by a purely visual switch.

Use real semantic buttons and labels, visible focus, stable text contrast and generous touch targets. Stack the window and controls on phones while preserving a recognizable piece of the water surface. Keep visual rain independent from functional countdown state, and allow it to stop without stopping the task.

## CSS Translation

Proposed application values, not sampled historical tokens: sheltered paper `#F4F0E7`, ink `#263A38`, secondary ink `#52635F`, wet gray-green `#526B69`, mist `#C7D3CC`, warm accent `#A9663A`. Put body text on the opaque paper plane; decorative gradients and refraction stay in the window.

- Use a framed grid with a generous wet-surface area, a smaller control column and a thin sill/footer rule; stack at the content's natural minimum width.
- Make original SVG bead highlights, irregular trails and blurred landscape masses; reserve blur for scenery, never semantic text or focus rings.
- Prefer shallow inset edges and a few dark window mullions over floating glass-card stacks. Paper/control corners can be gently rounded without making every element a pill.
- Proposed feedback: 160ms color/opacity; optional rain trails over several seconds. Under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, remove ambient transforms and retain static bead geometry.

## Typography / Fonts

System Georgia for a large regular/italic display and a system humanist sans for labels/body is one low-cost interpretation; no extra font download is necessary. Use tabular numerals for time, mixed-case controls and brief small-cap utility labels. Font identity is not a defining historical claim. Do not reduce captions below readable sizes to make the surface seem quiet.

## Cultural / Ethical Notes

Keep regional rainy seasons and everyday weather distinct from a universal autumn fantasy. Rain can mean danger, work, flooding or disruption as well as comfort. Do not turn real disasters into ambience or claim therapeutic efficacy. Use original objects/graphics rather than portraits, copied photography, protected characters or source-site branding. Preserve keyboard access, contrast, motion choice and independent audio consent.

## Anti-Patterns

- A generic dashboard over a rain photograph, with controls unrelated to the material or spatial grammar.
- Universal dark-blue, autumn-only, sad-only or rural-only rules.
- Neon city scenery silently relabeled Cyberpunk or noir without the relevant social/narrative context.
- Constant storm flashes, moving letters, compulsory sound or a motion switch that stops the task itself.
- Droplet distortion behind essential copy, weak gray labels or inaccessible transparent controls.

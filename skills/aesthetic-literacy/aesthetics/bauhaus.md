**Palette**: Primary triad (red/yellow/blue) + black/white; no mixing, no pastels

**Type**: Geometric sans-serif (Futura, Bayer Universal); type as pure form

**Texture**: Industrial material honesty — steel, glass, primary color planes

**Shape**: Circle, square, triangle — pure primary forms; nothing not derived from these

**Motion**: Form following function — purposeful; no decorative motion

**Spatial**: Asymmetric grid; diagonal tension; generous whitespace around bold forms

**Cultural markers**: Kandinsky color-form theory, Bayer typography, Breuer furniture language

**Non-negotiables**: primary palette + pure geometric forms + type-as-form

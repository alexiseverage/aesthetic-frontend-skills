# Capsule Toy Display Walls

## Scope

Repeated compact vending cells paired with collectible-series display inserts. The defining hierarchy is placard, payment/turning control and retrieval, repeated across a wall or bank. Product inserts add enlarged examples, named variants, feature callouts and a factual price/age/count rail. This is a descriptive commercial vernacular, not all Japanese toy design.

## 7-Dimension Profile

**Palette**: White or cream modular hardware repeats around saturated series inserts. Mint, pink, yellow, blue, brown and darker themed posters all occur. A single candy palette would erase the variety of product families.

**Type**: Large outlined or rounded series titles coexist with small labels, variant names, prices and age marks. Inserts differ by product theme; factual rails supply consistency. Use live readable labels rather than mimicking Japanese lettering with invented glyphs.

**Texture**: Smooth molded vending housings, clear product windows and flat printed inserts. Poster grounds include dots, rays and lace-like framing. Old machine scuffs, photographic glare and tiny reproduction artifacts are not required.

**Shape**: Repeated rectangular cells, prominent circular turning controls, enclosed collection hatches, capsule circles and framed product diagrams. Not every machine has the same height, payment mechanism or silhouette.

**Motion**: Qualifying evidence is still photography, product art and planned-installation illustrations. Knob movement and dispensing speed are not measured. Any browser turning feedback is a proposed interaction; selection can update immediately.

**Spatial**: Dense repeatable vending bays at wall scale; each insert has a dominant sample and a set of smaller variants, with fixed facts or feature annotations. Preserve the local cell hierarchy while making the wall responsive.

**Cultural markers**: Japanese capsule-toy retail and maker-specific collectible lines. Gashapon is Bandai branding; use it accurately in source attribution, not as a fabricated brand for original work. Planned renderings remain distinct from observed built installations.

## Non-Negotiables

**Non-negotiables**: Repeated self-contained vending cells; clear placard/control/retrieval separation; comparable collectible variants with visible practical facts.

## Connotation

**Mode:** Contemporary quotation of a documented artifact vernacular.

Discovery through a compact product wall: small, systematic and collectible. A useful contemporary translation can let people deliberately select a variant rather than reproducing paid random purchasing.

## Related / Subsets

Claw cabinets stage a large transparent chamber above a shared control deck. Digital gacha banners organize event navigation and currency around a campaign. Konbini Utility shares retail clarity; Shokuhin Sanpuru Display concerns lifelike food replicas rather than repeated vending cells.

## Frontend / UI Guidance

Use a collection selector with category filters, a wall of themed cells and a series detail pane. Each cell places its placard above a clearly labeled circular selection control and a lower saved/collection status. A carousel can compare variants; an accordion reveals materials and size; a labeled form can save a chosen collection. Distinguish available, selected and saved states with text.

## CSS Translation

Build a responsive repeated grid with compact internal rows, inset hatch borders and original SVG capsule/variant diagrams. Proposed application values: white shell #f5f4eb, charcoal #252b30, warm gray dividers #d9d9ce, a 10px housing radius and circular control. Let each series own a small accent palette. Avoid hiding live text in poster images. Disable nonessential knob transforms under reduced motion.

## Typography / Fonts

A rounded system sans is a proposed general display choice; a plain system sans carries facts. Mix weight and outlined short headings sparingly. For actual Japanese content, choose a suitable Japanese font and real localized copy with a readable fallback.

## Cultural / Ethical Notes

Create original collectible objects and series names. Licensed characters, maker marks and the original inserts remain research references. Show actual selection or honest demo outcomes; no false rarity, paid chance or invented availability pressure.

## Anti-Patterns

- A colorful product grid without the vending hierarchy.
- One generic kawaii palette across every series.
- Counting every toy shown on one insert as an independent reference.
- Making the knob the only unlabeled way to perform an action.

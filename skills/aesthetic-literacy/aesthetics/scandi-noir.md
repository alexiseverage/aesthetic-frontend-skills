# Scandi Noir

Scandi Noir is the visual and narrative idiom of Nordic crime stories in which ordinary institutions and domestic life conceal fractures. Regional light, weather and built environments give social unease a physical setting. Cold subdued color is common, but daylight, autumn warmth and stark white space can serve the same tension.

## Scope

The label spans literature, film, television and their international promotion; it is a broad, commercially circulated genre description rather than one fixed national style. Its earlier literary roots extend before the screen/export idiom named by this entry’s era. Nordic includes Finland and Iceland as well as Scandinavia. Do not label every Nordic drama or minimalist interior noir.

## 7-Dimension Profile

**Palette**: Slate, gray-green, dark water, pale sky, charcoal and muted concrete recur. Warm practical light, rust vegetation, red editorial accents, natural green and daylight white are valid variants. Saturated promotional color may differ from the underlying cinematography.

**Type**: Restrained, legible title and supporting information; narrow or widely tracked sans capitals are common in export key art. Serif titles also occur. Public-record labels are a useful application metaphor, not proof that every series uses a particular municipal typeface. Exact source fonts remain unverified.

**Texture**: Weathered infrastructure, wool and ordinary outerwear, painted doors, glass, damp surfaces, low-light image grain and matte architecture. Promotional cracks, torn paper and title distress are separate graphic treatments rather than universal on-screen materials.

**Shape**: Long bridges, shorelines, docks, roads, apartment blocks, corridors and practical institutional rectangles. Landscape and built systems can obscure, divide or isolate. Avoid turning this into an obligatory futuristic evidence HUD.

**Motion**: Patient environmental views and conversational/procedural movement can alternate with quick trailer cuts and sudden action. Actual trailers establish variety, not a universal slow duration or easing curve. In an archive-like interface, stillness and deliberate user-controlled reveal can preserve attention without decorative weather loops.

**Spatial**: Distance, obstructed sightlines, low horizons, isolated buildings and restrained interiors make the ordinary uneasy. Crowded cast montages belong to marketing; they are not evidence that the dramatic world is always dense. Keep interface reading order clear.

**Cultural markers**: Crime and investigation entangled with family, public trust, inequality, borders, governance and institutional responsibility. Place, language and social context matter alongside landscape. Use specific Nordic references rather than treating the region as a homogeneous cold country.

## Non-Negotiables

**Non-negotiables**:

- Regional place/light/material context, expressed beyond a blue palette.
- Noir unease or moral/social friction within ordinary life and institutions.
- Restrained hierarchy and believable material/architectural detail.
- A coherent relation between the setting and the investigative narrative; crime-themed copy alone is insufficient.

## Connotation

A grounded contemporary quotation can use a fictional public-records desk, infrastructure review or investigative publication. Preserve uncertainty, ordinary procedure and responsibility rather than presenting suffering as prestige decoration. The genre’s exported branding is part of its history, not a universal documentary portrait of Nordic societies.

## Related / Subsets

`scandinavian-modern` shares some functional architecture but need not contain the noir’s social unease. `film-noir` contributes crime and moral ambiguity; Nordic settings and institutions change its expression. `techno-noir` often foregrounds futuristic technology and neon, which are not required here. No canonical subsets are declared.

## Frontend / UI Guidance

Build a restrained editorial or records surface with an original empty harbor, bridge, corridor or document object. Let source comparison, notes, chronology or controlled disclosure perform a real task. Use opaque readable panels, restrained metadata, clear selection and visible keyboard focus. A scene should contribute setting and tension without relying on portraits or graphic violence. Reflow records and controls on phone; do not shrink a desktop evidence wall into unreadable fragments.

## CSS Translation

- Separate dark environmental ground, pale document surface, muted labels and one functional alert/accent.
- Favor shallow rules, square geometry and subtle matte grain; avoid glossy gamer panels.
- Create depth through obscured foreground, a low horizon or repeated utilitarian lines rather than glowing data webs.
- Keep body copy on stable surfaces. Dark atmosphere does not justify low-contrast text or invisible focus.
- Treat all interaction timings as proposed values unless measured in the actual source. Stillness is valid when the task is careful reading.

## Typography / Fonts

A neutral system sans with condensed/uppercase display treatment and a restrained monospace metadata layer can work. Serif title variants are supported; no exact font identity is established by the inspected key art. Limit tracking in long labels, maintain readable numerals and preserve accented Nordic names when used.

## Cultural / Ethical Notes

Keep crime fiction distinct from factual public records. Do not invent real police affiliations or use national emblems as decorative authority. Preserve the genre’s attention to social systems and consequences; avoid a tourist fantasy of universal Nordic misery, victim glamour, racialized threat or graphic violence as atmosphere. A fictional infrastructure inquiry can express these concerns through objects and procedure.

## Anti-Patterns

- Blue-gray minimalism with a detective label and no place or social context.
- Treating winter darkness, sans titles or slow motion as absolute requirements.
- Copying distributor premiere bars and logos as if they were in-world institutional design.
- Red-string conspiracy walls, cyberpunk HUDs or constant snow animation by default.
- Reducing diverse Nordic societies to interchangeable fjords and violence.

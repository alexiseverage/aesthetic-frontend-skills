# Chladni Resonance Patterns

## Scope

A descriptive catalog name for particulate nodal figures on vibrating plates and their scientific diagrams, rather than a historical design movement. Sand or comparable grains collect near nodal lines; geometry, mounting and excitation shape the result. Apply to loading indicators, reading cards, science publishing and original bounded visual assets. Keep the physical pattern grammar visible without building a pretend laboratory console.

## 7-Dimension Profile

**Palette**: Strong grain/ground separation. Pale grains on dark plates, warm grains on brass, colored salt on light metal and black seeds on pale wooden soundboards are evidenced. A grayscale scientific field is common; black backgrounds, brass and rainbow color are optional. Screen hex values are contemporary estimates.

**Type**: Physical figures have no intrinsic font. Historical tables use numbered figures and fine italic labels; contemporary diagrams use ordinary sans. Choose legible reading text with concise optional monospaced notation. Do not invent historical font identification or add fake scientific measurements.

**Texture**: Granular bands vary in thickness, break at junctions and leave stray particles between lines. Scratches, exposure and mounting belong to the physical object. Engraved diagrams may simplify into stipple or precise lines; avoid turning all physical examples into perfect glowing vector curves.

**Shape**: Rings, parallel lines, crosses, rounded lobes, lattices, loops, spokes and asymmetric branches. Circle, square, polygon, stadium and instrument outlines constrain geometry. A mandala, compulsory radial symmetry or maximal density is not the definition.

**Motion**: Actual demonstrations show grains redistribute under excitation and form nodal accumulations. Exact settling times/easing were not measured. A digital disperse/settle loading loop is a proposed analogy, not a calibrated plate simulation. Show only actual pending state, do not fake percentages, and provide a still reduced-motion drawing.

**Spatial**: One bounded surface with edge terminations, fixing points, negative space and sometimes apertures. Grain paths respond to that surface. Place reading and controls outside the active figure; do not turn the whole page into unbounded particle wallpaper.

**Cultural markers**: Experimental acoustics, public science demonstrations, instrument-making and historical scientific print. Chladni's eighteenth-century work has Hooke's earlier precursor. This physical phenomenon does not establish healing claims, sacred frequencies or universal pitch-image mappings.

## Non-Negotiables

**Non-negotiables**: Recognizable nodal-path geometry; a meaningful bounded plate or explicitly identified diagram; visible particulate character for the physical branch; honest separation of illustration, real loading state and scientific measurement. Generic dust, water ripples or a rainbow rosette alone are insufficient.

## Connotation

**Mode:** contemporary revival of experimental observation. Suggests patient discovery and order emerging from motion. It is a continuing scientific practice; the frontend adaptation is new, not a recovered historical interface.

## Related / Subsets

## Frontend / UI Guidance

Use an original nodal figure within a bounded card/header. Keep familiar buttons, article cards, accordions and navigation plainly usable. A loading indicator must correspond to an actual request; provide text, indeterminate semantics, error/retry, successful content and a stable focus target. Keep the idle image still. Do not add a timer solely to display the animation.

Buttons need44px targets, clear focus and an explicit pending state. Reading cards need quiet surfaces and generous line height. Inputs/navigation should retain normal labels and control semantics. Headings can use an editorial serif; dividers remain fine rules. Links stay visibly differentiated. Tags should describe actual content rather than fake experiment readings. Prefer inline details to unnecessary modals; errors need concise text and a retry action. Icons are secondary to the bounded grain figure.

Mobile stacks the figure and copy, preserves the full plate silhouette and wraps actual titles. Do not clip controls or shrink text to keep a scientific-looking panel dense. Reduced motion retains a static complete figure and functional status.

## Typography / Fonts

Readable serif/sans for ordinary content and optional monospace for short labels. Italic figure numbers belong to the historical print branch; they do not prescribe an entire interface. Fonts, screen colors, breakpoints and animation periods are application choices unless directly sourced and measured.

## Cultural / Ethical Notes

Credit scientific institutions, researchers, instrument makers and image authors. Modern photographs retain rights even when the phenomenon is old. Original procedural artwork is illustrative and excluded from research counts. Distinguish physical observations from numerical models and photographic reflection from nodal grains. Do not imply institutional endorsement or scientific calibration.

## Anti-Patterns

- Perfect radial mandalas, glowing molecular networks or unbounded dust replacing plate geometry.
- Calling water ripples, ferrofluid, moiré or particle trajectories the same phenomenon.
- Mandatory black/brass/rainbow palettes or a universal font.
- Fake frequency sliders, healing claims, arbitrary hertz labels or invented percentages.
- Counting modes, crops, frames and mirrors as independent specimens.
- Continuous motion while idle or fake request delays added for spectacle.
- Treating damaged folded scans or tiny compressed photographs as fine material evidence.

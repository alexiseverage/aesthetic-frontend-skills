# Haeckelian Biological Plates

The comparative biological plate grammar exemplified by Kunstformen der Natur: articulated organism structures, whole/detail relationships and numbered publication composition.

Evidence basis: [the research profile](../../../knowledge/aesthetics/haeckelian-biological-plates.md) records 30 qualifying unique artifacts, source distribution, claim support and limitations. This is a descriptive catalog category. Exact historical colors, fonts and screen timings are not inferred from appearance; proposed frontend values below are contemporary translations.

## Scope

The comparative biological plate grammar exemplified by Kunstformen der Natur: articulated organism structures, whole/detail relationships and numbered publication composition. This descriptive label is not a self-declared movement or a synonym for every scientific illustration.

## 7-Dimension Profile

**Palette**: Light paper with gray, olive, blue and warm colored structures is common, alongside dark olive/black grounds, saturated habitat and botanical variants. Preserve local structure-ground contrast rather than imposing universal sepia.

**Type**: Small serif-style running titles, plate/figure numbers and taxonomic captions support the illustration. Exact original faces are unknown; modern readable hierarchy should preserve the supporting role.

**Texture**: Fine hatch, stipple, repeated pores, translucent membranes and modeled relief. Some habitat plates are more painterly. Paper aging is incidental; internal structure is essential.

**Shape**: Radial, bilateral, tubular, branching, latticed and perforated forms with repeated subdivisions. Flowing tentacles, fronds and pitcher plants prevent an all-circles or mandala reading.

**Motion**: The inspected plates are still prints. No historical timing is inferred. Manual plate browsing and bookmark state are contemporary applications; keep organism studies still and remove optional page transitions under reduced motion.

**Spatial**: Comparative scale hierarchy, figure keys and balanced distribution, sometimes within perimeter rules. Overlap, sweeping diagonals and habitat anchoring are documented variants, not errors in symmetry.

**Cultural markers**: Biological groups, lithographic publication and scientific popularization. Actual prints should credit Adolf Giltsch after Haeckel; historical classification and ideology are not contemporary biological truth.

## Non-Negotiables

**Non-negotiables**:

- Articulated internal biological structure rather than silhouette alone.
- Whole/detail or multi-form comparison with a publication hierarchy.
- Specific plate composition with figure/caption relationships.

## Connotation

**Mode:** Contemporary editorial quotation: morphology as ornament, with historical authorship and ideology contextualized.

The implementation should quote the documented material and organizational relationships. Its contemporary controls and readable type must remain honest about source and application limits.

## Related / Subsets

- Art Nouveau shares organic line and historical influence, but sinuous branding alone does not establish comparative morphology, figure keys and internal structural articulation. Treat as a relation, not parent or alias.
- Autumn Field Guide Naturalism foregrounds seasonal observation and field metadata. The present system centers the deliberately formalized comparative plate and geometric/morphological repetition; its marine and microscopic focus need not be seasonal.
- Cyanotype is defined by a photographic process and blue/pale print relation. This system is lithographic/formal and includes multiple color and ground strategies.
- Op Art uses patterned optical effects. Here pattern describes represented biological structures and their views; visual instability is not a requirement.
- Engraved Celestial Atlas joins coordinate-based stars and engraved figures; the shared scholarly page organization does not erase their different information models.

No separate canonical subsets are introduced.

## Frontend / UI Guidance

Build a manual plate carousel with a large composed study, plate number, caption index and bookmarks. Keep the whole design visible, announce the current plate and make previous/next and saved-state controls explicit. Mobile should preserve the full illustration and stack captions rather than forcing a narrow horizontal book.

## CSS Translation

Thin rules, quiet paper or a deliberate dark plate field, restrained serif numerals and careful negative space. Use original SVG hatch/lattice illustrations with varied scale; keep functional text outside dense drawn structure.

Proposed small/medium/large radii: 0/2/4 px. Use a 4/8/12/16/24/32 px spacing scale. Validate actual rendered text, controls and states independently of these illustrative tokens.

## Typography / Fonts

Book serif titles, small-cap-like running labels and plain readable captions are a proposed publication system. Do not claim a modern font is the original lithographic lettering.

## Cultural / Ethical Notes

Credit Giltsch as well as Haeckel for source works. Contextualize Haeckel’s racial ideology rather than celebrating it as neutral scientific authority. Original artistic studies must not masquerade as verified taxonomy or copied historical plates.

## Anti-Patterns

- Generic jellyfish silhouettes or mechanical radial mandalas.
- Universal perfect symmetry, mandatory cream ground or compulsory isolation.
- Uncredited source lithographs and unqualified celebration of historical ideology.

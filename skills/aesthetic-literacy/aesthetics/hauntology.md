# Hauntology

A visual practice of unstable cultural memory: familiar media forms, institutions or objects promise a coherent past, then reveal absences, incompatible fragments or futures that never arrived. It can be playful, bright or unsettling. Retro styling alone does not establish it.

## Scope

Use for considered music/editorial archives, imagined educational programmes, public-history essays and memory-centered storytelling. State when material is fictional; retain provenance for real archives. The current [research profile](../../../knowledge/aesthetics/hauntology.md) inspects 32 unique sleeves across three principal practices, with a British record-art bias.

## 7-Dimension Profile

**Palette**: Cream, ochre, brown, green and teal recur with emphatic spot inks. Saturated magenta, red/orange and cyan are observed exceptions; a muted palette is not compulsory.

**Type**: Plain bold sans, publisher/catalog numbering and fine rules often establish institutional confidence. Curling serifs, custom geometric lettering and covers without type also occur. Exact fonts were not verified.

**Texture**: Halftone, overprint, photocopy-like edges, weathered print and painterly surfaces. Preserve clean type and empty areas; degradation is one device, not the identity by itself.

**Shape**: Circles, labels, title bands, diagrams and cut-paper fragments; uncertain painted objects form another route. Avoid copying real publisher marks or culturally specific objects as neutral motifs.

**Motion**: Unverified in the still-image corpus. Intentional stillness, manual frame swaps or a restrained dissolve are proposed adaptations. Flicker, jitter and tape wobble are neither required nor established by this evidence.

**Spatial**: Ordered series/layouts interrupted by incompatible scales, uncertain objects or displaced scenes. Both dense montage and broad negative space work when the temporal tension remains intelligible.

**Cultural markers**: Ghost Box's remembered educational/broadcast and library-record world, Castle's specific local histories, and Seal's imagined objects provide different routes. Attribute project meanings rather than making Britain, medical memory loss or a single era universal.

## Non-Negotiables

**Non-negotiables**: a recognizable memory-bearing media/object language + a concrete temporal contradiction, absence or unstable recollection + coherent visual hierarchy. A brown filter, ghost icon or empty corridor alone is insufficient. Neither literal damage nor institutional graphics are mandatory across every variant.

## Connotation

Use a declared nostalgic quotation or contemporary fictional reconstruction. Preserve uncertainty and the tension between reassuring form and incomplete memory; avoid treating every project as bleak or clinically diagnostic.

## Related / Subsets

- `vaporwave` shares displaced media memory but often uses different consumer/digital references.
- `early-internet` shares media archaeology; browser chrome alone does not establish hauntology.
- `liminal-space-backrooms` can emphasize vacant space without a remembered institution or temporal disturbance.
- No canonical subsets are defined.

## Frontend / UI Guidance

Compose an educational booklet, transmission card, sleeve-like editorial spread or object study. Give users a real reading/browsing action: manually advance frames, compare records or expose a transcript. Missing information should be deliberate narrative content with useful controls, not fake loading, inaccessible text or arbitrary errors. Keep the reference imagery analytical; original geometry and objects can carry the grammar.

## CSS Translation

- Proposed roles: paper `#e7dfc2`, ink `#253d38`, ochre `#b68c34`, red `#9d3c2e`, pale green `#aab39a`; these are application choices, not sampled historical standards.
- Use square fields, thin rules, one emphatic title band and unevenly weighted picture/text zones.
- Confine subtle radial-dot halftone or offset layers to decorative art. Keep body text on an opaque surface.
- Let print-first compositions remain still. If transitions clarify a change, propose a short opacity transition and remove it under reduced motion.
- Stack spreads into a readable sequence on phones, preserving catalog identity and visible navigation.

## Typography / Fonts

A licensed/system grotesque with bold display sizing and modest serif notes can quote educational print; it is not a verified exact historical font. Use readable sentence-case instructions, adequate line height and tabular frame numbers. Font licensing, fallback and coverage remain implementation responsibilities.

## Cultural / Ethical Notes

Preserve specific histories and distinguish source text from observation. Do not pass fiction off as real public information, copy institutional identities, turn religious/folk objects into generic unease, or use dementia as a decorative gimmick. The Caretaker's artistic framing is not a medical model. Obtain rights before reusing archive images or recordings.

## Anti-Patterns

- Generic spooky sepia with no concrete temporal tension.
- Mandatory static, flicker, damaged text or analog noise over controls.
- Calling all pastoral illustration or all retro typography hauntology.
- Presenting one British label's design system as the worldwide definition.
- Treating saturated color, clean paper or joyful surrealism as disqualifying.

# Hyperpop

Hyperpop's visual world is an artist-led negotiation between synthetic pop surfaces, ordinary digital life and self-authored identity. Glossy objects, impossible fashion images and exaggerated lettering form a substantial branch, but monochrome portraits, candid bedrooms, suburban photographs and engraved fantasy also belong. The musical umbrella is contested; no single font, colour or rendering effect defines every participant.

## Scope

This entry covers visual campaigns around PC Music and adjacent experimental pop, with later 100 gecs, Dorian Electra, Charli xcx and food house examples testing its boundaries. PC Music is a particular label and creative network, not a synonym for the whole musical category. The research emphasizes English-language release artwork and credited creator testimony; it is not a survey of every global scene or fan fashion.

Use a specific original artist, release or creative-tool world as the anchor. Deliberate synthetic material, emotional directness, camp persona and mundane web construction can coexist. Digicore's internet-rap community and circulation history require separate investigation; a distorted sound or shiny graphic alone does not establish that identity.

## 7-Dimension Profile

**Palette**: Cobalt, icy blue, pink, violet, silver and iridescent highlights recur, alongside black-and-white, muted bedroom whites, green darkness and pastoral brown. Contrasts often stage a single luminous object or face against a simpler field. PC Music's documented blue identity is label-specific, not a universal hyperpop hex value.

**Type**: Individually authored stretched, spiked, looped or chrome-like wordmarks coexist with plain sans, thin serif, engraved lettering and tiny handwriting. Many complete photographic works show no title at all. Preserve a distinct short identity while giving controls readable labels; expressive distortion does not justify illegible navigation.

**Texture**: Transparent plastic, gel, chrome, embossed product surfaces, wet reflections and exact studio highlights are important options. Grain, flashlight falloff, worn knit, wood paneling and ordinary camera surfaces provide counterexamples. Pixels cannot identify a universal software pipeline or prove a physical material. Use one intentional material vocabulary rather than indiscriminate glitch overlays.

**Shape**: Sculptural loops, cosmetic/product objects, oversized accessories, stars and familiar media hardware can become strange through scale, finish or context. Portrait/persona remains central in many works. Fantasy illustration, angular gothic lettering and domestic snapshots are legitimate alternatives to inflated bubbles. Existing artist marks and likenesses remain authored identities.

**Motion**: The inspected release minisite uses a rippling inverted portrait; an actual lyric video stages extruded words and objects through changing camera/foreground views; a live-action video uses gestures, cuts, fisheye framing and a circular light vignette. These are distinct authored behaviors, not one mandatory glitch rhythm. Exact loops, frame rates and easing were not measured. Proposed UI motion should be finite and user-triggered, with still reduced-motion equivalents and no automatic audio or strobe.

**Spatial**: High-impact object/portrait fields and clear hierarchy coexist with dense fantasy assemblages and deliberately modest minisites. Clean negative space is as grounded as excess. Separate a tactile visual stage from legible parameters, forms and prose. Preserve authored composition on mobile instead of cropping away its meaning.

**Cultural markers**: Artist-built worlds, experimental commercial-pop imagery, DIY web distribution, queer self-representation, collaboration, earnest emotion and playful persona. Sincerity and irony vary by artist and work. Hannah Diamond's self-portrait practice establishes agency, not a template for depicting women as anonymous products. A platform playlist label does not settle community membership.

## Non-Negotiables

**Non-negotiables**:

- A specific music/artist/creative world and identifiable authorship, rather than anonymous shiny decoration.
- A deliberate relationship between constructed pop surfaces or personas and ordinary digital/material life; this can be glossy, rough, intimate or fantastical.
- Strong focal hierarchy and a committed visual concept, retaining emotional or playful intent without making irony compulsory.

## Connotation

**Mode:** contemporary revival.

Synthetic, tactile, intimate, euphoric, awkward, theatrical or confrontational according to the chosen branch. An original sound-material playground can translate the glossy branch without claiming that three synthesized notes reproduce a genre. A surface that treats every object as chrome quickly fatigues; quiet controls and negative space let the chosen exaggeration matter.

## Related / Subsets

- `y2k` overlaps reflective objects and futuristic lettering; Hyperpop is anchored in later artist networks and self-authored pop worlds, not millennium nostalgia alone.
- `web-2-gloss` shares synthetic UI surfaces; Hyperpop does not require startup friendliness, beta badges or rounded controls everywhere.
- `organic-digital` shares malleable forms; these campaigns can be sharply commercial, theatrical or deliberately rough rather than uniformly calming.
- `rave-flyer` shares music distribution and exuberance; release/persona worlds are not all event bills or rave graphics.
- `myspace-chaos` overlaps DIY internet intimacy; many researched works are sparse and polished rather than cluttered personal pages.

PC Music-linked synthetic product imagery, fashion self-portraiture and rough domestic/fantasy collages are descriptive branches, not interchangeable brands or separate catalog subset slugs.

## Frontend / UI Guidance

A small sound-material playground can combine one original glossy sculpture with a disciplined instrument rail: native preset radios, pitch and decay ranges, explicit one-note playback, volume, stop and a genuine WAV download. Keep material names clearly metaphorical, not a scientific model or a claim to emulate named artists.

All controls must affect real state. Trigger audio only on an explicit action, announce playback/error/end, stop on parameter changes or leaving the view, and release resources on unmount. Use the same bounded synthesized sample for preview and export. A visual sculpture is not an audio analyzer; do not present decorative waveforms as measurements. Native keyboard controls and text values should work without pointer gestures.

## CSS Translation

- Proposed glossy-branch roles: ink `#171235`, cobalt `#233caa`, pink `#f8d8e8`, ice `#e7f5fc`, acid `#d6fb6c`, white `#fffafc`. These are application decisions, not sampled source inks.
- Reserve reflective gradients or outlined extended lettering for one short identity. Put functional text on solid cobalt, ice or pink with verified contrast.
- Combine a large contained original object with square instrument modules and small pill indicators; do not make every control an unrecognizable blob.
- Use a 4/8px rhythm, 16px body/control text, roughly 44px targets and obvious 3px focus rings. Stack the rail below the intact object on small screens.
- Proposed feedback can use 180ms colour changes and one finite gentle object lift after play. Reduced motion removes transforms/transitions; sound remains explicitly controlled and optional.

## Typography / Fonts

An extended or italic heavy sans can establish a new identity, paired with plain Arial/Helvetica and monospace parameter labels. System fonts are acceptable when their treatment fits; they are not identifications of source wordmarks. Avoid copying PC Music's logo, Pop 2's lettering, Umru's mark or food house's hand-drawn identity. Keep Latin display experiments separate from multilingual body text and test fallback metrics.

## Cultural / Ethical Notes

Distinguish creators, performers, photographers and platform delivery. Treat queer and trans artists as authors with agency; do not infer identity from styling or reduce it to a decorative trope. Avoid automatic parody, misogynistic product framing, dangerous flash effects and unlicensed recordings/lyrics/likenesses. Current streaming previews may differ from original print or animation. Linked public reference media does not grant reuse rights.

## Anti-Patterns

- Chrome blobs plus a distorted font with no particular release or creative concept.
- Treating PC Music, Hyperpop, Digicore and every internet-born pop artist as identical.
- Requiring pink, maximal density, CGI or satire despite documented counterexamples.
- Copying artist identities, treating still blur as measured motion, or calling a static preview a watched video.
- Fake playback, automatic sound, global keyboard traps, drag-only controls or unreadable mirrored labels.

# Cottagecore

Cottagecore is a contemporary internet/community aesthetic that romanticizes pastoral domestic comfort, slow living, handmade craft, gardens, cottages, and rural self-sufficiency. Use it as a web/app design system when the product needs warmth, care, gentle escapism, or handmade natural abundance rather than urban polish or high-tech speed.

## Scope

The aesthetic's center is not simply florals or beige. It needs a pastoral domestic story: baking, gardening, sewing, foraging, pressed flowers, cottage shelves, teacups, bees, mushrooms, linen, calico, and soft rooms or garden tables. It is useful for editorial commerce, community spaces, recipe/garden/craft surfaces, lifestyle launches, and brands that can credibly support calm, care, and handmade texture.

Keep the look cozy and accessible rather than frictionless rural fantasy. Cottagecore may be aspirational and online-native, but strong implementations preserve labor, sustainability, craft, and domestic practice cues instead of reducing the aesthetic to prairie-dress retail styling.

## 7-Dimension Profile

**Palette**: Warm cream, sage, dusty rose, terracotta, aged linen, herb green, butter yellow, berry red, and other low-saturation pastoral colors. Use candlelit or sun-dappled warmth; avoid synthetic neon contrast, glossy tech gradients, or cold corporate neutrals as the main signal.

**Type**: Soft serifs, handwritten notes, botanical script accents, recipe-card labels, seed-packet typography, and diary-like annotations. Decorative script belongs in small focal moments; body copy, forms, and navigation should stay readable and calm.

**Texture**: Linen, calico, embroidery, watercolor wash, aged paper, wicker, wood, ceramics, pressed flowers, quilts, doilies, sourdough crust, and garden tactility. Texture should feel handmade and cared for, not dirty, feral, or randomly distressed.

**Shape**: Rounded, organic, floral, and irregular forms: cottage windows, baskets, teacups, ruffles, trellises, garden clusters, puff-sleeve softness, and hand-cut labels. Keep geometry gentle and domestic rather than hard-edged, industrial, or aggressively symmetrical.

**Motion**: Gentle, unhurried, and seasonal: slow fades, drifting curtains, dappled light, soft page turns, recipe reveals, and small garden-growth transitions. Avoid snappy gamified motion or high-energy kinetic spectacle.

**Spatial**: Abundant but curated domestic fullness: cottage shelves, garden tables, cozy rooms, intimate outdoor nooks, and home-and-garden storytelling. Leave enough breathing room for usability, but avoid sterile emptiness that removes the sense of a lived-in handmade world.

**Cultural markers**: Rural self-sufficiency, slow living, sustainability, baking, gardening, handcraft, mushrooms, bees, pressed flowers, teacups, billowy dresses, picnics, bookshelves, candlelight, and nostalgia for a gentler life. Treat the online community context as part of the signal, not just a product moodboard.

## Non-Negotiables

**Non-negotiables**:

- Pastoral domestic comfort tied to cottages, gardens, food, craft, or household care.
- Handmade/natural texture through textiles, paper, botanicals, ceramics, wood, or craft processes.
- Warm low-saturation organic palette with soft, nostalgic light.
- Gentle slow-living tone rather than urban speed, luxury gloss, or high-tech precision.

## Connotation

**Mode:** contemporary internet nostalgia.

Cottagecore reads as anti-hustle escapism, comfort, self-reliance aspiration, and handmade domestic romance. It can be queer, community-coded, sustainability-minded, or retailer-amplified depending on context; do not present it as a timeless or politically neutral rural reality.

## Related / Subsets

- `goblincore` overlaps in mushrooms, foraging, and anti-urban nature feeling, but Goblincore is muddy, creaturely, and anti-pristine where Cottagecore stays pastoral, domestic, and carefully cozy.

## Frontend / UI Guidance

Use Cottagecore on surfaces that can benefit from care, slowness, and domestic warmth: recipe libraries, gardening tools, craft commerce, community journals, cozy editorial pages, lifestyle onboarding, and soft product storytelling. Build the primary task flow with clear modern accessibility, then layer the aesthetic through botanical dividers, recipe-card modules, seed-packet labels, soft paper panels, illustrated empty states, and garden-table composition.

Favor component states that feel handmade but legible: stitched or paper-cut borders, pressed-flower badges, herb-garden icon sets, handwritten annotations for secondary notes, and warm tactile cards. Keep controls predictable; the aesthetic should soften the interface, not hide affordances.

## CSS Translation

- Color roles: `--bg-cream`, `--surface-linen`, `--surface-paper`, `--accent-sage`, `--accent-rose`, `--accent-terracotta`, `--accent-berry`, and `--text-bark`.
- Borders/dividers: thin paper edges, stitched outlines, soft botanical rules, seed-packet frames, and watercolor separators.
- Radius language: medium to high organic radii, hand-cut irregular SVG masks, scallops, soft arches, and pill-like tags; avoid sharp industrial geometry.
- Effects: subtle paper grain, watercolor washes, linen overlays, pressed-flower illustrations, warm inner shadows, and low-contrast drop shadows.
- Layout: cozy card clusters, shelf-like groupings, garden-table sections, recipe-card grids, and curated fullness with readable scan paths.
- Motion: slow opacity/translate transitions, dappled-light overlays, gentle hover lifts, and reduced-motion equivalents that preserve softness without animation.

## Typography / Fonts

Pair a readable old-style or bookish serif with restrained handwritten or botanical script accents. Good direction includes warm editorial serifs for headings, legible humanist sans or serif body text, and recipe-card annotation styles for labels or notes.

Avoid all-script interfaces, fragile low-contrast type, or novelty fonts that turn the aesthetic into costume. The typographic hierarchy should feel personal and printed while remaining practical for forms, navigation, and dense content.

## Cultural / Ethical Notes

Cottagecore can romanticize rural life while ignoring labor, class, poverty, sanitation, land access, and sustainability realities. It also carries critiques around Eurocentrism, heteronormativity, and flattening rural aspiration into white/Western pastoral fantasy. Use inclusive imagery and copy, keep rural labor visible when relevant, and avoid turning the aesthetic into tradwife nostalgia or conservative gender essentialism.

## Anti-Patterns

- Reducing the aesthetic to prairie dresses, beige, or flower stickers without slow-living, craft, or domestic practice cues.
- Presenting rural life as a frictionless fantasy with no labor, class, ecological, or access realities.
- Confusing Cottagecore with tradwife nostalgia, generic old-timey femininity, or compulsory domesticity.
- Importing too much mud, bones, creature clutter, or rot, which shifts the signal toward Goblincore.
- Over-polished retail moodboards that keep the props but lose handmade tactility and community context.

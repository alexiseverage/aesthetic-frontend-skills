# Surrealism

Surrealism makes ordinary expectations unstable through unexpected relations: a familiar object made from the wrong material, incompatible scales, discontinuous bodies, contradictory images or associative signs. Its historical identity spans literary experiment, automatic processes, illusionistic painting, photography and objects. Preserve that relational logic rather than reducing the movement to a dreamy palette or a handful of famous motifs.

## Scope

Use for cultural/editorial sites, literary journals, exhibitions, exploratory portfolios and imaginative product stories where unexpected connections help the content. The reference frame is the movement formally announced in 1924 and its documented branches, not every strange image or all work by an associated artist. Abstract signs, precise rooms, photographic traces and physical assemblages can all belong.

Keep essential navigation, forms, errors and reading order literal. Disorientation belongs in the composed relationship between elements, not in unreliable controls. Dense operational workflows need a restrained functional layer.

## 7-Dimension Profile

**Palette**: No universal palette. Monochrome, warm paper, dark earthy rooms, blue sign fields, red textured masses and muted expansive skies coexist. Use tonal separation and one or two selective accents to clarify a particular contradiction. Proposed accessible UI colors should not be presented as historically sampled inks.

**Type**: Language may be an image, an annotation or a contradictory name. Handwritten labels, numerals and calligraphic marks appear in the inspected corpus, but no movement-wide typeface is established. Pair an expressive editorial display treatment with calm readable body text and direct control labels.

**Texture**: Smooth modeled painting, granular marks, wood, photographic traces, fur and scraped or printed-looking surfaces supply different branches. Texture should serve material contradiction or process; a universal noise filter is unnecessary. Keep ornament away from reading and input surfaces.

**Shape**: Familiar silhouettes and signs are displaced, recombined, enlarged, fragmented or given unexpected material behavior. Frames, openings and ordinary apparatus can become part of the proposition. Eyes, clocks and body fragments are optional historic variants, not required icons.

**Motion**: Stillness is fully appropriate to the inspected painting/object/print corpus. Visual tension does not require floating loops or melting animations. Historical film timing is outside this profile. Any contemporary transitions need an explicit purpose, proposed values and a reduced-motion equivalent; preserve stable targets.

**Spatial**: Incompatible scale, deep perspective with impossible contents, detached planes, frame gaps, thresholds and shallow associative sign fields. Large empty areas can be as characteristic as dense assemblage. Maintain a coherent semantic reading order under unusual visual placement.

**Cultural markers**: Literary experimentation, 1924 manifesto lineage, chance and psychological theories, differing individual approaches and international continuation. Surrealism is not a diagnosis or a single apolitical dream mood. Artist identity and culturally specific symbols require context.

## Non-Negotiables

**Non-negotiables**: a deliberate unexpected relationship; coherent composition that makes the contradiction legible; a defensible historical/process reference; and stable functional semantics when translated to an interface. A generic floating shape or pastel gradient alone is insufficient.

## Connotation

**Mode:** contemporary revival.

The style can suggest imagination, ambiguity, estrangement, humor or unease. Choose the intended reading and avoid assuming that eroticized or disturbing historical imagery is required. An original poetic interface can preserve the method without quoting a protected composition.

## Related / Subsets

- `dreamcore-weirdcore` shares strangeness but has internet-native nostalgia, edits and communities; it is not the historical movement's alias.
- `klecksography-inkblot` supplies a specific chance-based process rather than a complete equivalent.
- `german-expressionist-horror` favors theatrical psychological distortion; Surrealism also supports calm perspective, sparse signs and tactile objects.
- `de-stijl` is a contrast for flat abstraction: associative biomorphism does not become an orthogonal primary-color system.
- Automatist/abstract, illusionistic, object/assemblage and photographic branches remain useful prose variants, not separate entries here.

## Frontend / UI Guidance

Compose an explicit index beside displaced reading panes; let framing gaps or mismatched scale connect substantive articles. Use original assets that extend the same pane/object logic. Controls, active markers and headings should share the composition's geometry and typography. Clear text, visible focus, real state changes and meaningful disclosures preserve usability.

## CSS Translation

Use grid areas, bounded clipping, layered frames and original SVG objects for stable contradictory space. Keep decorative transforms separate from hit targets and semantic text. Use reserved dimensions for assets and responsive stacks that preserve article order. A possible application uses warm ground, near-black ink, a blue accent and hard offset frames; exact values are proposals, not universal historical tokens. Disable nonessential transforms, transitions and animated scrolling under `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Typography / Fonts

A high-contrast editorial serif and restrained sans or mono labels are suitable contemporary proposals. System Georgia/Times and Arial/Helvetica avoid new font transfers where their treatment fits. Actual handwriting may appear in an original small signature, never as the sole label for a task. Do not claim historical font identification from painted lettering.

## Cultural / Ethical Notes

Different artists had different political, psychological and cultural positions; do not flatten them into a single theory. Avoid decorative reuse of sacred/community-specific symbols, diagnostic claims and copied famous works. The inspected historical body imagery includes objectification and disturbing fragmentation; those are contextual evidence, not required product motifs. Use direct language and an equivalent accessible interaction.

## Anti-Patterns

- Dreamy color alone, obligatory eyes/clocks or copying a famous scene.
- Randomness without a meaningful relationship or stable content.
- Moving hit targets, unreadable text, hidden labels or deceptive controls justified as surreal.
- Treating all associated artists or all works on a museum theme page as identical members.
- Invented historical motion, precise ink values or font identities from still images.

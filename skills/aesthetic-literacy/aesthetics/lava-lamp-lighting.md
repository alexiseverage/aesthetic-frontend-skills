# Lava Lamp Lighting

## Scope

Self-lit vessels containing moving, cohesive wax forms in transparent or translucent liquid. Bottle geometry, metal or molded supports, and limited light spill establish the object. The effect can serve as an ambient display, product finish study or motion vignette. [Research profile](../../../knowledge/aesthetics/lava-lamp-lighting.md)

## 7-Dimension Profile

**Palette**: Use distinct liquid, wax, lamp foot, and ambient roles. Early ads and modern objects show warm saturated pairs alongside clear fluid, neutral metal, and cooler combinations.

**Type**: Typography is usually external to the vessel. Keep labels compact and use a product-style hierarchy with the finish, model and controls visibly apart.

**Texture**: Layer a lower light source, transmitted color, narrow side reflections and contrasting metal, plastic or frosted surfaces. Confine soft bloom to nearby surfaces.

**Shape**: Use an upright, contained liquid volume. Rounded masses stretch into thin necks, separate, settle and rejoin. Molded forms, rocket feet and cylindrical chimneys are documented variants.

**Motion**: Use slow, continuous deformation with pauses and unequal rising and falling paths. Derive the character from observed lamp states. Pause controls and a static composition preserve the experience when movement is unwanted.

**Spatial**: Make the vessel the brightest, relatively narrow object and keep the cap and base visually stable. Leave surrounding space for falloff before it meets text or controls.

**Cultural markers**: Use the construction, clear role of the heat and light at the base, vintage industrial details, or restrained contemporary material variants. Preserve the distinction between liquid wax and glitter or novelty lighting.

## Non-Negotiables

**Non-negotiables**:

- A stable vessel with a visually independent cap or base and a distinct liquid field.
- A small number of cohesive wax masses with a visible lower source and light falloff.
- Slow deformation or a still state that preserves the wax volumes and their containment.

## Connotation

**Connotation**: Domestic ambient light and industrial-design nostalgia. A colorful vessel rewards attention through patient changes in shape, translucency and brightness.

## Related / Subsets

Atomic Age shares some metallic bases and rocket silhouettes. Lava Lamp Lighting identifies the enclosed, self-lit moving liquid. Psychedelic shares a color history, and Gummy Jelly Design shares translucent masses without the same vessel and bottom-up heat cue.

## Frontend / UI Guidance

Let the user compare bottle finishes and see each at different ambient room-light levels. Keep the contrast of product labels stable. Provide a real pause button. Keep the vessel articulated even when the animation is disabled.

## CSS Translation

Use dark surround `#100c0d` and warm text `#fff3dc` with a saturated treatment inside the vessel. Clip wax and reflections to the bottle. Layer narrow linear gradients for metal and broad radial gradients for a local halo. Use independently phased multi-second cycles and honor reduced motion.

## Typography / Fonts

Pair a readable studio label and small finish names with a broad display face. Keep explanatory and safety information outside the light effect.

## Cultural / Ethical Notes

Treat the display as an original design study. Credit referenced makers and collections. Report playback time and visual proportions as observations limited by framing and exposure.

## Anti-Patterns

- A full-screen fluid effect with the containment and pedestal removed.
- Fast, evenly spaced particles or repeated bounce easing.
- Light spread across body text or aggressively saturated chrome.

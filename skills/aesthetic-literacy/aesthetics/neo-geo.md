# Neo-Geo

## Scope

A loose 1980s New York art association that reworked abstraction, signs, display and commodities with a critical or theoretical purpose. Its geometric painting branch uses enclosures, repeated modules, bright manufactured color and structural paths, but the movement also encompasses branded constructions and consumer-product sculpture. This term does not describe the SNK console or any colorful geometric website.

Translate the relation between order and subject: a system can organize and isolate; a display can make an ordinary thing look collectible; a label can manufacture identity. Use for exhibition programs, collection explorers, critical editorials and object catalogs. Preserve a coherent branch while acknowledging the range rather than piling unrelated period motifs together.

## 7-Dimension Profile

**Palette**: Bounded high-chroma fields, fluorescent/ordinary color clashes, black structural divisions and pale display grounds are common in the painting branch. Muted, nearly black and bronze/transparent objects are documented exceptions. Exact application hex values below are proposals, not sampled historical pigments.

**Type**: Many paintings are text-free. Sign/commodity works use names, labels, trademark-like framing and repeated institutional or commercial language. A clear sans inventory hierarchy is a useful interface interpretation, not a universal historical font. Keep interpretive prose readable and original branding distinct from cited art.

**Texture**: Deliberate rough/smooth contrast, granular paint, cut/printed collage, metal hardware, molded products and polished display surfaces depend on the branch. Use restrained local texture; photographed gallery shadows and reproduction noise are not required image grammar.

**Shape**: Enclosed rectangles, repeated sectors, right-angle channels and strong partitions in geometric systems; constructed cases and isolated products in commodity branches; quoted optical/organic patterns in other work. Halley's cells/conduits are artist-specific, not the only allowed forms or a reusable movement logo.

**Motion**: The inspected evidence is still. Favor stable artwork and immediate deliberate selection. Optical vibration is not measured animation. Optional brief functional feedback is a contemporary proposal; remove nonessential transitions for reduced motion. Do not make a conduit pulse to imply recovered historical behavior.

**Spatial**: Broad fields, emphatic enclosures, serial modules, isolated displays and systematic relationships. Wide, tall, allover and object-on-plinth branches coexist. A rigid UI grid should express a collection/system, not merely place identical cards in rows.

**Cultural markers**: 1980s East Village/New York exhibitions, revisited modernist abstraction, commodity identity, simulation, art-market discourse and technological organization. Geometry is often referential or critical rather than a promise of universal rational purity.

## Non-Negotiables

**Non-negotiables**: a legible chosen branch; deliberate serial/enclosure/display relationships; manufactured color or materially specific surface; a meaningful relation to systems, signs, quotation or commodity presentation. Bright rectangles alone are insufficient. Do not make fluorescence or straight lines compulsory across every work.

## Connotation

**Mode:** contemporary revival with a critical exhibition/editorial framing.

It can communicate systemic tension, conspicuous display and calculated order. The historical label is contested and broad; the artists did not all share one visual program. Use the name precisely and distinguish formal inspiration from institutional critique. Avoid cheerful generic retro-futurism that erases the subject of connection, isolation or commodification.

## Related / Subsets

- **Pop Art** supplies a relation through consumer signs and repetition; Neo-Geo carries a specific 1980s dialogue with abstraction and simulation.
- **Op Art** shares optical systems in selected paintings, while many associated objects have no optical vibration.
- **Memphis / Post-Modern** shares manufactured color and decade; playful ornamental furnishing is a different emphasis from critical partitions and commodity display.
- **Neubrutalism** can resemble a web surface of hard rectangles; thick drop shadows and comic cards do not establish this historical identity.
- Geometric diagrams, sign/brand constructions, quoted abstraction and commodity displays remain internal branches. Neo-Conceptualism and Simulationism overlap historically but are broader than safe universal aliases.

## Frontend / UI Guidance

Use an exhibition explorer with a large program heading, a rectilinear room/collection diagram, one selected object and concise inventory notes. Let structural channels connect meaningful zones. A personal route/list can expose how selection and ordering alter a system. Keep art zones quiet and text/control zones opaque, with clear reading order on mobile.

Room controls need full labels, at least 44px targets, visible focus and text selection cues. Add/remove/reorder actions must change the real list, preserve useful focus and announce results. Do not make a colored map the sole navigation. Use native labeled fields for visit planning; clearly distinguish a personal plan from a purchased ticket or confirmed booking. No autoplay is needed.

## CSS Translation

Proposed roles: ground `#F0EFE8`, ink `#141616`, lime `#D7F348`, red `#F04A32`, blue `#4075D6`, gray `#BABDB3`. Use ink on ground/lime and ground on ink; treat red/blue as graphic fields until actual text contrast is calculated. Use 0px radii, 1px rules, broad 12px structural channels and 8/16/24/48px spacing. Granular infill can be a subtle bounded procedural stipple, never across body text.

Use an asymmetric diagram/object/annotation split and stack in semantic order below the content breakpoint. Borders should clarify enclosure and grouping. Avoid glowing neon, floating glass panels or comic drop shadows as defaults. Keep changes immediate; under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, disable nonessential animation, transition and smooth scrolling.

## Typography / Fonts

A Helvetica Neue/Arial sans stack supports short assertive program headings and readable object labels; system monospace can distinguish inventory numbers. These are contemporary fallbacks, not identified artist fonts. Suggested body 16px/1.5, labels 12–14px and responsive 56–140px short display names. Keep long text in mixed case and separate from optical patterns.

## Cultural / Ethical Notes

Source works and logos remain protected. Create original objects, diagram relationships and names instead of tracing a known canvas, borrowing a museum image or manufacturing an artist attribution. Historical works can contain extremist, religious or stigmatizing signs as critical subject matter; these are not a decorative symbol kit. Do not trivialize incarceration or sell surveillance as a neutral lifestyle motif. An institutional-looking interface must describe its fictional planning state honestly.

## Anti-Patterns

- Confusing Neo-Geo with the game console or any geometric abstraction.
- Reducing a loose association to two neon boxes and a universal font.
- Ignoring rough/smooth material contrast or importing gallery-photo shadows as paint texture.
- Copying a Halley composition, commercial logos or a Bickerton sign inventory.
- Autoplay optical patterns, flashing conduits, color-only states or tiny map controls.
- Treating period critique of commodities as generic celebratory luxury branding.

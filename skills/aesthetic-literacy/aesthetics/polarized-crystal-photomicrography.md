## Scope

Photographic crystal fields whose optical color follows material structure: facets, fans, needles, grains and interference bands. This is a technique/material visual system, not a universal rainbow microscopy skin or a means to identify a substance by appearance.

## 7-Dimension Profile

**Palette**: Region-specific colors bounded by crystalline structures, often saturated but also gold-dominant, pastel or nearly monochrome. Black ground is optional. Exact physical/sample colors are not established from reproductions.

**Type**: Photographs contain no intrinsic lettering. Captions identify source-attributed materials, method and photographer; signatures/watermarks are presentation rather than crystal structure. UI typography is a contemporary choice.

**Texture**: Growth striae, cracks, interference bands, granular fields, needles and stepped contours. Mixed diffraction-grating examples can have softer lobes; not every image is sharply faceted.

**Shape**: Blades, fans, spherulites, layered sheets, grain mosaics and isolated forms. Perfect radial symmetry and a circular microscope vignette are optional, not defining.

**Motion**: The inspected references are still photographs. No stage rotation, growth speed or light-cycle timing was measured. Manual comparison and real resource-loading states are valid modern behaviors; no calibrated polarization simulation is implied.

**Spatial**: Edge-to-edge fields, asymmetric seams/fans and isolated complete crystals coexist. Let the image dominate while captions and controls occupy stable opaque surfaces.

**Cultural markers**: Optical technique, material attribution, named photographers and science/art/industrial contexts. Captioned chemical names are source claims, not visual diagnoses.

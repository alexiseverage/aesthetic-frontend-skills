# Gacha Banner Interfaces

## Scope

Campaign-selection and recruitment interfaces in collection games. A large featured campaign is coordinated with event/category navigation, resource balances, rates/details/history and a clearly priced action tier, often single and multiple draws. Portrait and landscape layouts, ornate fantasy and flat industrial treatments all belong. Results, chest openings and generic character cards alone are outside this narrower grouping.

## 7-Dimension Profile

**Palette**: Pale blue/cream, cyan/black, violet/gray, gold/brown and red/black systems all occur. Use a stable neutral chrome plus campaign-specific accents; cyan and yellow are recurring emphasis choices, not a universal brand palette.

**Type**: High-impact campaign lettering contrasts with plain resource numerals and compact utility labels. Serif fantasy headings and heavy angular sans coexist. Enlarge quantities, guarantee counters or costs without flattening the hierarchy into promotional text everywhere.

**Texture**: Campaign art may sit on clouds, circuit patterns, parchment, CRT-like panels or flat monochrome fields. Glassy/translucent panels and metallic trims are variants. Emulator toolbars, system watermarks and guide annotations are external to the game interface.

**Shape**: Framed or slanted tabs, thumbnail rails, currency pills, star/rank marks, compact utility buttons and larger priced actions. Rounded white capsules, hard rectangles, ornate frames and diamond buttons provide observed alternatives.

**Motion**: The qualifying corpus is static game-interface capture. It does not establish reveal choreography, easing or animation durations. Keep banner selection and information disclosure immediate in a still showcase; any future reveal needs playback research rather than inference from sparkle stills.

**Spatial**: A dominant campaign field is bounded by category navigation, resource/status information and an action tier. Left thumbnail rails, top tabs and bottom carousels vary by device. Details, availability and costs remain accessible outside the artwork.

**Cultural markers**: Free-to-play collection-game campaign and recruitment systems, including Japanese, Chinese, Korean and other game contexts. Not every game shares pity, rates or payment rules. A beta, tutorial or historic screen is not evidence of present-day mechanics.

## Non-Negotiables

**Non-negotiables**: Coordinated campaign/category selection, visible resource or progress context, and explicit information/action hierarchy. Paired single/multiple actions are common, not mandatory.

## Connotation

**Mode:** Contemporary or nostalgic quotation.

A contemporary quotation of a campaign-selection interface: anticipatory, collectible and highly organized. It can serve a free asset-pack browser or catalog sampler without reproducing paid random purchasing.

## Related / Subsets

JRPG Command Menus organize moment-to-moment actions; Trading Card Game Design organizes individual card metadata and tabletop relationships. Capsule Toy Display Walls repeat physical placard/control/hatch modules. Gacha Banner Interfaces own the digital campaign/resource/details/action relationship.

## Frontend / UI Guidance

Build an original object-pack catalog: campaign thumbnails change the collection, details disclose the actual contents, and single/ten selection actions add an explicit number to a review tray. Resource and selection counts update coherently; reset restores them. Use ordinary buttons, labeled quantities, visible active states and a readable contents list. Original relics or tools can replace portraits while retaining the complete chrome grammar.

## CSS Translation

Proposed values: navy #172337, pale #eff3e9, cyan #72dae3, gold #f2c972, 1px panel border, 4px utility radius, 24px primary-action radius, 8/16/24px spacing. Use a three-region grid, translucent neutral information panes and an original SVG faceted-object field. Reflow rails into a horizontal selector on narrow screens; keep cost labels and main actions uncut. All values are application proposals, not sampled game tokens.

## Typography / Fonts

Proposed category pairing: Georgia for a short fantasy campaign title and a system sans for balances, utility navigation and buttons. Use tabular numerals. A heavy sans campaign is equally supported. Do not copy game logos or infer a particular proprietary font from pixels.

## Cultural / Ethical Notes

Use original campaign names, objects and interface art. Disclose exact contents and deterministic behavior in the application where relevant. Do not copy false scarcity, hidden costs or undocumented probabilities. Source character art and screenshots are research only; people imagery must not carry the showcase.

## Anti-Patterns

- A character poster without functioning campaign navigation or details.
- Treating every result screen as a banner.
- Rarity communicated through color alone.
- Inventing animation timing or universal pity rules from stills.

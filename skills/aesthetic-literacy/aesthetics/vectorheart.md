# Vectorheart

## Scope

A retrospective cluster of constructed vector geometry, industrial symbols and technical typographic composition. Flat silhouettes, diagram-like assemblies and precision microtype organize the image. Some works combine vector and rendered elements; the distinction is a dominant graphic construction system, not a claim that all source files were made with one tool.

## 7-Dimension Profile

**Palette**: Black/white and sharp spot accents; red, blue, orange, purple and restrained pale variants. Contrast isolates constructed masses and information bands. Monochrome is useful but not compulsory.

**Type**: Constructed modular titles, condensed capitals, tabular serials and fine utility typography. Labels align to rails, grids or edges. Metadata can establish project-specific fonts, such as Neubau’s NB-Form; no universal font is established.

**Texture**: Crisp cut silhouettes, wireframe or line overlays, flat vector-like fills and selective print finishes. Debossing and spot varnish are source-supported in individual packaging projects. Rough paper/grunge is optional, not defining.

**Shape**: Angular fragments, circles, chevrons, crosshairs, grids, bar-like modules and diagrammatic structures. Repeated geometry behaves like a constructed alphabet, with deliberate cuts and apertures.

**Spatial**: Asymmetric dense graphic mass against quiet space, disciplined caption bands, modular alignments and oversized cropped geometric elements. Give functional data a stable grid rather than scattering fake numbers.

**Cultural markers**: Experimental music packaging, graphic-design publications, technical imagery and turn-of-millennium digital studios. Precision does not imply military authenticity or proprietary technical data.

**Motion**: Historical timing/easing is unknown in this still-led corpus. A current application may be intentionally still, with immediate state changes or brief optional feedback. Any squash, reveal or parameter transition is a design proposal, not an observed historical standard. Reduced motion preserves the complete composition and every task.

## Frontend / UI Guidance

Build a poster proof editor with module visibility toggles, a grid control and a meaningful export preview. Selected layers appear in an aligned list; the large graphic reacts to those controls. Use geometric edge tabs and plain numeric fields.

Use real buttons, labeled inputs and visible selected/disabled/error states. Support keyboard operation, 44px touch targets and announced feedback. Reflow modules on narrow screens; decorative geometry must not intercept pointer events or obscure focus. Use original abstract/object artwork; people imagery and copied source screenshots should not carry the interface identity.

## Cultural / Ethical Notes

Experimental music packaging, graphic-design publications, technical imagery and turn-of-millennium digital studios. Precision does not imply military authenticity or proprietary technical data. Credit creators and preserve uncertainty; source access is not a reuse license. Do not trace signature compositions, copy characters/marks, or turn sacred/personal symbols into generic ornament. Keep demonstration prices, schedules and form outcomes honest.

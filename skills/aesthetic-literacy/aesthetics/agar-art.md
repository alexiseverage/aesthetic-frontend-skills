# Agar Art

Art using microbial cultures and agar as an image-making medium, including representational, abstract and mixed-media work.

Evidence basis: [the research profile](../../../knowledge/aesthetics/agar-art.md) records 30 qualifying unique artifacts, source distribution, claim support and limitations. This is a descriptive catalog category. Exact historical colors, fonts and screen timings are not inferred from appearance; proposed frontend values below are contemporary translations.

## Scope

Art using microbial cultures and agar as an image-making medium, including representational, abstract and mixed-media work. The visual translation retains bounded material fields and colony/streak variation. It is not a clinical culture report or every digitally generated blob image.

## 7-Dimension Profile

**Palette**: Pigment-rich local colony color, translucent grounds and strong neighboring contrasts. Natural pigment, fluorescence, bioluminescence, dye and added watercolor are different source-specific mechanisms, not interchangeable explanations.

**Type**: Rim marks, specimen notes and captions support the image. Letterforms can be made in cultures but no universal font is established. Editorial typography should remain readable outside the material field.

**Texture**: Colony beads, rough streaks, branching edges, spreading halos and irregular accumulation. Smooth gradient blobs omit the medium; some mixed-media works preserve or supplement the traces.

**Shape**: Round dishes recur, but rectangular trays, multi-container compositions and mixed round/square works are substantial variants. Preserve complete finite container boundaries.

**Motion**: The researched corpus is still photography. Growth over time is source-supported, not a measured animation. Do not fabricate incubation progress; optional gallery/lightbox transitions are contemporary proposals and removable.

**Spatial**: Bounded shallow fields, overhead display, grouped containers and quiet surrounds. Perspective installations and process photography broaden the overhead-on-black shorthand.

**Cultural markers**: Named artists, science/art collaboration, exhibitions and ASM contest culture. Organism identity, viability or clinical meaning cannot be diagnosed from a photograph.

## Non-Negotiables

**Non-negotiables**:

- A bounded agar/material-field relationship.
- Irregular colony, streak or growth-trace texture rather than smooth digital blobs.
- Clear art/source context with synthetic translations labeled honestly.

## Connotation

**Mode:** Contemporary exhibition interpretation: lively material color within a disciplined gallery, without a biohazard or diagnostic fiction.

The implementation should quote the documented material and organizational relationships. Its contemporary controls and readable type must remain honest about source and application limits.

## Related / Subsets

- Organic Digital shares rounded forms and gradients, but does not require culture-medium provenance or bounded colony surfaces.
- Polarized Crystal Photomicrography is an optical/material photographic practice with facets, striations and extinction fields; agar art is authored or emergent growth on agar.
- Haeckelian Biological Plates organizes comparative printed morphology and numbered figures; photographed culture surfaces are not scientific engraving.
- Clinical laboratory interfaces can also show petri dishes, but an assay report's purpose and claims differ from an exhibition of authored artworks. Do not borrow diagnostic status or warning codes as decoration.
- Bio-art is the broader parent territory; not every biological installation, GAN artwork, microscopy video or porcelain culture reproduction belongs to agar art itself.

No separate canonical subsets are introduced.

## Frontend / UI Guidance

Use an exhibition grid with meaningful composition filters, preserved whole containers and a focused lightbox with title, material and caption. Filters should update results and support empty states. Dialogs need Escape, close, focus containment and restoration; preserve artwork scale on mobile and avoid autoplay.

## CSS Translation

Stable warm-white or charcoal editorial chrome, one restrained control accent and rich color inside art. Combine varied colony sizes, beaded tracks and translucent substrate in original assets; use both round and rectangular compositions.

Proposed application roles, not measured historical samples: background `#f1eee5`, surface `#fffdf6`, text `#27322b`, muted text `#56635b`, action `#2f5d4c`, secondary `#e7cf65`, accent `#b64158`. Proposed small/medium/large radii: 2/6/16 px. Use a 4/8/12/16/24/32 px spacing scale. Validate actual rendered text, controls and states independently of these illustrative tokens.

## Typography / Fonts

A clear editorial sans and restrained caption face are proposed. No microbiology-specific font is established; avoid tiny laboratory labels as a style shortcut.

## Cultural / Ethical Notes

This is visual analysis, not culturing instructions or diagnostic interpretation. Credit source artists. Original digital studies are not live cultures, microscopy evidence or photographs of real experimental work.

## Anti-Patterns

- Generic neon blobs with no substrate or container.
- All circles, all black grounds or a universal pigment mechanism.
- Fake growth timers, organism diagnoses and copied contest artwork.

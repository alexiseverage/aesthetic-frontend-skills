# Materia Medica Collections

The material and information design of teaching/reference collections of raw medicinal substances: particular materials held in containers, compartments and indexed records.

Evidence basis: [the research profile](../../../knowledge/aesthetics/materia-medica-collections.md) records 23 qualifying unique artifacts, source distribution, claim support and limitations. This is a descriptive catalog category. Exact historical colors, fonts and screen timings are not inferred from appearance; proposed frontend values below are contemporary translations.

## Scope

The material and information design of teaching/reference collections of raw medicinal substances: particular materials held in containers, compartments and indexed records. It is distinct from a pressed-plant herbarium, decorative apothecary branding and a contemporary prescription system.

## 7-Dimension Profile

**Palette**: Clear glass, cream paper, wood and contents spanning brown/olive, amber gum, red fragments and vivid minerals. Photographic black backgrounds are documentation choices, not historic cabinet paint.

**Type**: Compact names, taxonomic/provenance lines, handwritten additions and external keys. Some vessels show only numbers or no legible front label; the record provides identification. No single vintage font is required.

**Texture**: Transparent glass contrasts fibrous, flaky, granular, resinous or solid contents. Cork, paper/string caps, screw caps, liners and conservation padding differ; contents must not become interchangeable generic leaves.

**Shape**: Upright cylinders, slender pod tubes, shouldered powder bottles, glass-front rectangular boxes and compartment drawers adapt to material morphology. Repeated containment organizes irregular substances.

**Motion**: Still object photographs establish no historical handling timing. Filtering, disclosure and local reading-list actions are proposed web behavior. Keep source material quiet and remove optional transitions under reduced motion.

**Spatial**: Baselines, repeated containers, unequal compartments and identifying keys. Isolated studio photographs do not establish a historical shelf arrangement; cabinet and drawer views support organization separately.

**Cultural markers**: Teaching, pharmacognosy, reference holdings and collection provenance. These Western-held examples do not define every medical tradition or establish therapeutic validity.

## Non-Negotiables

**Non-negotiables**:

- A visibly particular raw material and an appropriate container.
- A retrievable identification/provenance relationship.
- Collection organization rather than treatment or retail remedy selection.

## Connotation

**Mode:** Contemporary collection-journal quotation: material curiosity and documented uncertainty, with historical context retained.

The implementation should quote the documented material and organizational relationships. Its contemporary controls and readable type must remain honest about source and application limits.

## Related / Subsets

- Autumn Field Guide Naturalism shares observation and identification, but organizes seasonal field evidence and illustrated/pressed specimens; this category centers physically contained raw materials and teaching/reference storage.
- Albarello Pharmacy Ceramics foregrounds painted ceramic volumes, glaze and decorative registers. A sealed raw-material reference vial or divided teaching drawer has a different design engine, even when both relate to historical pharmacy.
- Prescription Label Clarity centers current patient instructions and medication safety. Historical accession labels are not dosage labels and should not be adapted into clinical guidance.
- Spooky Science Lab centers classroom experiments and seasonal demonstrations. Materia medica collections document preserved materials; theatrical bubbles, hazardous procedures and glowing potion imagery obscure the distinction.
- Dark Academia can share wood and paper atmosphere, but a collection record needs material identity, containment and provenance rather than a library mood alone.

No separate canonical subsets are introduced.

## Frontend / UI Guidance

Use a specimen journal with a large material illustration, compact accession column, related-record selection and a provenance accordion. Selection must update image, title and notes together. Keep source uncertainty readable and mobile metadata adjacent to its object; native disclosure controls should work without hover.

## CSS Translation

Warm paper and dark ink support thin cabinet-like rules. Put glass highlights and material texture inside original artwork, keep body surfaces opaque, and use rectangular fields with restrained depth rather than full-page glassmorphism.

Proposed application roles, not measured historical samples: background `#ece5d5`, surface `#fbf7eb`, text `#302e26`, muted text `#625e50`, action `#4a5339`, secondary `#b6a27c`, accent `#906140`. Proposed small/medium/large radii: 0/2/4 px. Use a 4/8/12/16/24/32 px spacing scale. Validate actual rendered text, controls and states independently of these illustrative tokens.

## Typography / Fonts

Readable book serif headings, neutral sans metadata and tabular identifiers are proposed. Use botanical italics only for verified names; avoid faux handwritten medical authority.

## Cultural / Ethical Notes

Historical uses are historical claims, never preparation or treatment instructions. Preserve unknown makers, reconstructed contents, colonial acquisition questions and knowledge-holder credit where documented. No diagnosis or remedy recommendation follows from an image.

## Anti-Patterns

- Generic leaves in identical jars regardless of source substance.
- Invented curative claims or prescription-like controls.
- Unqualified colonial provenance and artificial distress on every surface.

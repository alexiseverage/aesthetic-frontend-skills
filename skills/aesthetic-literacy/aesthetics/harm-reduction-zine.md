# Harm Reduction Zine

Harm Reduction Zine is a community-care print and interface aesthetic that combines DIY zine tactility with urgent instructional clarity. Its purpose is practical peer support: safety steps, resource strips, warnings, and mutual-aid distribution, not edgy decoration.

## Scope

Use it for resource hubs, emergency guides, outreach tools, public-health explainers, safer-use instructions, community dashboards, and printable handouts. Avoid it for decorative campaigns detached from care, safety, consent, or resource access.

## 7-Dimension Profile

**Palette**: High-contrast black/white print bases, urgent accent colors, resource-zone highlights, and restrained risograph or photocopy tones. Palette must keep instructions readable.

**Type**: Heavy serif or sans headings, clear body copy, pamphlet hierarchy, handwritten annotations, bilingual-friendly layouts, and plain-language warnings.

**Texture**: Photocopy grain, risograph misregistration, folds, stamps, marker circles, tape, cut-and-paste diagrams, pocket-zine tactility, and printable artifact memory.

**Shape**: Panels, checklists, warning boxes, illustrated steps, folded-page pagination, sticker callouts, dosage/resource cards, and hotline/resource strips.

**Motion**: Accordion reveals, sequential instruction steps, emergency-mode expansion, low-friction utility transitions, and printable fallbacks. Motion should never delay critical help.

**Spatial**: Compact but legible modules, poster-to-pamphlet adaptability, repeatable steps, strong top-to-bottom scan, and clear separation between warnings, instructions, and resources.

**Cultural markers**: Naloxone cards, safer-use explainers, bilingual health sheets, peer-distributed resources, mutual-aid language, hotline/resource strips, street-medic practicality, and nonjudgmental care tone.

## Non-Negotiables

**Non-negotiables**:

- Instructional clarity over anti-design aggression.
- Peer-care or mutual-aid tone, not spectacle.
- Printable/resource-strip logic that works offline.
- Warnings and emergency actions remain highly legible.

## Connotation

**Mode:** authentic community-care utility.

This aesthetic requires ethical restraint: do not aestheticize crisis, obscure instructions, or turn harm-reduction work into edgy decoration.

## Related / Subsets

- `punk-zine` shares DIY print texture, but Harm Reduction Zine prioritizes peer-care clarity over anti-design aggression.
- `constructivism` overlaps in urgent graphic hierarchy, but this is bottom-up mutual aid rather than top-down mobilization.

## Frontend / UI Guidance

Design for fast comprehension, emergency use, translation, printing, and low-bandwidth access. Use resource strips, checklists, step-by-step accordions, offline PDF/print routes, and clear escalation actions.

## CSS Translation

- Color roles: `--bg-paper`, `--ink-copy`, `--accent-urgent`, `--accent-resource`, `--surface-warning`, and `--line-fold`.
- Borders/dividers: cut-paper frames, checklist rules, sticker outlines, and warning boxes.
- Radius language: mostly square paper panels with occasional sticker/card edges.
- Effects: photocopy grain, misregistration offsets, stamp marks, and tape accents only away from critical text.
- Layout: modular pamphlet grid, printable panels, resource strips, and emergency-first hierarchy.
- Motion: immediate expand/collapse and step progress with reduced-motion and print fallbacks.

## Typography / Fonts

Pair bold utilitarian headings with highly readable body type. Handwritten or stamped accents should label non-critical cues, not dosage, warnings, or emergency instructions.

## Cultural / Ethical Notes

Use nonjudgmental language and avoid sensational imagery. If medical, legal, or emergency details are present, source and update them responsibly; visual style must not outrank safety.

## Anti-Patterns

- Punk collage that makes instructions hard to read.
- Crisis imagery used as brand edge.
- Tiny low-contrast dosage or hotline text.
- Decorative bilingual fragments without real translation support.
- Web-only interactions with no printable/offline path.

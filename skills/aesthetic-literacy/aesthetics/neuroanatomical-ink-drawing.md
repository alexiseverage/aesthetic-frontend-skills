# Neuroanatomical Ink Drawing

Historical microscopic neural drawing and its publication grammar: differentiated bodies/fibres, stipple and local annotation serving explanation.

Evidence basis: [the research profile](../../../knowledge/aesthetics/neuroanatomical-ink-drawing.md) records 30 qualifying unique artifacts, source distribution, claim support and limitations. This is a descriptive catalog category. Exact historical colors, fonts and screen timings are not inferred from appearance; proposed frontend values below are contemporary translations.

## Scope

Historical microscopic neural drawing and its publication grammar: differentiated bodies/fibres, stipple and local annotation serving explanation. It includes cells, glia, sections and plexuses; it is not every branching doodle, brain icon or animated neural network.

## 7-Dimension Profile

**Palette**: Dark ink/graphite on pale paper with wash, gray tones and occasional corrections. Sepia, stains and blue accession stamps are archival context rather than compulsory biology or UI tokens.

**Type**: Small mixed-case letters, numbers and leader lines connect local details; manuscript notes and printed captions differ. Exact original fonts are unknown and labels need not cover every branch.

**Texture**: Fine stippling, hatching, wash, granular nuclei and overlapping thin lines. Heavy black arbors and almost empty sheets coexist; preserve varied mark weight and selective density.

**Shape**: Differentiated bodies, tapered branches, bundles, irregular terminals and layered sections. The iconic isolated neuron is one variant among broader neural tissue studies.

**Motion**: The source corpus is still drawing/reproduction. Explanatory arrows are not signal playback. Use manual annotation selection; optional highlight transitions are modern proposals removed under reduced motion.

**Spatial**: Quiet paper, locally dense fields, section boundaries, crossing bundles and letter/detail relationships. Do not turn every study into a full-bleed dendrite wallpaper.

**Cultural markers**: Scientific observation, publication practice, named researchers and institutional custody. The Cajal-school-weighted corpus is a bounded historical branch, not a total history or a clinical simulation.

## Non-Negotiables

**Non-negotiables**:

- Differentiated mark types that explain neural morphology.
- Selective density and readable whole/detail relationships.
- Annotation/caption structure without unsupported functional simulation.

## Connotation

**Mode:** Contemporary journal quotation: patient observation and intellectual precision, with historical drawing treated as evidence rather than decoration.

The implementation should quote the documented material and organizational relationships. Its contemporary controls and readable type must remain honest about source and application limits.

## Related / Subsets

- Haeckelian Biological Plates shares comparative morphology, labels and paper, but its illustrated lithographic organisms, decorative plate symmetry and whole-body forms differ from selective neural tissues and pathways.
- Anatomical Flap Atlases shares explanatory anatomy and print; physically layered flaps and reveal order are a separate book-engineering system.
- Generative network graphics may share branching, yet geometric nodes, straight edges and synthetic data are not evidence of historical cell drawing.
- Klecksography / Inkblot Graphics can share dark ink on pale paper; folded ink shapes and perceptual play differ from an observed/constructed neuroanatomical argument.
- Polarized Crystal Photomicrography is photographic optical evidence with material interference fields; an ink rendering is a mediated drawing with selective emphasis.

No separate canonical subsets are introduced.

## Frontend / UI Guidance

Pair a long-form article with a large original ink study and linked annotation list. Native text buttons should activate the same detail as a visual marker; all captions remain readable without hover or color. Stack article, figure and list on mobile; keep the complete drawing visible.

## CSS Translation

Opaque pale reading surface, varied SVG stroke widths, stippled bodies and fine asymmetric branches. Keep essential prose outside dense drawing and use a distinct active marker plus text. No automatic firing or pulsing is required.

Proposed application roles, not measured historical samples: background `#eeeae1`, surface `#fbf8f0`, text `#282c2e`, muted text `#5d6263`, action `#3d4b56`, secondary `#d6c8b2`, accent `#a35b3f`. Proposed small/medium/large radii: 0/2/4 px. Use a 4/8/12/16/24/32 px spacing scale. Validate actual rendered text, controls and states independently of these illustrative tokens.

## Typography / Fonts

Readable serif article type with neutral small metadata and stable letter markers is a proposed pairing. Avoid fake archival handwriting and invented catalogue stamps.

## Cultural / Ethical Notes

Name source researchers and the limits of historical interpretations. Original artistic schematics are not diagnostic images or modern functional models. Do not imply that drawings reveal a person’s thought, mood or disease.

## Anti-Patterns

- Endless firing neurons, random pulses or brain wallpaper.
- Universal dense arbors and forced antique stains.
- Tiny SVG hotspots as the only route to information.

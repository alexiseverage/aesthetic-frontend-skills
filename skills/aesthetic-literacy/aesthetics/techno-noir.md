# Techno-noir

Techno-noir brings speculative technology into noir investigation: evidence is partial, authority is compromised, and technical power has social consequences. Its identity is a relationship between light, material, information and mistrust, rather than one neon palette.

## Scope

Use for investigative editorial tools, fictional archives and speculative narratives where uncertain knowledge and unequal technology matter. An ordinary analytics dashboard does not acquire this meaning from scanlines. Preserve the distinction between critical fiction and real surveillance products.

## 7-Dimension Profile

**Palette**: dark surrounds and local illuminated evidence; cyan/green monitors, grey casing and amber environments recur. Clean black/white corporate systems and daylight cityscapes are documented variants. No mandatory six-color scheme.

**Type**: practical records, technical labels, narrow numerics, coordinates and signs. Condensed/monospaced categories are useful; sparse sans and coarse dot-matrix commerce also occur. Keep essential modern text readable.

**Texture**: optical depth, glass reflection, defocus, grain, seams and retrofitted hardware can communicate age/status. Polished privilege is a meaningful counterpoint; distress is not universal.

**Shape**: framed observations, thick instrument housings, rectangular windows, circular optics, access slots and reticles. Blinds and diagonal light are options, not compulsory geometry.

**Motion**: observed interface reels switch records, slide/focus card-like information and reframe scans. Still artifacts are also complete expressions. Exact genre-wide speed/easing, rain cycles and alarm pulses are unsupported; modern interaction timing is a proposal.

**Spatial**: incomplete evidence is situated inside layered, unequal environments. Local light against heavy surrounds, records beside machinery and old architecture beside advanced devices recur. Sparse corporate emptiness and daylight commercial scale are alternatives.

**Cultural markers**: noir uncertainty, artificial identity, class, corporate/institutional power and fatalism; Blade Runner1982/2049, The Terminator1984 and Ghost in the Shell2017 supply distinct contexts. The research is concentrated in film production and does not stand for all regions/media.

## Non-Negotiables

**Non-negotiables**:

- Make speculative technology consequential to investigation, identity, access or power.
- Compose a clear relationship between what can be seen and what remains uncertain.
- Give equipment/materials a situated purpose and social context.
- Preserve readable navigation and explicit real interaction states.

## Connotation

**Mode:** contemporary revival.

Translate cinematic tension into an original usable artifact. The source films' human/artificial bodies remain part of their history; an object-led application can express the same tensions through damaged records, withheld access and optical equipment without copying a character.

## Related / Subsets

Cyberpunk shares technological inequality. Techno-noir foregrounds mistrust and investigation; it is not universally slower. Synthwave nostalgia and generic HMI telemetry need additional narrative/material grounding to become techno-noir.

## Frontend / UI Guidance

Begin with a useful evidence-reading, comparison or archive interaction. Establish a luminous inspection zone, separate readable captions and an explicit confidence/uncertainty boundary. Keep material texture behind content. Native controls should select, compare, save or annotate actual states; do not present fictional decryption as real progress or force users through suspense delays.

## CSS Translation

- Proposed roles: near-black casing, cool muted scan field, warm paper and one warning accent. These are application choices, not sampled universal tokens.
- Use inset borders, restrained radial lighting and a low-opacity fixed raster to suggest optics; avoid flashing layers and continuous glitching.
- Build thick frames and sparse technical marks around original diagrams, with essential text on solid high-contrast surfaces.
- Reflow instruments and records vertically on phones; preserve controls and readable labels rather than clipping a desktop console.
- Prefer immediate user-triggered changes for inspectable evidence. If motion is useful, declare modern duration/easing and provide the same final state under reduced motion.

## Typography / Fonts

A system mono for equipment labels plus readable sans/serif record copy can express technical and archival layers. Category and hierarchy matter more than a fake police font. Film lettering is not automatically licensed; no exact face or historic timing follows from stills.

## Cultural / Ethical Notes

Retain critical context around surveillance, bodily classification, corporate power and unequal access. Do not naturalize fictional police profiling, racialized city stereotypes or dehumanizing classification as neutral product values. Use original fictional records and avoid protected film marks, copied screens and decorative pseudo-Asian lettering.

## Anti-Patterns

- Mandatory grime/neon/rain on every surface, ignoring clean corporate and daylight variants.
- Unreadable tiny telemetry replacing essential content.
- Generic cyberpunk HUD decoration without investigation or social stakes.
- Fake indefinite decoding, flashing alarms, unrequested sound or compulsory motion.
- Treating a production concept as proof of final filmed color, timing or an exact software behavior.

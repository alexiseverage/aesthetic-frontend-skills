# Health Goth

## Scope

A networked aesthetic articulated by Chris Cantino and Magic Fades members Mike Grabarek and Jeremy Scott in 2013. Their image curation connects specialized sportswear, clothing fetishism, cleanliness, body technology, rendered environments and commercial imagery. The name does not prescribe exercise, physical ability, a body type or a single music genre.

Preserve the tension between organic bodies and engineered surfaces: fitted or exaggerated technical layers, hard/soft membranes, sparse commercial marks and clean or artificial space. Black clothing alone is insufficient. A current interface should apply material contrast, seam geometry, technical typography and spatial restraint to its working controls and hierarchy.

## 7-Dimension Profile

**Palette**: Black, off-white, silver and charcoal are frequent. Pale blue, translucent gray, plant green and occasional colored framing expand the corpus. Use deliberate value contrast rather than assuming every surface must be black. Functional labels require tested solid backgrounds.

**Type**: Short forceful sports lettering, spaced uppercase labels, compact technical captions, repeated wordmarks and directional instructions. No universal font is established. Condensed system sans and monospace metadata are useful contemporary choices; do not copy commercial logos or make body text a tiny specification label.

**Texture**: Mesh/perforation, glossy shells, matte cloth, translucent film, metal hardware, rubber-like suits and synthetic reflective forms. Represent material differences through restrained CSS patterns, seam lines and original drawings. The juxtaposition matters more than a stock photo or a uniform grain overlay.

**Shape**: Curving body-contour tracks meet rectangular wraps, straps, clips, high collars, gathered volumes, layered panels and plinths. Controls may use clipped ends, tensioned bands and inset construction. Keep an ordinary rectangular hit area and visible keyboard focus.

**Motion**: Played films show garment turns and forward/back movement, camera travel, reflections and water. These do not establish a standard web animation. Intentional stillness can foreground construction and comparison; immediate selection feedback is sufficient. Any added movement must be user-triggered and removable under reduced motion.

**Spatial**: Isolated figures or objects in empty gray/white space, controlled industrial corridors, clean display platforms, strong seams and dark/light partitions. Memes also relocate sports marks to everyday objects. Use a disciplined panel grid with meaningful material zones and a linear mobile order rather than a generic dark dashboard.

**Cultural markers**: Portland creative networks, Facebook/Tumblr curation, technical fashion, transhumanist speculation, advertising recontextualization and disputed mainstream interpretations. Originators expressed ambivalence about ideals of perfection. A creator being cited as an influence does not make all their work Health Goth.

## Non-Negotiables

**Non-negotiables**: engineered clothing/material or rendered-body relationships; sparse, deliberate technical geometry and value structure; tension between commercial/functional cues and an uncanny or subcultural reading; visual treatment extending through the usable interface. Black, logos, a gym, animation and any particular body are optional.

## Connotation

**Mode:** contemporary translation of early-2010s networked material and fashion imagery.

Convey precision, bodily autonomy and unusual material combinations. Participant testimony rejects the reduction to exercising in dark clothes. Use the aesthetic as a critical visual vocabulary, without making health, performance or enhancement promises.

## Related / Subsets

- **Nu Goth / Pastel Goth:** shared contemporary dark fashion; occult or pastel motifs alone do not supply technical material/body relationships.
- **Bootleg Streetwear Logo Remix:** brand displacement overlaps; Health Goth adds specialized clothing, artificial space and body/material concerns.
- **Techno Noir:** engineered darkness overlaps; cinematic crime, neon city scenes and terminal interfaces are not defining here.
- **High Tech Architecture:** exposed structure and engineered surfaces overlap, but architectural construction alone lacks the body/clothing and networked context.
- **Seapunk:** neighboring creative networks and synthetic imagery; aquatic fantasy does not define Health Goth.
- Clinical white, black shell, logo intervention and rendered-space examples are variations, not independently promoted subsets.

## Frontend / UI Guidance

Apply the language to a technical wardrobe planner. Contour seams divide a product's material zones; strap-like tabs select garments; mesh surfaces carry secondary specifications; a clean light comparison sheet contrasts the dark catalog. The title, fieldsets, selected state, fit controls and saved-kit summary should share the construction language.

Make selection, material comparison and kit editing real. Explain garment dimensions as illustrative product measurements, not bodily judgments. Label units and fit choices; support native radios, keyboard commands, stable focus after removal, announced feedback and legible disabled states. Do not imply checkout, stock, certification or external saving where none exists.

## CSS Translation

Proposed roles: near-black #101314, shell #1C2224, off-white #F1F2ED, secondary #B7C2C3, seam #697778 and acid-free pale gray #DCE4E3. These are authored application tokens, not historic sampled colors. Use solid high-contrast text planes; reserve semitransparency for inert material diagrams.

Use 1–2px construction borders, restrained 8px or clipped corners, 8/16/24/40px spacing, thin directional lines and a sparse dot/mesh pattern. Let a white inspection panel break the black field. Avoid multiple glow effects. Minimum 44px targets, 16px body, flexible grids and wrapping metadata preserve usability. A still interface needs no decorative loader.

## Typography / Fonts

Use a short condensed grotesque masthead with forceful scale and tight leading; readable system sans for descriptions; monospace for product codes and dimensions. Arial Narrow/Impact fallbacks are contemporary interpretation, not identified historic fonts. Uppercase belongs to short labels; ordinary case belongs to prose. No added font download is required.

## Cultural / Ethical Notes

Do not equate disability, prosthetics or medical devices with menace. Treat transhumanist imagery as situated speculation, not a promise or a universal ideal. Avoid compulsory fitness, thinness, gender coding, dehumanizing labels or appropriated Japanese characters. Use original marks rather than borrowing sports brands' identities; their appearance in source collages is not reuse permission. Public image access is not a license.

## Anti-Patterns

- Standard dark cards with one fashion image providing the entire aesthetic.
- Black gym clothes, occult symbols or a heart-rate chart used as sufficient evidence.
- A single brand's entire output classified as Health Goth.
- Invented material certifications, health advice or body-enhancement claims.
- Low-contrast gray-on-black captions, unreadable mesh behind prose, tiny controls.
- Forced animated scans, fake loading, fake stock or fake checkout.

# Seapunk

## Scope

A networked early-2010s aquatic fantasy vocabulary that connects ocean imagery with deliberately synthetic graphics, internet collage, rave references and self-presentation. Glossy spheres, translucent forms, impossible watery environments and visibly pasted fragments coexist with pale, flat or humorous variants. The community and its contested circulation matter; it is not a name for every blue picture or every work by an associated musician.

Preserve the relationship between aquatic content, artificial surface/depth and playful recombinant composition. A current interface should carry those relationships into its navigation, type, containers and selected states. Historical websites, exact software, font families and universal interaction timing are not established by this corpus.

## 7-Dimension Profile

**Palette**: Aquamarine, cyan, blue, violet, pink, chrome highlights and luminous horizon bands are common. Gold accents, very pale icy fields and orange seas are documented variants. Use solid dark ink or dark control planes for functional text; bright glints cannot guarantee readable contrast.

**Type**: Short angular, extruded, outlined or widely spaced condensed display contrasts with serif captions and plain informational text. No universal house font is supported. Use readable HTML for labels and prose; reserve reflective or warped lettering for a short title with a solid fallback.

**Texture**: Glossy fake metal, transparent glass, tiled water, coarse 3D edges, photo cutouts and visible rectangle seams. Smooth renders and rough internet collage both belong. Grain or VHS tearing is optional; neither a clean corporate glass panel nor a natural water photograph alone establishes the style.

**Shape**: Floating orbs, facets, triangular frames, wave strips, mirrored fish/dolphins, palms and synthetic architectural enclosures. Shape hierarchy should form a navigable environment, not an indiscriminate sticker pile. The dolphin is common, not compulsory.

**Motion**: Inspected videos show virtual camera travel, rolling/reflected water, composited performers, environment cuts and occasional tearing. No standard duration/easing was measured. In a useful web app, immediate editing and a brief user-triggered hover lift can translate this lightness. Reduced motion removes movement while preserving every state; avoid flashing or forced continuous loops.

**Spatial**: Layered planes, centered reflective objects, mirrored axes, luminous vanishing horizons and inset framing give impossible depth. Other examples use flat badge or garment surfaces. Keep stable opaque text islands, clear focus and a linear mobile reading order rather than shrinking a fixed desktop collage.

**Cultural markers**: Twitter/Tumblr circulation, bedroom-scale creative networks, aquatic fantasy, early-web/1990s rave reference, music/fashion crossover and arguments about appropriation. Participants differed about the name and its commercialization. Preserve artist credits and uncertainty; do not assign every adjacent creator compulsory membership.

## Non-Negotiables

**Non-negotiables**: aquatic fantasy linked with synthetic/recombinant visual construction; deliberate artificial depth or collage layering; a playful network-culture register; aesthetic treatment carried through the actual content hierarchy and controls. Blue hair, dolphins, pink, marble busts and animation are individually optional.

## Connotation

**Mode:** contemporary quotation of early-2010s networked aquatic fantasy.

Convey self-directed curiosity and internet play without presenting a commercial interface as an authentic historical scene artifact. Early participants' accounts conflict with imposed genre boundaries; later pop visibility is reception/adoption, not the movement's origin.

## Related / Subsets

- **Vaporwave:** overlapping early-internet graphics and synthetic space; Seapunk centers aquatic fantasy and scene self-presentation rather than assuming corporate/mall nostalgia or one critical theory.
- **Y2K:** chrome, translucent plastic and digital forms overlap; Seapunk is a later networked aquatic remix, not a direct synonym for millennium futurism.
- **Cyberpunk:** a participant's explicit association, but dark dystopian terminals and neon cityscapes are insufficient.
- **Frutiger Aero:** water/nature/technology overlap; polished corporate usability and ecological optimism differ from Seapunk's conspicuously impossible collage.
- **Rave Flyer:** music and synthetic geometry connect them; event information and fluorescent type alone do not establish the aquatic branch.
- Flat tropical badge, glossy ocean render, pastel collage and surreal aquatic object remain internal variations, not additional canonical slugs.

## Frontend / UI Guidance

Apply the system to a personal start-page editor: chrome-edged translucent-looking windows sit over a synthetic ocean field, with wave separators, porthole selectors and a short dimensional wordmark. The actual page title, navigation links, editing fields, reordering controls and preview share the visual grammar. Solid text/control interiors preserve readability.

Support real link addition, meaningful validation, deduplication, reordering, removal and a preview using saved state. State clearly whether changes are local, downloaded or published. Use native buttons/fieldsets, labelled inputs, redundant selected-state text, announced feedback and stable focus after removal. Decorative scene layers are inert. External links need safe URL handling and clear labels; no fake publishing or network status.

## CSS Translation

Proposed current-web roles: deep ink #102D50, pale aqua #DDFBFF, lilac #E7DFFF, blue #144A88, pink #C3559D and lemon #F7EFA2. These are authored tokens, not measured historic swatches. Use multiple hard/soft highlight stops on borders and short display, with dark solid text surfaces. Check real state pairs independently.

Combine radial sphere gradients, thin perspective/wave lines, original SVG geometry and selective asymmetric frame corners. Keep 16px/1.5 body and at least 44px controls. Proposed spacing 6/12/18/24/36px. Optional 160ms hover lift is a modern translation; disable transforms/transitions under reduced motion. Do not infer a historical animation rate from a still.

## Typography / Fonts

Georgia/system serif italic can provide a fluid short masthead, with system sans for controls and monospace for small link metadata. These are application choices, not period font identifications. Keep complex display short, give it a dark fallback and avoid all-caps prose. No added font download is required.

## Cultural / Ethical Notes

Creator interviews are situated testimony, not unanimous origin verdicts. Use current artist names in new narration; older sources may use former names. Retain disputes without endorsing personal accusations. Religious, classical and East Asian motifs visible in particular collages do not become anonymous decorative stock. Avoid meaningless scripts, copied commercial characters, signature artist designs or claims that dyeing hair grants scene membership. Reference access is not a reuse license.

## Anti-Patterns

- Generic components with one aquatic picture pasted above them.
- Every teal gradient, mermaid picture, internet collage or musician image called Seapunk.
- Compulsory dolphins, pink, glitch, marble busts or the same typeface everywhere.
- Corporate eco-claims, invented movement consensus or pop adoption presented as origin.
- Tiny pale labels over water, hover-only commands, fixed collage layouts on mobile.
- Flashing, unpausable loops, fake loading or fake publishing.

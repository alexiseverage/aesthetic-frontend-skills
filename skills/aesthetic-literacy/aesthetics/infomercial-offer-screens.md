# Infomercial Offer Screens

## Scope

The ordering and value graphics of direct-response television: product or demonstration field, emphatic price/value/bonus treatment and a dominant contact/action rail. Full-screen recap cards, offer-sidebars and persistent lower-thirds belong. Short direct-response end cards provide boundary comparisons with long-form infomercials; generic broadcast tickers, credits and product demonstrations without an offer treatment do not.

## 7-Dimension Profile

**Palette**: Deep blue/white/yellow, white/red, purple, turquoise and warm brown systems are observed. High-value contrast separates the contact rail and price from the program field. Blue is common, not mandatory.

**Type**: Oversized telephone numerals and price digits, raised decimal fragments, bold condensed or italic sans calls to action, and occasional large serif contact lines. Shadows and outlines improve historical broadcast separation but should remain restrained around live readable text.

**Texture**: Flat or mottled video-graphic fields, gradients, glossy badges and framed product footage. VHS softness, interlacing, compression and black borders belong to surviving recordings rather than obligatory UI texture.

**Shape**: Hard rectangular rails, side panels, price discs, star/scalloped badges, slanted labels and boxed guarantee rows. Large contact fields maintain a stable shape while the demonstration can change.

**Motion**: Three actual playback excerpts confirm held offers, visible countdown changes and cuts between product/value/presenter scenes while the contact hierarchy remains. No universal easing or duration is established. A modern quote builder can keep the panel still and update only on deliberate bundle selection; avoid fake urgency timers.

**Spatial**: Product/demonstration plus a dominant price/value tier and a separate contact/action area. Some full recaps form a column of bonuses and total value; sparse end cards reduce this to a product and large contact line.

**Cultural markers**: Direct-response retail advertising, value stacking, installment language, bonus bundles and call-now copy. The sample spans US and Australian recordings with broadcaster/retailer template concentration. Advertiser claims are described as historical copy, not accepted as medical or financial fact.

## Non-Negotiables

**Non-negotiables**: A stable action/contact tier; deliberately oversized offer numerals or value emphasis; a readable relationship between product/demo, offer details and action.

## Connotation

**Mode:** Contemporary or nostalgic quotation.

Nostalgic quotation of direct-response sales graphics, expressed through a clear modern quote/configuration form. The performance is emphatic and practical rather than dependent on a presenter or a distressed VHS filter.

## Related / Subsets

Sports Scorebug describes live game status, while an infomercial rail asks for a response to an offer. Web2 Gloss shares glossy buttons but not the demonstration/price/contact hierarchy. Konbini Utility shares commercial clarity with a different retail information architecture.

## Frontend / UI Guidance

Use a desk-kit bundle configurator with an original product diagram, visible included items, an honestly calculated one-time total and a large Save quote action. A package selector updates contents and total; a details accordion exposes materials; a confirmation panel shows exactly what was saved. Inputs, bonus labels, dividers and the action strip must carry the aesthetic. No external payment or contact submission is needed for the showcase.

## CSS Translation

Proposed navy #112b73, white #fffdf0, yellow #ffe153, red #b62223; heavy display sans, thin light rules, square feature strips and a circular value badge. Use a two-field grid above a full-width action rail, with modest offset text shadows confined to display text. On mobile stack product above terms/action. Proposed micro-interaction duration160ms if used, removed under reduced motion; it is not measured TV timing.

## Typography / Fonts

Impact or a heavy condensed sans for short promotional headings, Arial/system sans for terms and controls, and Georgia for an optional large contact-style line. Use real font sizing for price fractions; preserve DOM reading order. System fallbacks avoid font downloads and do not imply historical font attribution.

## Cultural / Ethical Notes

Use original product names and diagrams. Make costs, contents and conditions explicit. Avoid false savings, fake countdowns, unverifiable guarantees and copied health/wealth claims. Research videos may include presenters; the original showcase uses objects and functioning controls, without people imagery.

## Anti-Patterns

- A generic landing page with one infomercial photograph.
- A fake timer or permanently flashing order button.
- Making terms illegible to imitate broadcast microtype.
- Counting separate frames or bonus items from one program as separate artifacts.

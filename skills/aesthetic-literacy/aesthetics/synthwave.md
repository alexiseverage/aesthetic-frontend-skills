# Synthwave / Retrowave

Synthwave / Retrowave is a contemporary internet-native revival of 1980s action, sci-fi, arcade, and electronic-music imagery: neon grids, dark horizons, glowing suns, chrome vehicles, and cinematic night-driving energy. It is nostalgic but usually earnest and romantic rather than Vaporwave's ironic melancholy.

## Scope

Use Synthwave for music visuals, game/arcade pages, event posters, campaign heroes, creator portfolios, speculative-tech teasers, and entertainment interfaces where neon atmosphere is the point. Avoid it for dense SaaS dashboards, routine productivity apps, civic services, healthcare/finance flows, or long-form reading where dark neon contrast and glow would fatigue users.

## 7-Dimension Profile

**Palette**: dark purple, black, midnight blue, hot pink, neon magenta, electric cyan, violet, and occasional orange sun gradients. The palette depends on saturated light against darkness; pastel daylight weakens the signal.

**Type**: condensed angular display faces, chrome-extruded 1980s-style lettering, sci-fi sans, italic speed forms, and wide hero titles. Pair novelty headings with readable sans labels for actual UI.

**Texture**: neon glow, atmospheric haze, light trails, chrome highlights, VHS-like softness when used sparingly, laser grids, and glossy poster lighting. Texture should feel cinematic and luminous rather than corrupted.

**Shape**: grid floors, horizon suns, vector mountains, angular panels, rectilinear roads, triangles, cassette tapes, sports cars, DeLorean-like silhouettes, and chrome or cyborg bodies when they stay in poster territory.

**Motion**: driving rhythm, lateral parallax, horizon travel, pulsing neon, reveal glints, and chase-scene energy. Keep loops measured and offer reduced-motion states.

**Spatial**: dark infinite space with a strong horizon line, receding perspective grids, foreground silhouettes, and atmospheric depth. The composition often points toward a vanishing point.

**Cultural markers**: Outrun grid roads, neon sun/grid sunsets, Sega arcade memory, cassette and VHS references, 1980s sci-fi movie-poster language, night drives, chrome heroes, and internet retrowave music culture.

## Non-Negotiables

**Non-negotiables**: dark background; neon cyan/magenta/purple palette; horizon/grid or equivalent night-drive depth motif; and earnest 1980s sci-fi/action romance. A random neon gradient without horizon, grid, or cinematic retro-futurism is not enough.

## Connotation

Synthwave connotes affectionate 1980s futurist nostalgia, speed, romance, and cinematic cool. It can include cyborgs, robots, and speculative tech, but the tone should remain poster-heroic and atmospheric rather than dystopian surveillance or social decay.

## Related / Subsets

- `vaporwave` shares retro-futurist colors and grids, but Vaporwave is slower, more ironic, and filled with consumer-memory fragments.
- `cyberpunk` shares neon and technology, but Cyberpunk adds dense urban infrastructure, rain, coercion, and social critique.
- `y2k` shares synthetic futurism, but Y2K is glossy millennium plastic/chrome rather than dark 1980s night-drive cinema.
- Retrowave is a common synonym; Outrun is often used casually as a synonym but can be treated more precisely as the racing/grid-road subset.

## Frontend / UI Guidance

Use Synthwave in bounded hero systems, media players, event pages, game menus, promotional cards, and decorative backgrounds. Keep forms, navigation, and copy on stable dark surfaces with sufficient contrast; avoid making neon glow the only boundary for active state or focus. Reserve the full grid-horizon treatment for moments that can tolerate spectacle.

## CSS Translation

- Color roles: `--sw-bg`, `--sw-purple`, `--sw-magenta`, `--sw-cyan`, `--sw-sun`, and `--sw-grid`.
- Effects: layered `text-shadow` neon glows, perspective grid backgrounds, radial sun gradients, horizon masks, and atmospheric blur used behind content rather than on text.
- Layout: centered horizon compositions, road/grid perspective, poster-like hero framing, and dark cards with neon accents.
- Motion: parallax grid drift, neon pulse, hover glints, and reduced-motion still states.
- Assets: stylized cars, cassette tapes, vector mountains, and sci-fi silhouettes only when licensed or generated for the project.

## Typography / Fonts

Use display type for headlines only: angular retro-future sans, chrome-inspired scripts, condensed sci-fi faces, or wide-tracked grotesks. Pair with a legible modern sans for body copy and controls; avoid setting essential text in glowing outlined type.

## Cultural / Ethical Notes

Treat Synthwave as nostalgic quotation of media memory, not a factual recreation of the 1980s. Avoid collapsing it into Cyberpunk's social critique or using endless neon motion where users need calm, readable interfaces. Test contrast because glow can make bright text appear legible while failing actual reading needs.

## Anti-Patterns

- Neon gradients without dark horizon/grid or cinematic retro-futurist structure.
- Confusing Synthwave with Cyberpunk grime or Vaporwave irony.
- Applying glowing type to long text, form fields, or legal content.
- Overusing pulsing/flickering effects without reduced-motion alternatives.
- Using nostalgia cues so heavily that product state and navigation disappear.

# 88×31 Badge & Webring Culture

## Scope

88×31 Badge & Webring Culture is the old-web micro-system of tiny rectangular affiliation buttons, "best viewed" badges, link exchange graphics, and previous/next webring navigation. Use it when the fixed badge format, footer strip, or ring-navigation grammar is the subject. It is narrower than `early-internet`, which includes many homepage conventions beyond badges.

Evidence is limited and source-based but the format itself is strongly constrained. Reference old badge archives as provenance; do not copy badge artwork unless reuse rights are clear.

## 7-Dimension Profile

**Palette**: Limited bitmap palettes, often two to four high-contrast colors per badge, plus small saturated highlights, browser-era blues, and dithered bevel gradients. Full photographic color is uncommon and usually compressed.

**Type**: Tiny pixel lettering, Silkscreen-style bitmap fonts, condensed labels, anti-aliased-at-most display words, and micro slogans. Body text does not belong inside the badge; text must be short.

**Texture**: Pixelated edges, 1px borders, bevels, dithered gradients, GIF compression, hard aliasing, and occasional blinking or animated GIF frames.

**Shape**: The fixed 88×31 rectangle is the key shape. Variants such as 80×15 exist, but arbitrary responsive cards are outside the system.

**Motion**: Mostly static; some badges animate with simple GIF frame changes, blinking, or tiny scrolling effects. Animation should be optional and reduced-motion safe.

**Spatial**: Footer badge strips, clustered button collections, webring rows, previous/next/random links, and compact affiliation shelves. The badge is read in multiples.

**Cultural markers**: GeoCities and old-web homepages, IndieWeb and Neocities revivals, "Best viewed in..." badges, browser-wars buttons, link exchange, webring membership, and archive culture.

## Non-Negotiables

**Non-negotiables**: fixed 88×31 canvas; tiny bitmap type; badge/button border grammar; footer or webring placement; affiliation/link-exchange meaning. Without the fixed micro-format, it becomes generic pixel art.

## Connotation

This reads as nostalgic quotation or old-web revival. It signals hand-curated affiliation, decentralized navigation, and small-site community rather than modern social sharing.

## Related / Subsets

`early-internet` includes these badges but also page backgrounds, tables, guestbooks, and homepage content. `8-bit-pixel` shares bitmap constraints but not the web-affiliation format. `browser-chrome-netscape` is the application frame around pages.

## Frontend / UI Guidance

Use badges for footer affiliations, easter-egg link shelves, webring navigation, retro catalogs, or small calls-to-action. Keep modern accessible link text and hit targets around the tiny visual badge.

## CSS Translation

Use exact 88px by 31px artboards or scaled replicas, 1px borders, pixel fonts, nearest-neighbor image rendering, dithered gradients, and grid strips. If visually scaling up, preserve the original ratio and pixel steps.

## Typography / Fonts

Use bitmap/pixel fonts, tiny condensed labels, and short words. Provide adjacent accessible text because 88×31 lettering can be too small for many users.

## Cultural / Ethical Notes

Badge archives may contain copyrighted, personal, adult, political, or abandoned-site material. Link as research; do not redistribute or scrape badges into public products without rights review.

## Anti-Patterns

- Making a modern responsive button and calling it 88×31.
- Enlarging pixel art with smoothing.
- Copying archived badges without permission.
- Treating webring navigation as decorative if links do not work.

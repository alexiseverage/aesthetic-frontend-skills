# Vectordelia

## Scope

A retrospective label for exuberant commercial vector-like composition built from circles, rays, arrows, ribbons, stars, halftone fields and urban/cultural cutouts. Frutiger Metro is a common alternate search name; it does not imply a Frutiger typeface or a direct relationship to Microsoft’s Metro interface. Connected botanical growth remains separately described by Vectorbloom.

## 7-Dimension Profile

**Palette**: Saturated multicolor, black/white with bright accents, and peach/sage/cream variants. Repeated spot colors connect layered motifs. Color variety is optional; crisp shape relationships and hierarchy are essential.

**Type**: Bold geometric sans, outlined display, condensed titles and integrated short labels against plain information zones. No single historical font is established, and the alternate name does not mandate Frutiger.

**Texture**: Clean layered silhouettes, gradients, halftone dots, transparent overlaps and occasional posterized photos. Flat and pseudo-dimensional effects coexist. Do not infer a particular software tool from the look.

**Shape**: Concentric circles, discs/speaker-like rings, radial rays, swooshes, arrows, ribbons, clouds, stars and geometric cutouts. Botanical motifs may join the mix, but need not organize every composition.

**Spatial**: Dense directional flows, focal circular masses and ribbons that connect or frame quiet information islands. Scale shifts produce rhythm. Preserve a stable controls column beside a rich editable canvas.

**Cultural markers**: 2000s advertising, music packaging, digital illustration and consumer graphics. Retrospective community naming is unsettled; neither every vector stock pack nor every work by one illustrator belongs.

**Motion**: Historical timing/easing is unknown in this still-led corpus. A current application may be intentionally still, with immediate state changes or brief optional feedback. Any squash, reveal or parameter transition is a design proposal, not an observed historical standard. Reduced motion preserves the complete composition and every task.

## Non-Negotiables

**Non-negotiables**: Crisp layered vector-like motifs; rhythmic circles/rays/ribbons or related geometric system; deliberate directional density; a clear information hierarchy.

## Related / Subsets

Vectorbloom uses connected botanical/hybrid growth as its organizing principle. Vectordelia covers broader non-botanical geometric ornament. Frutiger Aero centers glossy nature/technology imagery and interface polish. Vectorheart is more industrial/diagrammatic and usually less florid.

Related catalog owners: `vectorbloom`, `frutiger-aero`, `psychedelic`, `vectorheart`. Internal variants remain descriptive unless they establish a separately useful, researched system.

## Frontend / UI Guidance

Build a poster-layout tool with motif layers, ribbon/orbit choices and undo. Selected layers change the visible composition; use plain controls, circular active markers and striped caption rails. Export must reflect the actual selected state.

Use real buttons, labeled inputs and visible selected/disabled/error states. Support keyboard operation, 44px touch targets and announced feedback. Reflow modules on narrow screens; decorative geometry must not intercept pointer events or obscure focus. Use original abstract/object artwork; people imagery and copied source screenshots should not carry the interface identity.

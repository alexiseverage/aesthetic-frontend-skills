# Whole Earth Catalog Editorial

## Scope

A practical editorial vernacular anchored in the late-1960s/1970s Whole Earth Catalog and supplements. Its identity is a system of resource evaluation, self-education and reader exchange: dense flexible columns, sturdy display type, signed personal review, compact access metadata, useful diagrams and photographs, and an index that makes the abundance navigable.

Use for tool libraries, repair/resource directories, learning catalogs, community notebooks and reading collections. Apply the information structure across the interface. An Earth picture, beige background or generated tool illustration beside ordinary modern cards does not establish this aesthetic. CoEvolution's later essays/diaries are a related reading branch; they do not replace the core catalog lookup pattern.

## 7-Dimension Profile

**Palette**: Dark ink on pale paper carries most interior information. Black covers with isolated Earth photography are common early, while vivid comic, painted, illustrated and colored later covers are documented. Scan yellowing, foxing and faded ink are not compulsory original colors. Proposed UI values appear below.

**Type**: Sturdy heavy serif masthead/category headings, compact serif text, italic evaluative voice, small bold item titles and large emphatic page numbers establish hierarchy. Sans instructional headings, sans reading, typewritten notes and hand lettering are real variants. Physical Letraset use is documented; no single universal font is identified.

**Texture**: Cutout book jackets, documentary images, precise line diagrams and occasional hatching, dotted/chain/net rules. Keep paper and text readable; concentrate texture in original diagrams or local separators. Do not copy creases, dirt and scan damage over essential reading.

**Shape**: Rectangular columns and inset notes, thin local rules, variable-sized modules, small diagrams and useful cutouts. Oval crops, ornamental frames and broad section breaks are optional branches. Avoid default rounded-card repetition that destroys the page's varied editorial rhythm.

**Motion**: Source evidence is static print. Use stable reading and immediate deliberate filtering, disclosure, comparison and notebook updates. Printed checkboxes or image sequences do not establish animation timing. Disable nonessential transitions and smooth scroll under reduced motion; avoid autoplay page flipping.

**Spatial**: Dense but purposeful two/three/four-column lookup, with review/identity/price/supplier information kept close. Text lengths and image roles shape the modules. Spare covers, long essays and diaries are distinct modes. Mobile should stack semantic content in order while keeping an efficient category index and usable comparison.

**Cultural markers**: Tools broadly include books, knowledge and community resources; independent learning, practical experimentation, reader corrections and attributed experience are central. The publishing network includes political/social voices as well as back-to-the-land/technology interests. Do not reduce it to a neutral rural nostalgia moodboard.

## Non-Negotiables

**Non-negotiables**: useful evaluation plus access information; clear lookup/category hierarchy amid dense varied content; an attributed reader/practitioner voice; functional diagrams or documentary images integrated with what they explain. The aesthetic must shape information and controls, not be carried by one illustration.

## Connotation

**Mode:** contemporary revival of a practical community/editorial catalog.

It can convey curiosity, shared competence and a resourceful personal recommendation. Preserve an honest distinction between opinion and specification. The historical catalog's universalizing claims, dated instructions and cultural borrowings should not become modern product truth. A contemporary service can share knowledge and invite correction while remaining clear about what it actually provides.

## Related / Subsets

- **Financial Newspaper Editorial** shares columns, serif hierarchy and rules but centers news/market reporting; this identity centers practical resource evaluation, access and reader exchange.
- **Punk Zine** shares independent production and participation; aggressive photocopy distress and typographic disruption are a different emphasis.
- **Solarpunk** shares ecological/community subjects but has a different period/visual system; greenery and solar futurism are insufficient here.
- **Desktop Publishing** is a production-history neighbor. The documented catalog process used physical cutting, pasting, ruling and transfer lettering, not a recovered modern software workflow.
- Journal reading, correspondence supplements and later color catalogs are branches, not universal aliases for other counterculture publications.

## Frontend / UI Guidance

Build a community tool/resource catalog with a strong serif masthead, edition/page imprint, brief Function note, category index and dense review columns. Put short italic signed opinions beside practical availability/loan/format data and original diagrams. Let the visual hierarchy distinguish description, opinion, specification and action. Avoid placing all identity in a hero image above generic cards.

Use real category filters, labeled comparison checkboxes, readable expandable how-to articles and a local-session contribution form. Show comparison limits, validation and actual submitted notes honestly. Keep visible focus, full accessible labels, at least 44px action targets and text-based state. Do not copy historical tiny imprint sizes into essential controls. A digital filter is a proposed translation, not an original print behavior.

## CSS Translation

Proposed roles: paper `#F3EAD5`, ink `#24241E`, muted ink `#59594C`, inset `#E8DFC9`, olive `#596142`. Use ink on paper/inset and calculate actual action/label/error contrast. Keep body 16px/1.5 and metadata 12–14px; density should come from information grouping rather than illegible type. Suggested spacing 8/12/20/32px, 0px radii, 1px rules and bounded original line-work assets.

Use CSS grid for unequal review columns while preserving DOM reading order; stack at a content-driven narrow breakpoint. An in-flow detail section should expand the article rather than overlay a fake sheet. Comparison can reflow to labeled records or use an explicitly scrollable named table with a clear cue. Optional local patterned rules may separate content without obscuring text. Preserve static paper/ink treatment in dark host themes and remove nonessential motion for `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`.

## Typography / Fonts

Georgia or a sturdy serif fallback can support display and readable body, with Arial for functional labels and system mono for inventory references when needed. These are contemporary fallback choices, not exact original faces. Pair a short heavy masthead with mixed-case reading text and brief italic review passages; do not set whole forms in decorative script, heavy display or tightly tracked capitals.

## Cultural / Ethical Notes

Research access does not license scans, book jackets, photographs, logos or illustrations for reuse. Create original diagrams, text and service identity. Period medical, food, construction, financial and legal advice is historical content, not validated contemporary guidance. Do not transplant it into a functional product. Preserve author/contributor attribution and avoid extracting real personal addresses from letters.

The network is not one demographic or ideology: the Fall 1974 CoEvolution cover explicitly credits The Black Panther Party as guest editors. Other historical material also contains culturally generalized/stigmatizing language and borrowed Indigenous/non-Western practices. Treat these as history requiring context, not a decorative motif inventory. A fictional service must state its real local-session/inventory limits.

## Anti-Patterns

- A beige ordinary shop with one generated Earth/tool image.
- Equal rounded cards that separate opinions from practical access data.
- Fake distressed texture across reading or tiny print-sized controls.
- Copying the catalog name/layout, a recognizable source illustration or period quotations.
- Treating all pages as black/white serif, or imposing comic lettering from one cover on every state.
- Dead supplier links, pretend inventory, fake contribution submission or color-only selection.
- Uncritical reuse of dated instructions or flattening the network's social/political range.

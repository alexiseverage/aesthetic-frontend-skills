# Lotto Scratcher Commerce

Lotto Scratcher Commerce is a ticket-first vernacular commerce aesthetic built from printed scratch-off objects, high-saturation prize hierarchy, concealed reveal fields, retail-counter optimism, validation artifacts, and specimen/no-cash-value safety framing. It is not casino glam; it is paper-first chance ephemera.

## Scope

Use it for demo scratch-card interfaces, drag-to-reveal moments, instant-win-inspired onboarding, prize-grid layouts, fictional promotion artifacts, and state-lottery ephemera studies. Avoid it for real-money gambling UX, casino interiors, sportsbook odds boards, table games, or luxury nightlife branding.

## 7-Dimension Profile

**Palette**: High-saturation retail brights: red, orange, yellow, cyan, electric blue, lime, magenta, purple, prize-led contrast, and metallic gold/silver cues for premium tickets. Muted archival paper-lottery tones are secondary.

**Type**: Oversized block or script display type, thick black outlines, bevel/inline highlights, drop shadows, prize-first headline hierarchy, compact utility microcopy, validation labels, and instruction text.

**Texture**: Printed-paper substrate, paper grain, foil shine, halftone/security print, matte grey/silver latex scratch fields, chipped scratch edges, residue, debris, currency swirls, radial bursts, faux chrome/gem shine, and dense legal labeling.

**Shape**: Rectangular ticket frames, dense play grids, reveal circles/medallions, dotted separators, price badges, lottery/specimen badges, prize labels, barcode strips, void markers, validation boxes, and laddered bonus compartments.

**Motion**: Drag, scrape, uncover, match, scan, and reveal. Digital versions should expose only the brush path and require substantial scratched coverage; one click must not complete the ticket.

**Spatial**: Mini-poster at top plus mini-form below, dense compartment stacks, low whitespace, denomination/title/top-prize/instructions/winning-numbers/your-numbers/prize/bonus/legal-footer sequencing.

**Cultural markers**: Scratch zones, winning numbers/your numbers, multiplier badges, top-prize bursts, dollar signs/cash/gold bars/coins, serials/barcodes, validation text, void/specimen markers, state-lottery framing, convenience-store optimism, fictional disclaimers.

## Non-Negotiables

**Non-negotiables**:

- Ticket object model with printed-paper and validation artifacts.
- Concealed/reveal scratch mechanics or clear scratch-zone quotation.
- Prize-first dense hierarchy and retail-counter context.
- Fictional, non-redeemable, no-cash-value framing for demos.

## Connotation

**Mode:** nostalgic quotation or authentic retail ephemera mode.

It quotes the optimism and tactile density of scratch-off tickets while avoiding real-money gambling UX. Preserve clearly fictional/specimen copy.

## Related / Subsets

- `casino` is a sister gambling/chance-commerce aesthetic, but lotto is ticket-first, paper-first, reveal-first, and retail-counter oriented rather than table/room oriented.
- `convenience-store-backoffice` shares lottery/counter context, but lotto is consumer-facing printed chance commerce.
- `b2b-quick-order-grid` only overlaps in grid density; lotto grids are play/reveal compartments, not procurement rows.

Crossword scratchers, bingo scratchers, and premium foil tickets are internal modes, not separate canonical subsets yet.

## Frontend / UI Guidance

Build a ticket frame with discrete scratch compartments, brush-path reveal, progress threshold, keyboard/button fallback, validation/specimen markings, and clear no-cash-value text. Keep essential instructions on solid high-contrast plaques.

## CSS Translation

- Color roles: `--bg-ticket`, `--accent-prize`, `--accent-burst`, `--foil-gold`, `--latex-scratch`, `--ink-legal`, and `--line-validation`.
- Borders/dividers: perforations, dotted separators, compartment frames, barcode blocks, and validation boxes.
- Radius language: mostly square ticket geometry with occasional medallions/badges.
- Effects: halftone, foil gradients, paper grain, scratch residue, and reveal masks away from essential copy.
- Layout: top prize poster, central play grid, bonus compartments, and legal footer.
- Motion: pointer/touch scratch reveal plus accessible non-pointer controls.

## Typography / Fonts

Use display type for ticket title and prize claims; use condensed/tabular utility type for instructions, numbers, serials, barcodes, and legal/specimen copy.

## Cultural / Ethical Notes

Do not create deceptive gambling flows. Mark demos and promotions as fictional or non-redeemable when appropriate, avoid real-money conversion pressure, and provide accessible alternatives to drag-only mechanics.

## Anti-Patterns

- Casino chips, felt, roulette, dealers, or sportsbook odds boards.
- One-click reveal that ignores tactile scratch behavior.
- Real-money urgency or manipulative gambling copy.
- Metallic effects behind critical instructions.
- Coupon/flyer design without conceal/reveal ticket logic.

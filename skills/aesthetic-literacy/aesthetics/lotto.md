**Palette**: High-saturation retail brights: electric blue, cyan, lime, magenta, orange, yellow, and premium metallic gold/silver (`#00AEEF`, `#00D084`, `#FF2DAA`, `#FF7A00`, `#FFD400`, `#C0C0C0`, `#111827`). Prize fields use aggressive contrast and bright burst seals rather than casino-lounge darkness.

**Type**: Oversized block display sans, thick outlines, bevel/shadow effects, prize-first headline hierarchy, compact instructional microcopy, tabular serials, and legal/footer text. Headlines sell the top prize; small copy explains “winning numbers,” “your numbers,” bonus play, odds, and validation.

**Texture**: Printed-paper substrate, latex or foil-like scratch fields, silver/gold overprint simulation, currency swirls, radial bursts, gem/chrome shine, serial/barcode noise, and security/confusion-pattern cues. Texture should feel like a disposable state-issued artifact, not a screen-native casino game.

**Shape**: Tall rectangular ticket frames, repeated reveal circles or medallions, strict prize grids, badge bursts, denomination stamps, barcode strips, crossword/puzzle modules, and laddered bonus compartments. Corners can be sharp or lightly rounded (`0–6px`) but the object should remain ticket-like.

**Motion**: Reveal-first implied motion: scratch, uncover, match, scan, bonus, multiply. Digital motion should support the tactile before/after reveal with quick scrape/fade transitions and accessible button fallbacks, not replace the scratch-ticket object model.

**Spatial**: Mini-poster at the top plus mini-form below: denomination badge, oversized title, top-prize callout, winning-number row, your-number grid, bonus cells, and legal/footer strip. Density is high, compartmentalized, and retail-readable with low whitespace.

**Cultural markers**: Scratch zones, “winning numbers,” “your numbers,” multipliers, top-prize bursts, dollar signs, cash stacks, coins, vaults, barcodes, serials, validation text, state-lottery product framing, and convenience-counter optimism.

**Non-negotiables**: reveal-ticket object model + scratch/security-print texture + prize-first retail hierarchy + convenience-retail/state-lottery context. If these are removed, the result becomes a generic promo flyer or drifts into casino.

## Connotation

**Nostalgic quotation or authentic retail ephemera mode**. It quotes the optimism and tactile density of scratch-off tickets while avoiding real-money gambling UX. Do not let it drift into casino glam, table-game objects, or generic coupon/flyer design.

## Scope

Suitable for demo scratch-card interfaces, instant-win-inspired reveal moments, prize-grid layouts, ticket-like promotion artifacts, and state-lottery ephemera studies. Not suitable for casino interiors, table games, sportsbook odds boards, or luxury nightlife branding.

**Related / Subsets**: Sister to `casino` inside gambling / games-of-chance commerce, but distinct because lotto is ticket-first, paper-first, reveal-first, and retail-counter oriented rather than room/table oriented. Crossword scratchers, bingo scratchers, and premium foil tickets are internal modes, not separate slugs in this pass.

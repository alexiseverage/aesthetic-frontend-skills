# Gummy / Jelly Design

## Scope

A descriptive material-centered hybrid: soft-looking colored volumes, translucent or milky internal depth, thick softened edges and concentrated gloss evoke jelly or candy. The source objects include rigid glass and plastics as well as resin; appearance does not establish physical softness. This is not a verified single historical movement or a name shared by all cited makers.

## 7-Dimension Profile

**Palette**: Tinted orange, pink, lime, cyan and lavender, with clear or creamy interiors. Overlap and thickness change apparent color. Pale quiet variants exist alongside saturated candy hues; retain solid text surfaces.

**Type**: Material can enter lettering itself as swollen strokes, thick glossy edges and closed counters. Plain condensed or geometric type also appears beside jelly objects. A rounded display face is an application option, not a universal historical font.

**Texture**: Colored transmission, internal gradients, thick rim highlights, overlap darkening, tiny trapped bubbles and occasional poured irregularity. Milky glass is an important variant. Blur behind a flat panel alone does not produce this volumetric effect.

**Shape**: Bulbous pills, coils, irregular rounded lozenges, softened molded loops and inflated glyphs. Differentiate face, rounded side and bright rim; a flat pastel blob without internal depth is insufficient.

**Spatial**: Isolated volumes on calm grounds or a few overlapping material layers. Graphic projects contrast material display with flat information panels. Keep actions on clear stable interiors and avoid placing paragraph text across bright highlights.

**Cultural markers**: Candy and jelly analogies are explicitly documented by multiple makers and graphic designers. East Asian food references belong to specific projects, including Pangmei Shitang; do not recast them as anonymous Western bubblegum nostalgia.

**Motion**: Historical timing/easing is unknown in this still-led corpus. A current application may be intentionally still, with immediate state changes or brief optional feedback. Any squash, reveal or parameter transition is a design proposal, not an observed historical standard. Reduced motion preserves the complete composition and every task.

**Non-negotiables**: Soft-looking rounded volume; colored translucent or milky depth; visible thickness/gloss cues; readable solid task hierarchy alongside material effects.

## Related / Subsets

Glassmorphism primarily treats translucent planes and background blur; this hybrid emphasizes rounded bodies. Claymorphism is usually opaque modeled volume. Clear conventional glass, matte rough resin and food subject matter alone were excluded. Historical jelly objects are material references, not proof of today’s label.

Related catalog owners: `glassmorphism`, `claymorphism`, `y2k`. Internal variants remain descriptive unless they establish a separately useful, researched system.

## Frontend / UI Guidance

Build a flavor sampler with thick jelly quantity controls, selectable flavor segments and a real basket summary. A pressed state can compress briefly, but text remains stable. Tint overlap belongs in borders and decorative lobes; error/help text uses opaque surfaces.

Use real buttons, labeled inputs and visible selected/disabled/error states. Support keyboard operation, 44px touch targets and announced feedback. Reflow modules on narrow screens; decorative geometry must not intercept pointer events or obscure focus. Use original abstract/object artwork; people imagery and copied source screenshots should not carry the interface identity.

## Cultural / Ethical Notes

Candy and jelly analogies are explicitly documented by multiple makers and graphic designers. East Asian food references belong to specific projects, including Pangmei Shitang; do not recast them as anonymous Western bubblegum nostalgia. Credit creators and preserve uncertainty; source access is not a reuse license. Do not trace signature compositions, copy characters/marks, or turn sacred/personal symbols into generic ornament. Keep demonstration prices, schedules and form outcomes honest. Corpus is weighted toward physical objects from Helle Mardahl and Fish Design; two creator-led graphic projects and one live website support translation, not a universal UI canon. Behance/Dribbble access failures, small product previews, generic transparent objects and unclear AI examples were excluded. Ploy website assets’ production method is unknown; it is an observed published application. No exact deformation timing was measured.

## Anti-Patterns

Calling every transparent pane jelly; mandatory bounce on all content; low-contrast white labels on highlights; treating material appearance as edible or flexible; using candy brand logos as the aesthetic.

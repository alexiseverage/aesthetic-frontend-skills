# Cyberpunk

Cyberpunk is a speculative high-tech/low-life aesthetic of dense megacity infrastructure, cybernetics, corporate power, and worn technological surfaces. It should feel socially coercive, compressed, and materially dirty rather than merely neon.

## Scope

Use Cyberpunk for speculative interfaces, games, media sites, robotics/cybernetics storytelling, security-fiction dashboards, and campaign surfaces where social tension is intentional. Avoid using it for safety-critical workflows unless glow, grain, and density are heavily constrained behind clear solid content surfaces.

## 7-Dimension Profile

**Palette**: black/asphalt/bruise-purple with toxic cyan, sodium orange, surgical green.

**Type**: narrow techno sans, segmented displays, multilingual overlays.

**Texture**: rain, fog, grease, scratched plastic, chrome, HUD ghosts.

**Shape**: megacity slabs, prosthetics, cables, exoskeletons, visor slits.

**Motion**: flicker, glitch, surveillance sweeps, parallax signage.

**Spatial**: compressed layered infrastructure; claustrophobic despite scale.

**Cultural markers**: augmentation, corporate sovereignty, black-market repair, Ghost in the Shell / Blade Runner lineage.

## Frontend / UI Guidance

Start with compressed spatial layering, panel stacks, signage strata, and machine/body silhouettes, then add neon and glitch as controlled accents. Critical content should sit on opaque plates; reserve atmospheric noise and HUD ghosts for edges and backgrounds.

## CSS Translation

- Color roles: `--bg-asphalt`, `--surface-terminal`, `--accent-cyan`, `--accent-sodium`, `--accent-surgical`, `--warning-corp`.
- Borders/dividers: HUD hairlines, scan brackets, signage bands, cable-like separators, and dense panel seams.
- Radius language: hard corners, clipped panels, visor slots, and industrial modules.
- Effects: rain grain, neon bloom, scanlines, chromatic glitch, scratched plastic, and fog layers.
- Layout: stacked megacity panels, multilingual overlays, compressed grids, and readable foreground plates.
- Motion: flicker, surveillance sweeps, parallax signage, data jitter, and reduced-motion static alert states.

## Typography / Fonts

Use narrow techno sans, segmented numerals, multilingual overlays, and machine labels for atmosphere. Body copy and controls need high-contrast support faces on stable surfaces so the dense worldbuilding does not undermine readability.

## Cultural / Ethical Notes

Cyberpunk carries critique of corporate power, surveillance, extraction, and bodily modification. Do not strip those tensions into a cool neon skin for exploitative or opaque technology.

## Anti-Patterns

- Neon-on-black decoration without cybernetics, infrastructure, or social tension.
- Clean futuristic SaaS with no grime, density, or coercive corporate layer.
- Unreadable glow, scanlines, or glitch over critical text.
- Retro synthwave nostalgia mistaken for cyberpunk’s high-tech/low-life critique.

# Streamline Moderne

Streamline Moderne translates aerodynamic imagery into continuous shells, rounded corners and repeated horizontal bands across transport, buildings, furniture and stationary appliances. The evidence includes colored moulded radios, brick, lacquered wood, upholstery and chrome. It is a historically situated branch of modern design, not merely a shiny retro finish.

## Scope

Use transport/hospitality history, departure tools, product interpretation and deliberately period-framed interfaces. Distinguish original 1930s work, postwar continuity and later diner revivals. Not every Art Deco tower, neon sign or rounded modern appliance is Streamline.

## 7-Dimension Profile

**Palette**: Cream, silver, black and navy coexist with olive/amber resin, bright blue/white appliances, red grilles, terracotta brick and warm wood. Strategic two-tone fields and bands are more useful than a chrome/cream-only rule.

**Type**: Spaced geometric uppercase, condensed transport names, slender numerals and occasional script signage appear. Make the transport hierarchy clear; no single Futura-like typeface is established for every object.

**Texture**: Polished metal, smooth enamel or moulded resin, lacquered/laminated wood, upholstery, brick and glass block. Continuous form can coexist with visible construction. Aging and scratches are not mandatory new-design textures.

**Shape**: Rounded corners, tapered shells, long ribs, portholes, swept fenders and unified functional housings. Straight planes and sharper functional parts can coexist; not every element needs capsule rounding.

**Motion**: Still objects and print imply movement through elongation and perspective. No actual glide timing was measured. A contemporary interface may remain still with direct selection changes; any proposed sweep must be user-triggered and reducible.

**Spatial**: Long lateral rhythm, wrapped window bands, low bodies and grouped controls. Monumental transport posters and compact sculptural appliances are also valid. Reflow horizontal layouts into useful narrow columns rather than require side scrolling.

**Cultural markers**: 1930s industrial design, Depression-era consumer modernization, rail/ocean travel and the application of speed imagery to static goods. Loewy, Dreyfuss, Teague, Frankl and Weber illustrate different media. International architecture, later continuity and revival should retain their dates.

## Non-Negotiables

**Non-negotiables**: Aerodynamic or smoothly unified contour; directional/lateral rhythm; coherent material/construction grammar; explicit transport-era or industrial-modern context.

## Related / Subsets

- `art-deco` — parent/adjacent style; Streamline strips vertical Deco ornament into horizontal aerodynamic forms.
- `bauhaus` — shares industrial modernity but is orthogonal and intellectual where Streamline is curved and populist.
- `atomic-age` — inherits streamline transport optimism and amplifies it into space-race spectacle.
- Subsets include Style Paquebot, Miami Beach Streamline, and streamline diner architecture.

## Frontend / UI Guidance

Use a departure console, ticket-like summary, route selector or equipment interpretation with real choices. Integrate speed rules into dividers and curved casing into hierarchy; original object drawings can carry the form without people. Mark fictional timetables clearly.

## CSS Translation

Proposed values: enamel #eee9d6, navy #234b56, sage-metal #8b9e9b, oxide #a44a36. Combine double horizontal rules, restrained metal gradients, asymmetric 20px/55px casing corners and porthole-like framed artwork. Controls remain labelled with clear focus; reduce to a single column on phones.

## Typography / Fonts

Pair geometric/system sans titling, spaced small service labels and tabular departure numerals. Condensed or sloped display treatment may signal movement, but preserve readable locations and times.

## Cultural / Ethical Notes

Keep the period’s industrial optimism, consumer marketing and labour/economic context visible. A streamlined shell did not necessarily improve aerodynamic function on a stationary object. Do not sell fictional service details as real transport information.

## Anti-Patterns

- A vertical Art Deco tower or angular textile counted solely from a search term.
- Chrome-only or cream-only restrictions that erase colored and warm-material works.
- Every component a pill, regardless of function.
- Compulsory gliding animation inferred from a static train poster.

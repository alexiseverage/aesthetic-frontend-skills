# Corporate Memphis / Illustration Flat

## Scope

An informal label for late-2010s scalable product-brand illustration, associated with Big Tech optimism, Alegria, SaaS onboarding and modular illustration libraries. These maker systems differ: do not credit one company with every related style, or treat every flat illustration as Corporate Memphis. The existing era label describes its prominent period and critical reception; it is not evidence the visual language ceased to exist afterward.

## 7-Dimension Profile

**Palette**: Flat bright/muted color masses, often cobalt/navy, coral/orange, yellow, mint/turquoise, purple and white ground. Natural and nonnatural skin colors both occur; pastel is optional. Essential text/controls require measured readable pairings.

**Type**: Clear friendly product hierarchy around illustration, often practical sans copy/buttons. Prominent chunky serif headlines appear in Slack and Humaaans; no neutral-geometric-only requirement or exact font license inferred.

**Texture**: Simplified illustrated planes, with optional local stipple, dither, brush edges, thin accents and tonal panels. Smooth Humaaans, textured Lee/Alegria and dimensional Atlassian variants coexist; no universal ban on grain, shadow or gradients.

**Shape**: Reduced modular geometry, expressive disproportion/gesture, oversized props/devices, small heads/long limbs, angular or rounded clothing and broad leaves. Faces may be expressive and detailed. Objects/flora can share the vocabulary, though human figures are historically central.

**Motion**: Complete still scenes qualify. Observed modular demonstrations swap clothes/body components, show recorded editor changes and change backgrounds while figures stay fixed. Exact easing, generic bobbing and scroll-drift rules are unsupported; modern motion is an application choice.

**Spatial**: Airy product narratives, onboarding/empty states, explanatory heroes and spot systems; oversized devices/objects beside small figures. Mobile compositions adapt rather than merely shrink. Strong story zones remain separate from essential labels/controls.

**Cultural markers**: Scalable corporate friendliness, teamwork and accessible-seeming product worlds; Alegria2017 commission/2018 outcome, Slack2017, Atlassian's evolving illustration language and stock modular libraries. Critiques of sameness/inclusion are context, not universal claims about artist intent.

## Non-Negotiables

**Non-negotiables**:

- Connect a coherent simplified illustration vocabulary to a specific product task/story.
- Use deliberate gesture, scale and color relationships rather than arbitrary blobs.
- Preserve clear information hierarchy and functioning ordinary controls.
- Retain maker-specific and corporate/product context; do not equate every flat image with the category.

## Connotation

**Mode:** contemporary revival.

Friendly, optimistic and scalable. Depending on context and familiarity it can also read as generic institutional reassurance. That reception is contingent, not a compulsory fatigue warning or proof a particular artist intended empty inclusivity.

## Related / Subsets

Flat Design is a broader interface language. Material can structure the surrounding UI. Web2.0 Gloss emphasizes shine and reflections, although light tonal depth does not automatically invalidate this illustration style. Alegria is a specific proprietary system inside the wider informal category; canonical alias continuity does not erase that distinction.

## Frontend / UI Guidance

Choose a concise useful onboarding, empty-state recovery or explanatory interaction. Let illustration, headings, scale, step geometry and controls express one coherent task world. Original object/flora compositions can adapt the grammar where human imagery is unsuitable; do not rewrite historical figure use as irrelevant. Keep essential content in HTML, with explicit state/validation/confirmation and no fake product promises.

## CSS Translation

- Proposed roles: navy text, warm white ground, cobalt primary action and coral/mint/yellow illustration accents. These are modern role choices, not universal sampled tokens.
- Use SVG/CSS for controlled geometric shape, overlap, exaggerated object scale and optional localized grain. Avoid copying branded characters, layouts or marks.
- Reserve generous negative space and clear text zones; turn a desktop side-by-side composition into a phone sequence with all controls available.
- Treat motion as optional and purposeful. Immediate state changes can support inspectable onboarding; any proposed transition has the same final state with reduced motion.
- Check text, selected state, disabled state and focus contrast separately from the decorative palette.

## Typography / Fonts

Use a friendly readable sans and, when appropriate, a bold serif display hierarchy. The pairing/category and generous spacing matter more than copying a named brand font. System stacks are suitable; added fonts require actual licensing and loading decisions.

## Cultural / Ethical Notes

Maker accounts show varied faces, natural/abstract skin, bodily proportions and inclusion goals; abstraction alone neither proves nor prevents representation. Avoid racial/religious/ability stereotypes and token inclusion claims. Corporate cheer should clarify a real task rather than conceal confusing, exploitative or inaccessible behavior. Preserve critical context without flattening practitioners into one cynical intention.

## Anti-Patterns

- Mandatory blank faces, blue skin, pastel colors, shadowlessness or neutral sans everywhere.
- Treating a human-character pose, clothing swap or mobile crop as a separate independent research work.
- Generic decorative figures/blobs that obscure the task or replace working controls.
- Compulsory bobbing loops, weak pastel text contrast or motion-dependent comprehension.
- Copying a proprietary library instead of making an original, context-specific composition.

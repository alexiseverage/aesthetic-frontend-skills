# Barbiecore

Barbiecore translates the aspirational doll-world into self-aware pink pop glamour: polished materials, miniature dream environments and playful display staging. Its 1959 brand lineage and 2022–2023 revival are related contexts, not a claim that every historical Barbie design was pink.

## Scope

Use for entertainment, celebratory commerce, beauty or toy-world editorial with room for theatrical display. Express it through the interface itself: original molded-looking objects, packaging frames, type and surfaces. People, dolls, celebrity photographs and licensed logos are unnecessary.

## 7-Dimension Profile

**Palette**: Dominant bubblegum/hot pink or fuchsia with white and darker readable ink; aqua, lilac, lemon, chrome or gold are useful accents. Blush and gingham are documented variants, not a replacement for the wider toy-glam system.

**Type**: Glamorous display lettering against crisp package-label sans. Script is an accent, not a requirement or permission to reproduce protected wordmarks. Exact reference fonts are unverified.

**Texture**: Glossy molded plastic, translucent packaging/glass, satin folds and metallic/faceted highlights. Tulle and glitter are variants. Deliberate material contrast is stronger than an indiscriminate sparkle filter.

**Shape**: Sculpted furniture, curved vanity frames, miniature room modules, toy-box windows, hearts/stars and sweeping paths. A coherent aspirational object set matters more than repeated heart icons.

**Motion**: Actual source timing is unknown in the audited still corpus. Propose brief selection reveals or object changes linked to controls; static shine works. Perpetual sparkle/bounce is optional, never an identity requirement.

**Spatial**: Display box, vanity/worktable, open Dreamhouse-like room or theatrical showroom with a clear focal object and smaller labels. Preserve control hierarchy and a usable phone layout.

**Cultural markers**: Barbie-associated pink spectacle, aspirational play, fashion glamour and self-aware mainstream revival. The mood can be enjoyed across genders and bodies. No specific figure, physique, wealth or protected branding is a non-negotiable.

## Non-Negotiables

**Non-negotiables**: Pink-led pop glamour + polished material contrast + an aspirational doll/toy-world object or display logic. Pink alone is insufficient. No human/doll image, exact logo, perpetual animation or rigid body ideal is necessary.

## Connotation

Contemporary revival is a useful default: playful agency and carefully made spectacle. Nostalgic quotation should name its period rather than blending all Barbie history into one pink stereotype.

## Related / Subsets

- `y2k`: shared candy colors/gloss, but its period digital/technology language differs from an aspirational miniature world.
- `pop-art`: shared mass-cultural spectacle; flat graphic quotation need not have Barbiecore's polished toy materials.
- `balletcore`: quieter rehearsal, wrap and rib materials; blush alone does not collapse this distinction.
- No canonical subsets are defined.

## Frontend / UI Guidance

Stage an original configurable furniture/accessory set within a clear package frame. Finish chips should alter materials; accessory controls should change the visible composition. Use glossy panels with solid readable control surfaces, short package labels and one confident title. State the local demonstration result instead of implying an order was placed.

## CSS Translation

- Proposed roles: `--bg-dreamhouse`, `--surface-gloss`, `--accent-hot-pink`, `--ink-pop`, `--trim-metal`.
- Layer broad linear/radial gradients, inset highlights and small cast shadows to distinguish plastic, paper, glass and chrome. Keep shine behind text.
- Use generous sculpted corners, arch/mirror outlines and a box window; preserve rectangular readable labels where appropriate.
- Separate large staged object space from controls. Stack the box above compact controls on phones.
- Short control-linked transitions are proposed application values; respect reduced motion and keep the same final selected state.

## Typography / Fonts

Choose an available expressive display face or distinctive sans treatment, paired with legible package labels. A system sans is a valid practical choice when spacing/scale carry the hierarchy. Do not pretend the brand script has been identified or licensed. Avoid small all-script labels.

## Cultural / Ethical Notes

Include playful authorship, material craft and accessible choice. Avoid equating femininity with shallowness, requiring a body/gender/skin tone, copying protected brand identity or replacing interface design with a doll/celebrity portrait. Research product photographs are linked evidence, not delivery assets. The 35-work audit is brand/product-heavy and still-led; type identity, source motion and broader community practice remain limited.

## Anti-Patterns

- Generic pink SaaS cards without a dream-world object/display system.
- Every surface sparkling, glossy or low-contrast at once.
- A photograph of a person/doll doing the aesthetic's work for the components.
- Protected logo/film-prop imitation or implied official affiliation.
- Treating all historic Barbie designs, all pink or all femininity as one aesthetic.

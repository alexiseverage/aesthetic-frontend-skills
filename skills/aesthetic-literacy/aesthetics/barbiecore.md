# Barbiecore

Barbiecore is a self-aware doll-pop glamour aesthetic built around high-chroma pink, glossy surfaces, and mainstream spectacle. Use it when the design can embrace hyperfeminine performance without reducing the signal to pink alone.

## Scope

Use Barbiecore for fashion, beauty, entertainment, pop-culture campaigns, toy-adjacent commerce, launches, and celebratory editorial surfaces that can carry overt spectacle. Keep purchase, sign-up, and navigation states readable; let high-chroma moments concentrate in heroes, cards, badges, CTA treatments, and campaign modules.

## 7-Dimension Profile

**Palette**: Hot pink, bubblegum, fuchsia, glossy white, black, metallic silver, lavender, and candy brights.

**Type**: Rounded glamorous display type, polished pop sans, doll-packaging hierarchy, and legible labels.

**Texture**: Gloss, satin, patent, sequins, metallics, faux fur, crystals, shiny plastics, and beauty-counter polish.

**Shape**: Dollhouse curves, hearts, stars, platforms, vanity objects, and sculpted hyperfeminine cards.

**Motion**: Peppy performative entrances, sparkle flashes, premiere-night pop, and theatrical readable state changes.

**Spatial**: Showroom, vanity table, dreamhouse, spotlight, and product-display staging.

**Cultural markers**: Barbie doll history, pink spectacle, convertibles, rollerblades, celebrity cosplay, tiaras, and runway pink.

## Non-Negotiables

**Non-negotiables**: high-chroma pink dominance + doll/pop glamour + glossy surface + self-aware mainstream spectacle. If these are removed, the result collapses into an adjacent or generic style rather than Barbiecore.

## Connotation

Barbiecore reads as a source-backed contemporary characterization whose cues should be treated as a multi-dimensional system rather than a palette.

## Frontend / UI Guidance

Let the interface perform: glossy cards, spotlight modules, doll-packaging labels, sparkle accents, vanity/showroom staging, and confident CTAs. Balance spectacle with simple information architecture so the result is glamorous rather than chaotic.

## CSS Translation

- Color roles: `--bg-dreamhouse`, `--surface-gloss`, `--accent-hot-pink`, `--accent-fuchsia`, `--accent-silver`, `--ink-pop`.
- Borders/dividers: glossy outlines, doll-box frames, vanity mirror rules, and star/heart badges.
- Radius language: confident curves, showroom cards, and sculpted product tiles; avoid muddy grunge edges.
- Effects: gloss highlights, sparkle bursts, patent shine, metallic gradients, and polished shadows.
- Layout: spotlight heroes, vanity/showroom grids, dreamhouse sections, and product-display staging.
- Motion: peppy entrances, sparkle flashes, confident CTA pops, and reduced-motion static shine.

## Typography / Fonts

Use glamorous rounded display faces, polished pop sans, and doll-packaging hierarchy. Keep small labels crisp; the typography can sparkle, but checkout and navigation copy should not become novelty lettering.

## Cultural / Ethical Notes

Keep the self-aware glamour legible without implying that hyperfemininity is shallow or universal. Respect brand-specific Barbie references when provenance matters and avoid substituting generic pink for the full aesthetic system.

## Anti-Patterns

- Pink-only UI without doll/pop glamour, gloss, or spectacle.
- Muted blush minimalism that reads as generic feminine branding rather than Barbiecore.
- Chaotic maximalism where sparkle, type, and objects compete with primary actions.
- Uncritical brand imitation where protected Barbie identity is implied without permission.

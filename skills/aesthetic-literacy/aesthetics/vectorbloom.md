# Vectorbloom

## Scope

A retrospective label for 2000s digital botanical/hybrid ornament: precise stems, petals, fronds, animal/plant hybrids and curls form connected, layered graphic fields. Crisp silhouettes may coexist with gradients, translucent surfaces, cutouts or physical print/cut translations. This is a useful formal branch, not a verified self-declared movement whose designers all used this name.

Connected natural growth must organize composition and hierarchy. One flower photograph, isolated mascot, cursive flourish or soft blob is insufficient. Neither every work by a relevant designer nor every digitally manufactured natural pattern belongs.

## 7-Dimension Profile

**Palette**: Coherent pink/green, red/olive/cyan, black/white with small accents, blue monochrome, warm metallic or near-white branches all occur. Bright rainbow is optional. Layered tones create depth while clear ground openings preserve legibility. Keep solid text/control roles independent of decorative colors.

**Type**: Expressive but readable short display may be heavy/stencilled, geometric outlined, pattern-integrated or graceful serif. No single period font is established. A current interface can pair a restrained serif display with clear sans task labels. Intricate ornamental lettering belongs in short display/marks, never long input labels or body copy.

**Texture**: Clean vector-like edges, silhouette/gradient overlays, print, weave, translucent layers and cut-material negative space. Exact source tools are not provable from pixels. Some documented designs began with hand-cut models and were digitized; do not claim all work was born in software. Vintage wear and distressed noise are unnecessary.

**Shape**: Interlacing stems, pointed/lobed leaves, radial petals, small faunal motifs and invented hybrids at multiple scales; solid silhouettes alternate fine contours and cutout holes. The identity comes from connected relationships and density changes, not a stock corner flourish. Shapes can be geometric and sparse as well as exuberant.

**Motion**: Historical timing is unknown in this still corpus; some source projects describe animations or changing light, which were not inspected as playback. Stillness is valid for an ornamental service interface. Use immediate selection/disclosure changes; avoid ambient growing vines, parallax and forced animation. Reduced motion preserves every task and completed state.

**Spatial**: Continuous fields cross surfaces, connect zones or grow around quiet central openings. Repetition can become lace, a border, an environment or a loose modular network. In a web app, branches and negative space should connect actual navigation/content choices. Reflow reading islands and controls on mobile; do not scale an intricate fixed scene down.

**Cultural markers**: Nature/technology dialogue, hybrid ecosystems, narrative detail and precise contemporary ornament. Specific authored characters and Karakusa/mythological lineages retain their own context. The retrospective label does not license treating those traditions as anonymous motif stock.

## Non-Negotiables

**Non-negotiables**: connected botanical or natural/hybrid growth; deliberate silhouette/contour layers with scale and negative-space relationships; ornament that organizes the surface and actual hierarchy; clear functional reading/control planes. Multicolor, full density, floral symmetry, pseudo-3D and animation are optional.

## Connotation

**Mode:** contemporary ornamental studio quotation.

Convey curiosity, elaboration and the coexistence of engineered precision with imagined nature. Preserve a sense of discovery without turning an inquiry or purchase task into a visual maze. Avoid claiming universal ecological virtue or a single designer as the unqualified inventor of a later-named style.

## Related / Subsets

- **Art Nouveau:** relevant organic lineage; contemporary digital precision, hybrid imagery and production differ from a period reconstruction.
- **Frutiger Aero:** nature/technology adjacency; glossy skies, grass and button chrome alone do not establish this ornament.
- **Psychedelic:** color/transformation overlap; kaleidoscopes and moving optical effects are not compulsory.
- **Organic Digital:** biomorphic forms overlap; connected detailed growth differs from generic soft blobs.
- **Supergraphics:** continuous surfaces and zoning overlap; detailed natural/hybrid silhouettes distinguish this branch.
- Dense hybrid environment, quiet cut-silhouette, translucent print and sparse geometric botanical branches remain internal variations.

## Frontend / UI Guidance

Build an applied service planner or studio inquiry surface. A continuous original vector ecosystem connects step navigation, service selection, project case descriptions and a reviewable inquiry summary. Service choices must change real scope/deliverables or an explicitly illustrative estimate. Disclosures reveal substantive process details; the form validates a meaningful brief and clearly states whether anything is sent.

Carry the growth grammar into petal/leaf enclosures, stem dividers, selected markers, headings and quiet reading islands. Use semantic buttons, fieldsets and labelled inputs, redundant selected-state text, announced feedback and stable keyboard focus. Give touch targets at least 44px. Irregular outlines must preserve recognizable affordances and focus visibility. Decorative branches are inert, hidden from assistive technology and never block inputs.

## CSS Translation

Proposed roles: cream #FFF7E7, dark plum #342337, teal #126C67, coral #B63754, lime #BCD369, lavender #DAD0E4. These are authored current-web tokens, not sampled historical colors. Keep cream/plum text zones solid, with restrained gradients in ornament only. Check actual foreground/state contrast; pale lime/lavender are surfaces, not assumed body-text colors.

Use original SVG paths with shared leaf/petal geometry across header, dividers, service markers and panel edges. Layer large translucent shapes behind smaller crisp silhouettes, varying density around clear openings. Selectively use asymmetric petal corners; avoid universal pill cards. Proposed spacing 6/12/18/24/36/54px, 16px/1.55 body, 12–14px metadata and 52–80px/1 short display. Default to immediate state updates; reduced-motion CSS removes nonessential animation/transitions.

## Typography / Fonts

Georgia/system serif can provide graceful display; system sans provides neutral practical tasks. These are application choices, not historical font identifications. A small original botanical wordmark may accompany accessible HTML text. Keep complex display short and avoid stock cursive everywhere. No single “Vectorbloom font” is supported.

## Cultural / Ethical Notes

Link historical images for analysis; do not trace creator silhouettes, copy commercial characters, marks or signature patterns. Access is not a reuse license. Preserve credits/collaborations and unresolved dates: Cristal Bar's creator page has 2007/2008 conflict; Midsummer project/chronology has 2004/2003 conflict. Keep Shiseido Karakusa and specific mythology situated. Ecological and medical language in individual briefs is contextual, not universal environmental/health evidence. Use original inclusive content and honest illustrative prices/estimates.

## Anti-Patterns

- Generic components whose only aesthetic signal is a botanical picture.
- Every cursive flourish, isolated cute creature or natural photograph called Vectorbloom.
- Treating all digitally cut/printed natural patterns as the same style.
- Compulsory pastel/rainbow, faux vintage noise, generic blobs or glossy Aero button chrome.
- Intricate patterns behind prose, tiny fixed-scene mobile controls or concealed focus.
- Copied signature characters, culturally unsituated motifs, fake ecological/medical claims.
- Ambient growing vines, forced parallax or unverified historical animation timings.

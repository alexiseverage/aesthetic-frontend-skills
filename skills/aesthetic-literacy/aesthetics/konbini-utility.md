# Konbini Utility

Konbini Utility is a Japanese convenience-retail aesthetic built from compact service signage, shelf-grid logic, packaging utility, barcode/receipt cues, kiosk flows, and everyday transactional clarity. Utility is the cultural marker; it is not exoticized decoration.

## Scope

Use it for checkout, quick baskets, pickup flows, kiosk services, store maps, loyalty coupons, compact commerce, and convenience-retail dashboards. Avoid it for luxury, sparse editorial experiences, or Japan-themed ornament without operational convenience logic.

## 7-Dimension Profile

**Palette**: Shelf-white bases, service-icon colors, private-brand bands, barcode black, receipt neutrals, and compact product/category accents. Brand-specific colors can vary, but wayfinding must stay clear.

**Type**: Pragmatic sans with Japanese/Latin support, clear numerics, compact labels, packaging-style hierarchy, service signage, and receipt/kiosk microcopy.

**Texture**: Matte packaging board, shelf-label repetition, small utility icons, product photo cutouts, receipt paper, barcode texture, laminated service signage, and kiosk tile surfaces.

**Shape**: Onigiri triangles, circular product photos, rounded barcode/service chips, superellipse frames, shelf strips, coupon tabs, product tiles, and kiosk buttons.

**Motion**: Scanner flashes, basket additions, queue-safe confirmations, stepwise service flows, coupon activation, and receipt printout feedback.

**Spatial**: Compressed but legible modules, shelf-as-grid logic, transactional signposting, product/service lanes, and high information density with clear wayfinding.

**Cultural markers**: Onigiri opening diagrams, private-brand bands, coffee cups, bento labels, service kiosks, self-checkout signage, barcode/receipt cues, payment icons, seasonal shelf callouts, and everyday convenience vocabulary.

## Non-Negotiables

**Non-negotiables**:

- Convenience-service utility over lifestyle polish.
- Compact shelf, packaging, or kiosk information architecture.
- Clear transaction, pickup, coupon, or service affordances.
- Respectful use of Japanese retail cues with real utility.

## Connotation

**Mode:** authentic or contemporary revival.

It should feel like convenient everyday infrastructure. Utility, speed, and shelf clarity matter more than nostalgia.

## Related / Subsets

- `city-pop` is nostalgic urban leisure; Konbini Utility is operational retail convenience.
- `warm-minimalism` can share calm surfaces, but Konbini Utility is denser, more transactional, and shelf-driven.

No canonical subsets are defined yet.

## Frontend / UI Guidance

Use service lanes, compact product modules, barcode/receipt feedback, coupon tabs, pickup status, and payment icons. Design for one-handed, queue-safe decisions and bilingual/locale-sensitive labeling when appropriate.

## CSS Translation

- Color roles: `--bg-shelf`, `--surface-package`, `--accent-service`, `--accent-coupon`, `--ink-barcode`, `--receipt-paper`, and `--line-shelf`.
- Borders/dividers: shelf strips, coupon perforations, service chip outlines, and receipt rules.
- Radius language: small friendly chips, superellipse tiles, and product-photo circles.
- Effects: matte packaging, receipt texture, barcode lines, scanner flash, and kiosk press states.
- Layout: dense product/service grid with clear lanes and sticky transaction summary.
- Motion: quick confirmation, scan, basket, and receipt transitions with reduced-motion fallback.

## Typography / Fonts

Use a practical multilingual sans with strong numeric readability. Keep labels compact but not cryptic; support Japanese/Latin pairing only when the product can localize it responsibly.

## Cultural / Ethical Notes

Do not use Japanese text, icons, or retail rituals as decorative exoticism. If using locale-specific cues, tie them to actual convenience, service, packaging, or wayfinding functions.

## Anti-Patterns

- City-pop nostalgia standing in for store utility.
- Luxury whitespace that removes shelf/queue efficiency.
- Random kana or Japanese labels without meaning.
- Cute packaging motifs that obscure product, price, pickup, or payment state.
- Overanimated checkout flows that slow queue-safe use.

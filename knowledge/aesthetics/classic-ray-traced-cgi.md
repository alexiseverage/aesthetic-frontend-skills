## Dimension Synthesis

The following separates direct visual observations from proposed web translation. Color descriptions and relative geometry are estimated from visual inspection; no corpus-wide numerical sampling or measured animation is claimed.

| Dimension | Synthesis / scope | Confidence and limitation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Palette | Saturated object colors, dark blue/purple surroundings, orange gradient skies and strong light/dark separation occur frequently. Pale fog, monochrome terrain and restrained interiors are meaningful variants. Color often identifies a material or object rather than a global brand accent. | High for the inspected visual sample |
| Type | Many works contain little typography beyond a title, signature or modeled inscription. Interface chrome is not part of every render. For a materials application, use compact technical labels, tabular figures and a large plain title; do not claim a particular font as historical canon. | Low for a historical type system; proposed UI typography only |
| Texture | Marbled, veined, wood-grained, bumpy or tiled surfaces contrast smooth metal and transparent glass. Repetition and differences in procedural scale remain conspicuous. Reflections, hard highlights and refraction distinguish materials rather than providing one blanket gloss effect. | High for the inspected visual sample |
| Shape | Spheres, cylinders, toruses, planes, repeated architectural elements and more elaborate modeled subjects sit within explicit perspective. Smooth primitive surfaces can coexist with visibly faceted objects. Every region need not be rounded or chromed. | High for the inspected visual sample |
| Motion | The audited corpus is still imagery. Progressive rendering, object rotation and material changes are proposed application behaviors, not measured period animation. Keep them user-controlled and provide direct, stable reduced-motion results. | Unknown historical behavior; still composition directly observed |
| Spatial | A camera viewpoint, coherent light and a receiving ground connect objects into a scene. Cast shadows, reflections, overlaps and atmospheric layers explain depth. Impossible arrangements often retain internally consistent perspective and material response. | High for the inspected visual sample |
| Cultural markers | Artist-authored scene construction, renderer experiments, competition themes, tool notes, render times and mathematical surfaces reflect the surrounding enthusiast and research culture. The look predates and overlaps later internet nostalgia; it is not synonymous with Y2K chrome. | Medium; supporting context and variable examples |

## Direct Media Audit

**31 qualifying unique still artifacts**, individually inspected at accessible original/full-size resolution. Zero qualifying videos or interactive websites; zero generated images count as historical evidence. The 10-artifact floor and 30-artifact working target are met. Exact source, attribution, observed resolution, content hash and dimension observations are in the [linked media ledger](../media-audits/classic-ray-traced-cgi.json). Only analytical links and observations are published, not the reference files.

The broader discovery pool contained 35 image leads, including repeated views, logos, thumbnails and unresolved candidates. These are not 35 analyzed works. 31 records received full-media inspection; 0 were explicitly marked duplicate views, and 33 files were retrieved for analysis. Retrieval itself contributes zero to the qualifying count.

Thirty of the 31 qualifying works are top-listed submissions from five IRTC rounds in 1996–1997. This is substantial artist diversity but concentrated collection, period and selection bias. The additional 2003 POV-Ray work checks mathematical and low-saturation variants. Attempts to broaden to historical publication imagery produced tiny reproductions and a relative-image-link failure; neither was counted as full artwork evidence. Motion and original application UI behavior remain unknown. Ray tracing is a technique; the profile only classifies the described conspicuous geometry/material grammar, not all rendered work.

Review method: single-agent comparison of individual originals, repeated-client/product grouping, source-caption checks and mechanical ledger audit. This is not independent review or an exhaustive historical survey.

## Connections

`skeuomorphism` uses object familiarity to explain interaction; this grammar may celebrate artificial and impossible scenes. `surrealism` shares impossible staging without requiring this renderer/material vocabulary. `y2k` and `frutiger-aero` overlap chronologically or visually but contribute different typography, cultural stories and interface patterns.

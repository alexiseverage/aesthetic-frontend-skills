### Bibliography

### Visual evidence summary
Vogue documents the 2022 breakout around Margot Robbie's Barbie film stills, hot pink Valentino Pink PP, celebrity adoption, metallic separates, and the "more is more" ethos ([1]). CNN supplies the longer lineage: Barbie launched in 1959; 1990s designer-Barbie collaborations and 1990s/early-2000s super-feminine runway looks prefigure the term; Paris Hilton's 2001 hot-pink, tiara, glossy-lip, furry Barbie-bag look becomes a pre-Barbiecore pop-cultural prototype ([2]). Together they show Barbiecore as both film-boosted microtrend and recurring hyperfeminine pop spectacle.

### Seven-dimension synthesis
- Palette: Hot pink is canonical, especially saturated bubblegum/fuchsia/Valentino-pink ranges, with supporting white, glossy black, metallic silver, lavender, and candy brights. Pale pink alone is insufficient; some degree of high-chroma pink insistence is required ([1], [2]).
- Type: Rounded, bubbly, glamorous display treatments and polished sans-serif support text. Typography can be playful and shiny, but should still read as mainstream pop rather than twee scrapbook.
- Texture: Gloss, satin, patent, sequins, metallics, faux fur, crystals, shiny plastics, and polished beauty-surface finishes are central ([1], [2]).
- Shape: Figure-hugging minis, platform shoes, flared trousers, dollhouse curves, heart/star accessories, roller-disco references, and sculpted hyper-feminine silhouettes ([1], [2]).
- Motion: Peppy, performative, camera-aware, and extroverted. Motion can be bouncier and more overtly pop than in most adjacent aesthetics; this is one of the few aesthetics that tolerates theatricality well ([1]).
- Spatial: Maximal but legible. Barbiecore likes spotlight composition, vanity-table layouts, dreamhouse staging, and product-display confidence more than cluttered chaos. It is glamorous showroom space, not messy maximalism ([1], [2]).
- Cultural markers: Barbie doll history, Mattel iconography, movie tie-ins, pink convertibles/rollerblades, celebrity cosplay, Y2K starlet energy, tiaras, and fashion-week hot pink runway moments ([1], [2]).

### Related-aesthetic boundaries
- Versus `y2k`: barbiecore often borrows Y2K celebrity styling, but it is more narrowly organized around Barbie-coded hyperfemininity and pink spectacle than general millennium futurism or mall-pop nostalgia ([2]).
- Versus `maximalism`: barbiecore is a specific glam-pop subset; maximalism accepts many palettes and motifs, while barbiecore needs doll/Barbie-coded femininity and pink centrality.
- Versus `coquette`: coquette is softer, flirtier, lace-and-ribbon romanticism; barbiecore is louder, shinier, and more commercial-pop.
- Versus `warm-minimalism`: essentially opposite in palette temperature, finish, and restraint.

### Implementation notes for dictionary entry
- Non-negotiables: high-chroma pink dominance + hyperfeminine glam object vocabulary + glossy/pop surface treatment + knowingly performative attitude.
- Connotation mode can alternate between nostalgic quotation and contemporary revival. The 2022 wave is a contemporary revival through film/paparazzi/runway culture, but many references intentionally quote 1990s/2000s celebrity Barbie performance ([1], [2]).
- Important framing nuance: barbiecore is not simply "pink." It is pink plus doll/pop glamour plus mainstream spectacle.
- Likely aliases: Barbie aesthetic, Barbie pink aesthetic. Avoid treating McBling as a synonym; overlap exists, but McBling is more trash-glam celebrity tabloid/Y2K-luxury coded.

### Website-showcase guidance
Toast/notification is the assigned website component. This is a strong fit: glamorous pop-up cards, glossy rounded corners, sparkle or flash accents, playful entrance motion, and bold hierarchy. Use one hero pink plus white/metallic support. Toast states can riff on doll packaging, vanity labels, or premiere-night announcements. Keep readability high; a Barbie-pink object with illegible white text will fail instantly.

### Cultural-sensitivity / caution notes
Barbiecore carries obvious femininity politics. Avoid implying that hyperfemininity is inherently childish, trivial, or limited to a single body type. Better framing: self-aware, mainstream pop femininity with runway and celebrity amplification ([1], [2]).

### Unresolved ambiguities
- The boundary between barbiecore and pink-led Y2K/McBling can blur in celebrity examples; downstream entry should mention Y2K adjacency explicitly.
- Film-marketing-era Barbiecore may over-index on specific 2022-2023 references; dictionary entry should preserve the deeper doll

### Application decision

Keep the catalog identity and relationships. Contemporary revival can stage an original, people-free miniature desk set: curved lamp, vanity mirror, memo pad, toy-box frame, clear shine layer, finish selection and actual accessory controls. Pink Hour's wordmark, hex values, type stack, dimensions and interaction timings are proposed application choices. No Barbie logo, doll, celebrity portrait, body ideal or licensed film prop is needed. Shiny materials and theatrical packaging must not obscure readable controls. Separate Barbiecore's glossy aspirational toy world from Balletcore's rehearsal materials, Y2K's period technology and McBling's conspicuous accessory vocabulary.

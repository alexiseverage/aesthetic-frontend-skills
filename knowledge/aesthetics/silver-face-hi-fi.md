# Silver-Face Hi-Fi

## Scope and classification

The front-panel language of silver-faced domestic receivers, amplifiers and related hi-fi equipment: broad metal surfaces, a hierarchy of listening controls and closely aligned functional legends. Tuning bands, meters, warm illumination and wood sleeves are recurrent branches, not an obligatory checklist. The corpus includes meterless amplifiers and later electronic readouts; black-only equipment was excluded from the defining count.

This is a new canonical entry, screened against current catalog slugs, labels, aliases, redirects and subset names. Analog-era test instruments organize calibrated measurement. Cassette futurism invents future technology from period cues. General skeuomorphism supplies material rendering, but the source/volume/reading-band hierarchy identifies hi-fi specifically.

## Dimension Synthesis

### Palette

Silver/gray metal and dark legends with black display bands, selective warm lamps, green/blue displays or red pointers. Wood enclosures add warmth in some examples.

### Type

Small restrained sans labels, repeated function names and carefully aligned scales. Branding is usually subordinate to the whole panel, while a dominant volume/tuning control supplies visual hierarchy.

### Texture

Brushed/anodized metal, machined knobs, transparent meter/tuning windows and optional wood. Distress and grain are not required; many inspected faces are clean and precise.

### Shape

Wide horizontal chassis, round knobs of differentiated sizes, short switches and rectangular readout windows. Meterless units still retain a listening-control hierarchy.

### Motion

Still photographs do not establish authentic needle rates or tuning response. A web quotation can update meters on user action, with an instant reduced-motion fallback. Sound must be deliberately enabled, never assumed.

### Spatial

A long faceplate with a prominent reading band or large control, supported by rows of smaller labeled functions. Physical spacing communicates hierarchy more strongly than separate dashboard cards.

### Cultural markers

Source, volume, balance, tone, speaker and tuning labels. Preserve a coherent listening task rather than inventing laboratory quantities. Some components are amplifiers, not radio receivers.

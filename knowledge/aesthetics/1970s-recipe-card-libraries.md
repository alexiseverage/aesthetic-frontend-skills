## Dimension Synthesis

The following separates direct visual observations from proposed web translation. Color descriptions and relative geometry are estimated from visual inspection; no corpus-wide numerical sampling or measured animation is claimed.

| Dimension | Synthesis / scope | Confidence and limitation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Palette | Cream or white card stock with dark reading ink; restrained black, red and sometimes cyan rules; category colors in the filing furniture. Food photographs range from amber, brown, tomato and leafy green to saturated turquoise, magenta, blue and pale white. A green box is directly documented but is not universal. Apparent yellowing and shifts in scans can be age, lighting or reproduction; do not impose sepia on every surface. | High for the inspected visual sample |
| Type | Uppercase dish names have a stable filing position, with category and a prominent serial number often above or to the right. Heavy serifs, flared capitals and tall condensed sans-serifs all occur. Recipes use a clear distinction between bold ingredients, regular instructions, subrecipe titles and yields. Tiny publisher/copyright or collection lines sit at the foot. No universal historical font family is established by the raster scans. | High for observed publisher-specific hierarchy and treatment; exact historical families unverified |
| Texture | Smooth printed stock, fine photographic/halftone grain, glossy or moist food surfaces, woven cloth, ceramic, glass and polished cutlery create different texture scales. Creases and scuffed corners document particular used copies, not a necessary original finish. Use subtle procedural stock texture behind reading areas; retain crisp letters, rules and focus outlines. An oily stain over essential text turns useful domestic print into a distressed prop. | High for the inspected visual sample |
| Shape | Rectangular portrait cards, small filing margins, straight rules and sometimes rounded photographic corners. Category strips, raised divider tabs, letter/number codes and a bounded storage box make classification visible. Circular platters, molded rings, repeated portions and pedestal dishes recur in photographs but are not mandatory dish shapes. The outer card remains controlled even when the photograph is exuberant. | High for the inspected visual sample |
| Motion | The evidence is printed cards and still photographs, not an observed interface animation system. Intentional stillness suits reading and comparison. A contemporary library can change cards or faces on explicit user action, with immediate feedback or a brief optional transition. Avoid automatic rotation, spinning cards or simulated cooking progress. Reduced motion preserves the same filing, checklist and navigation operations without movement. | Unknown historical behavior; still composition directly observed |
| Spatial | A quiet header band establishes category, dish title and serial before a dominant image. A small footer anchors the collection. Reverse layouts vary between one-column ingredients/instructions and two ingredient columns above wider paragraphs; secondary recipes repeat the hierarchy. The box groups many small units under a few visible categories. Retain one active reading surface rather than compressing an entire card into a decorative grid thumbnail. | High for the inspected visual sample |
| Cultural markers | Branded home-economics publishing, convenience ingredients, entertaining categories, family/party occasions and collectible recipe organization. Elaborate molded dishes are prominent in humorous modern selections, but breakfast soup, pasta, casseroles, desserts and packed lunches also appear. Gendered headings, diet marketing and geographically broad labels belong to the historical record; they are not requirements for a contemporary audience or current nutritional authority. | Medium; supporting context and variable examples |

## Direct Media Audit

**35 qualifying unique still artifacts**, individually inspected at accessible original/full-size resolution. Zero qualifying videos or interactive websites; zero generated images count as historical evidence. The 10-artifact floor and 30-artifact working target are met. Exact source, attribution, observed resolution, content hash and dimension observations are in the [linked media ledger](../media-audits/1970s-recipe-card-libraries.json). Only analytical links and observations are published, not the reference files.

The broader discovery pool contained 268 image leads, including repeated views, logos, thumbnails and unresolved candidates. These are not 268 analyzed works. 52 records received full-media inspection; 6 were explicitly marked duplicate views, and 52 files were retrieved for analysis. Retrieval itself contributes zero to the qualifying count.

The35 qualifying works comprise10 McCall fronts,13 individually dated1971–1972 Betty Crocker cards from Vintage Recipe Cards,10 Weight Watchers cards and two Red Violet objects (the box/divider assembly and a front/back Tuna Ring card). Three publishing systems are represented across four independently useful hosts, but the sample is US-commercial and heavily selected by collectors for memorable photographs; it is not a demographic survey or a complete decade. McCall’s series date is institutionally corroborated, while exact editions of its undated mirrored fronts remain unknown. Individual graphic designers and photographers are not credited. Weight Watchers front originals are intentionally small linked collector scans,282–291px wide, distinct from94–96px gallery thumbnails: category/title/rules and whole staging are legible, but no print microtexture or precise geometric measurement is inferred. Three complete published reverse PDFs strengthen the text hierarchy; those scans are not extra works. Eight explicitly1981 Betty cards and a potentially top-cropped1971 scan were inspected but excluded. Two six-card photographic surveys contribute zero qualifying examples, and alternate scans/fronts/reverses are grouped. The attempted Marguerite Patten tag returned404 and did not become evidence; British/Australian systems remain outside the bounded claim. The final batches added vivid blue/pink/turquoise, portable meals, ordinary casseroles, child-party scenes, actual reverses and box/dividers, contradicting the all-brown/gelatin stereotype. Collection stopped after35 independent works with the target and key layout variants covered. Motion and original navigation behavior remain unknown; proposed responsive controls are application choices. Reference files remain private, with analytical links only in the public profile.

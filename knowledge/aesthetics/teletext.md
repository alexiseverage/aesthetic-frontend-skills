# Teletext

## Scope and Sources

European broadcast teletext's character-cell vocabulary and documented art continuations. Treat this as a medium-derived visual system with distinct information-service and art branches, not a single movement. The [1976 BBC/IBA/BREMA specification](https://decode-orc.github.io/analogue-video-specifications/teletext/BBC-Broadcast-Teletext-1976/BBC-Broadcast-Teletext-1976/) supplies historical technical context through a clearly identified transcription mirror: bounded rows/characters, alphanumeric/graphics modes, restricted display colors, double-height and conceal/flash controls. It does not establish one compulsory typeface, modern interaction timing or universal international format.

The [mb21 Ceefax reconstruction](https://www.teletext.mb21.co.uk/gallery/ceefax/evening/19831003/nojs.html), [ORACLE archive](https://teletext.mb21.co.uk/gallery/oracle/main1.shtml) and [4-Tel archive](https://www.teletext.mb21.co.uk/gallery/ancillary/c4-s4c/index.shtml) anchor historical information layouts. [MUTA/VILKE](https://www.vilkecollection.fi/muta/index.html) identifies the artists and selected works; [Horsenburger](https://www.horsenburger.com/teletextartgallery) and [Paula Lehtonen](https://www.paulalehtonen.net/paula_lehtonen/works_pages/teletextart.html) provide creator-published originals. The official [NOS](https://nos.nl/teletekst), [ARD](https://www.ard-text.de/) and [Yle](https://yle.fi/aihe/tekstitv?P=100) viewers provide actual inspected navigation behavior. Their news freshness was not verified and no headline claim is reused.

### Claim support and boundary

- **Observed, service grammar:** grouped Ceefax layouts, ORACLE,4-Tel and all three live viewers support numeric addresses, short aligned fields, title/footer bands, colored hierarchy and topic directories. NOS/ARD/Yle navigation was actually exercised; none is counted once per page.

- **Observed, contradiction:** the low-information Transmission export is an authentic contextual art lead but its isolated bars do not justify a defining visual rule or fill the qualifying count. Game imagery in Horsenburger's arcade work does not make every teletext artifact game-native.
- **Source-supported:** the bounded page and display-mode context distinguish this from arbitrary pixel art. Exact technical constraints should be tied to the named standard, not projected onto every international system or every artist raster.
- **Proposed:** a responsive directory/table, validated page lookup, explicit saved items, useful detail disclosures and deliberate focus management are contemporary software translations. They are not claims about 1970s receivers.
- **Unknown:** original screen calibration and font files, detailed provenance of every archive renderer, full multi-image series beyond the archived representatives, exact timing of the sampled GIF, historical polling/flash cadence and universal responsive behavior.

### Classification and software suitability

New distinct aesthetic, placed in `technical-institutional` for its broadcast information system. Pixel art shares hard pixels and color limits but usually emphasizes sprites/tiles/game HUDs. Bloomberg-style terminals emphasize financial command density and concurrent expert panes. Early Internet emphasizes authored web publishing, browser furniture and link culture. A designer preserving page references, character-cell hierarchy, banded information and mosaic construction would produce a different interface from those neighbors.

Strong fits: event/programme guides, public directories, reference browsers, compact schedules, editorial indexes and small utility dashboards. The grammar survives without a large illustration in headers, page lookup, navigation, table rows, detail disclosures, selected states and alerts. Weak fit: long prose or high-stakes tasks forced into tiny emulator text. Artistic subject matter does not dictate the software content.

## Application Handoff

Contemporary interpretation for a fictional local-events planner. Proposed roles: black `#000000`, white `#FFFFFF`, cyan `#00FFFF`, yellow `#FFFF00`, green `#00FF00`, magenta `#FF00FF`, warning `#FF5555`, blue panel `#0000BB`. These are chosen CSS values, not sampled broadcast metrics. White on blue and bright text on black suit reading; blue-on-black essential text must be avoided. Color always has a textual/state counterpart.

Use system monospace/Courier New, 16px body/1.4 leading, 12–14px secondary labels and 32–44px short title lettering. No added font payload required. Keep 0px radii, flat bands, strong solid rules, a 4/8/12/16/24px spacing scale and tabular numeric columns. On mobile, reflow metadata and rows instead of shrinking everything into a literal miniature. Exact sizes are proposed. Stillness is intentional; disable inherited motion, with the same reduced-motion states.

Include: numbered page references, colored title strips, aligned schedule fields, explicit subpage/section labels, and an original mosaic mark. Avoid: copied broadcaster logos, compulsory sprite-game motifs, green hacker monochrome, fabricated current news, fake reception waits, and essential blinking.

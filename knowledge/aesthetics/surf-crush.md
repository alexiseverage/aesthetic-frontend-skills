# Surf Crush

Classification: **separately useful canonical visual system**, related to the neighbors below. A retrospective commercial cluster of circa-2000 surf-retail and youth graphics: flat tropical flowers, wave contours, surf emblems, sporty stripes and beach-material accents. Bound this as a mediated retail/entertainment look, not the whole history or identity of surfing and not every tropical design.

## Analysis

A retrospective commercial cluster of circa-2000 surf-retail and youth graphics: flat tropical flowers, wave contours, surf emblems, sporty stripes and beach-material accents. Bound this as a mediated retail/entertainment look, not the whole history or identity of surfing and not every tropical design.

Tiki/Polynesian Pop has a different bar/exotica lineage and greater cultural appropriation risks. Mermaidcore centers underwater fantasy. Palm Beach Prep favors resort/preppy styling. A hibiscus alone does not prove a period date or surf provenance.

The non-negotiable formal traits are: Commercial surf/tropical graphic vocabulary; flat floral/wave or sporty band relationships; coordinated beach/sport material cues; usable content hierarchy. They are qualitative conclusions from the inspected corpus, not a universal frequency claim. Palette descriptions are estimated by visual inspection; exact hex colors below are authored application choices. Physical images cannot establish software timing, responsive breakpoints or interaction states.

Application proposal: Build a surf-school booking flow with lesson tabs, day selection and equipment quantities. Wave-shaped separators, printed floral panels and contrast piping should style the controls themselves. Times are illustrative schedules, not live tide or weather data.

## Historical / Origin Notes

New Objectivity emerged in Germany after World War I as artists rejected Expressionism for sober, often satirical realism attentive to war trauma, social inequality, bureaucracy, and urban modernity.

## Analysis

Weimar-era unsentimental realism: sober observation, sharp social detail, restrained typography, clinical light, and class-conscious critique that rejects expressionist mysticism and decorative escape.

Non-negotiables for implementation: unsentimental observational stance; Weimar social/historical context; muted documentary materiality; critical rather than heroic framing.

Cultural sensitivity assessment: New Objectivity is historically sensitive because it comes from post-WWI Germany, urban poverty, disability, veterans, sexuality, class conflict, and the fragile Weimar Republic before Nazi repression.

Iwasaki-related sources dominate the corpus, with independent Sample Kobo and Japan House documentation adding display and workshop contexts. Promotional compositions are counted once, never once per dish; repeated physical designs in other photographs were conservatively grouped. Ordinary retro toys, store exteriors and unrelated decor were excluded. Static pictures establish no serving animation or edible ingredient/allergen facts. The profile concerns visual/material craft, not a tested restaurant ordering workflow.

## Connections

`skeuomorphism` shares material imitation, but this entry retains food-specific craft and menu/display structure. `konbini-utility` shares practical food identification while relying on packaged labeling and retail systems. The independent maker’s retro toys were useful counterexamples: context in the same shop does not make them food-replica artifacts.

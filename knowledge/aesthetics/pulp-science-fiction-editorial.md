# Pulp Science-Fiction Editorial

## Scope and classification

Distinct print-editorial system, related to Atomic Age, Art Deco and Space Western without being an alias for those worldbuilding/period languages. Scope includes early scientifiction visual precursors and later specialist pulps. Magazine issue is the conservative deduplication unit: extra cover/interior/ad pages from one issue count zero additional works even when independently drawn illustrations appear.

## Source / Evidence Links

- [Encyclopedia of Science Fiction, Pulp](https://sf-encyclopedia.com/entry/pulp): distinguishes material/format from broader metaphor and dates specialist pulp development. Early Amazing was letter-size before its1933 change; early precursors here are explicitly labeled. Coarse absorbent paper and garish covers are historically supported, not a command to degrade text.
- [Pulp Magazines Project, Amazing Stories](https://www.pulpmags.org/content/info/amazing-stories.html): scholarly publication history and original1926 launch context. Full facsimile May/June contents/editorial/story pages inspected, not merely gallery thumbnails.
- [Pulp Magazines Project, Planet Stories](https://www.pulpmags.org/content/info/planet-stories.html), Nathan Vernon Madison: history of Fiction House magazine, interplanetary escapism, multiple illustrators and correspondence culture. Winter1939 contents/story opener directly inspected; different issue identifiers are archive codes, not exact cover month claims.
- [Museum of Pop Culture, Frank R. Paul](https://www.mopop.org/sffhof/frank-r-paul): museum account connects technical/architectural training, enormous machines and bright basic color to Paul's work. Its claim about illustration history does not identify every unattributed scanned cover.
- [Asgard Press curated cover gallery](https://www.asgardpress.com/a-gallery-of-vintage-pulp-sci-fi-covers/): complete580px published originals, not smaller srcset thumbnails. Artist credits are gallery attributions. A paragraph on the page conflates a1929 title; the individual caption/cover reads Science Wonder Stories, retained here. Broad UFO-first claims and dates are not adopted.
- Commons full original-file records supply three additional issues. These hosting collections support analysis only; reproduction rights are not assumed from availability.

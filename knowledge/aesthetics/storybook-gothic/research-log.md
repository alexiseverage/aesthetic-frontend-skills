## Observation

The pilot pass found enough source-grounded evidence to treat Storybook Gothic as a canonical but limited-evidence dictionary entry. The strongest evidence came from VistaPrint's US 2026 logo-trends report and its German edition, both of which name Storybook Gothic and describe folklore/fantasy branding, moon phases, enchanted flora, mythic creatures, blackletter-inspired display typography, clean sans support type, rich dramatic color, and metallic accents.

Secondary commentary from Digital Synopsis and KOTA corroborated the broader direction as story-driven gothic or heritage typography, modern gothic emotion, folklore, rooted brand identity, and a selective layer paired with modern layout discipline.

## Dimension Impact

- Palette: supports jewel-dark forest/midnight/burgundy/charcoal palettes with restrained metallic accents.
- Type: supports gothic-informed display, blackletter accents, illuminated-initial energy, and readable support typography.
- Texture: supports engraved, foil, stamped, parchment, and ceremonial print tactility.
- Shape/spatial: supports seals, crests, medallions, moon-phase badges, centered emblem-first hierarchy, and framed hero compositions.
- Motion: only indirectly evidenced; slow reveal, shimmer, and glow should remain synthesis-level guidance rather than a source-quoted mandate.
- Boundaries: distinguishes Storybook Gothic from Dark Academia, Gothic Revival, Witchcore, and Dark Fantasy.

## Analysis

Pierrotcore is application-ready as a limited-evidence entry when it is presented as a contemporary revival of historically grounded theatrical archetypes, not as a massively documented internet-native label. The load-bearing pattern is explicit Pierrot/Harlequin theatrical archetype + monochrome/diamond costume language + melancholy or trickster-formal connotation + stage-role spatial organization.

The final synthesis chose `pierrotcore` as the cleanest canonical slug for the theatrical monochrome/diamond sibling lane. Evidence is weaker than clowncore or kidcore as a modern community label, but stronger as a coherent design system because Pierrot and Harlequin provide stable, well-documented costume/performance vocabularies. The profile therefore marks evidence as limited and avoids claiming a single-origin internet naming history.

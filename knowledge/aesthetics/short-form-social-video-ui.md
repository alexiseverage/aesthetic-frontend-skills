# Short-Form Social Video UI

## Scope and classification

A descriptive digital interface vernacular organized around a short clip as the dominant media plane, compact social actions and creator/caption context. Full-height portrait feeds are common; a loop viewer with horizontal reactions, creation tools and comments/share sheets provide meaningful variants. This is a separately useful compositional system, related to flat utility design and contemporary social media rather than a branded color theme. Live-stream gifts, card-based discovery, general chat and avatar games are boundaries. The source corpus does not establish a single inventor or a universal chronological lineage.

## Analysis

The useful defining relationship is a dominant moving media plane plus compact controls and identity/context. Multiple publishers support white reaction rails, lower captions and dark navigation; Coub prevents turning the right rail into a universal law. Reels 2020 and Shorts recording show that creation surfaces have their own tools and staging. Byte makes the control vocabulary visibly authored through chunky silhouettes and strongly segmented tools without requiring a TikTok palette. Pinterest and Rednote expose a second layer of social action in rounded sheets, while the live Loops website verifies a desktop panel that remains beside the clip.

Source-supported launch context establishes that these products introduced specific creation/discovery surfaces in particular releases. It does not settle invention or a single line of influence. Interface Lineage was useful discovery but its TikTok comparison sheet and Tinder illustration were excluded from qualifying media. Recovered historical behavior, current recommendation algorithms, safety promises, engagement values and reward policy remain unknown or outside visual scope. No specific font or contrast figure is inferred solely from screenshots.

The proposed application is a finite art-loop viewer with original silent clips, readable captions, explicit playback/seek, local reactions/bookmarks/follow and a responsive conversation surface. Its CSS values, interaction semantics, clip lengths and reduced-motion behavior are design decisions, not historical measurements. Default posters and no automatic advancement preserve user control. A local comment demo should say where its state lives; it should neither claim a network submission nor fabricate real users. The new component’s silhouette must remain a media viewer with overlays and a structured sheet/panel, rather than an ordinary gallery of videos.

Self-review checked the core-versus-live-room boundary, repeated app views, regional kinship, promotional-versus-live behavior, producer-added motion, source rights and source concentration. No independent review is claimed. Confidence is high for palette roles, geometry and composition, medium for motion flow and cultural breadth, and low for exact historical timing or origin attribution. The 28/30 exception and historical-access limitations remain visible.

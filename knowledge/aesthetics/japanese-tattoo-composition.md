## Dimension Synthesis

The following separates direct visual observations from proposed web translation. Color descriptions and relative geometry are estimated from visual inspection; no corpus-wide numerical sampling or measured animation is claimed.

| Dimension | Synthesis / scope | Confidence and limitation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Palette | Dark gray and black grounds contrast skin reserves and selected red, yellow, green, blue or pink subjects. Some works are substantially monochrome; bright primary color is common rather than compulsory. Pigment, healed skin, photography and lighting prevent a universal sampled palette. | High for the inspected visual sample |
| Type | Lettering is subordinate or absent in many compositions. Occasional signatures and inscriptions are contextual evidence, not a mandatory display font. Use restrained readable type for interface content; invented kanji or a generic “Asian” display face is inappropriate. | Low for a historical type system; proposed UI typography only |
| Texture | Fine scales, feathers, cloth patterns and petals sit against broad shaded grounds. Heavy subject contours, thinner internal lines and pale separation channels produce several levels of visual weight. Do not simulate damaged skin or add indiscriminate grunge. | High for the inspected visual sample |
| Shape | Long coiling bodies, overlapping flower heads, cloud lobes, curling water and rhythmic wind bars connect across surfaces. Sleeve ends, chest openings and thigh borders have designed terminations. The tattoo follows an anatomical field rather than a rectangular illustration crop. | High for the inspected visual sample |
| Motion | Flow and rotation are implied by still composition. These references do not establish animation timing. A contemporary interface can remain still, with immediate disclosures and selection changes; reduced motion must retain every operation. | Unknown historical behavior; still composition directly observed |
| Spatial | One dominant subject occupies a large connected field, supported by secondary motifs and a shared ground. Paired chest panels may frame an open central channel; other works cover the front continuously. Open skin is an active compositional element, not unused background. | High for the inspected visual sample |
| Cultural markers | Horimono and wabori practice, practitioner lineages, pictorial narratives and the relationship to Japanese print culture matter. Religious figures, historical characters and inscriptions carry specific meanings. Neither criminality nor exoticism is an acceptable shorthand for the art or its wearers. | Medium; supporting context and variable examples |

## Direct Media Audit

**30 qualifying unique still artifacts**, individually inspected at accessible original/full-size resolution. Zero qualifying videos or interactive websites; zero generated images count as historical evidence. The 10-artifact floor and 30-artifact working target are met. Exact source, attribution, observed resolution, content hash and dimension observations are in the [linked media ledger](../media-audits/japanese-tattoo-composition.json). Only analytical links and observations are published, not the reference files.

The broader discovery pool contained 284 image leads, including repeated views, logos, thumbnails and unresolved candidates. These are not 284 analyzed works. 59 records received full-media inspection; 9 were explicitly marked duplicate views, and 66 files were retrieved for analysis. Retrieval itself contributes zero to the qualifying count.

The corpus is concentrated in museum photography, one international studio and two documentary photographers. It includes many artists but is not a survey of every Japanese regional school or historical period. Client views, group photographs, uncertain repeated suits, isolated motifs and drawing-only material were excluded or grouped. Pigment colors are affected by skin, healing, lighting and photographic treatment. Religious figure identification beyond source captions remains unverified.

### Translation and remaining uncertainty

The defining palette/material, shape and spatial relationships have strong direct support within this bounded sample. Type and motion must retain their stated limitations. Proposed colors, web spacing, breakpoints, animation duration and controls in the canonical entry are design decisions, not measurements recovered from static imagery. No font family is identified without metadata. Cultural narratives beyond the linked source descriptions remain outside the claim scope.

Review method: single-agent comparison of individual originals, repeated-client/product grouping, source-caption checks and mechanical ledger audit. This is not independent review or an exhaustive historical survey.

## Connections

Compared with `cybersigilism`, these works use continuous narrative/figural grounds rather than barbed abstract sigils. `chicano-lowrider-art` is a neighboring tattoo culture, not a substitute cultural vocabulary. Isolated Gan animal motifs, Polynesian-pattern work and illustrative American-style tattoos were inspected as boundary comparisons and excluded from the count.

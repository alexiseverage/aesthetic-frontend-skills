## Historical / Origin Notes

Shaker furniture was developed by the United Society of Believers in Christ’s Second Appearing, whose religious values of simplicity, utility, honesty, and communal order shaped objects and interiors.

## Analysis

Religious-community craft language of simplicity, utility, honesty, durable joinery, calm proportion, and storage/order systems derived from Shaker communal life.

Non-negotiables for implementation: utility before ornament; honest material/joinery; ordered storage logic; calm religious-community restraint.

Cultural sensitivity assessment: Medium religious/living-community context: Shaker design is not just “simple farmhouse.” Treat it as a tradition from a specific religious communal movement, and avoid stripping its spiritual discipline into empty lifestyle austerity.

## Connections

Warm-minimalism shares restraint but is lifestyle-commercial; arts-and-crafts shares craft honesty but is more ornamental and medievalist; scandinavian-modern shares utility and lightness but not the Shaker religious communal context.

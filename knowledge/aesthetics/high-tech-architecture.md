## Historical / Origin Notes

High-tech architecture emerged in the 1970s as late modernism began expressing industrial technology, lightweight materials, and construction systems as the visible language of buildings.

## Analysis

Late-modern architectural language of exposed structure, visible services, lightweight industrial materials, modular bays, and building-as-machine legibility.

Non-negotiables for implementation: exposed structure/services; industrial steel-glass materiality; modular/mechanical legibility; color-coded systems.

Cultural sensitivity assessment: Low identity-risk but real power-context risk: many icons are corporate, museum, airport, or institutional projects. Avoid presenting infrastructure as automatically neutral or democratic.

## Connections

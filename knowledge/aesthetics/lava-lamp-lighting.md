## Direct Media Audit

30 independent artifacts qualify (2 artifact, 24 still, 4 video). Complete source media, full publication figures and sampled playback intervals are identified above. Source scans, diagrams, and footage were used for analysis. Related crops and variants of a given work were grouped.

## Analysis

Mathmos catalog reproductions show change in supports and enclosure from Astro Lantern and Lunar to Fluidium and Fireflow O1. The museum base photograph isolates tooling and reflectance details. Relative apparent wax area varies substantially across stills and clip frames. Vincentz shows an attached head narrowing and separating over a six-second sampling window, while the Anthony clip and Swaby footage have different shape density and color gradients. The Pexels/Pixabay motion example explicitly uses slow-motion playback. Treat clip intervals as observable within the displayed footage and avoid converting them to a common physical cycle length or minimum apparent speed. Absolute photometry cannot be recovered from source exposure. In U.S. examples, the Consort uses a cylinder, the Imperial is documented with a nine-stage instruction diagram, and the Empress places the bottle in a wall fixture. A thin light band at the base and a bright meniscus can coexist with a substantially darker room. Photo-based ratios describe projected area or height. They do not establish liquid volume, wax fraction by mass, or illuminance.

## Connections

- Atomic Age contributes rocket associations and metal supports within a separate design movement.
- Psychedelic can share historical color associations without being defined by illumination.
- Light and Space shares attention to perceived illumination and falloff, sometimes at architectural scale.

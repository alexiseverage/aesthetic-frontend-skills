# New Age Dolphin Fantasy

## Source / Evidence Links

- [Christian Riese Lassen's official artist statement](https://www.lassenart.com/the-artist/) describes a blend of real and imaginary marine worlds. The [Dolphins & Whales](https://www.lassenart.com/dolphins-whales/) and [Two Worlds](https://www.lassenart.com/two-worlds/) collections identify distinct works. Promotional superlatives and supernatural-talent claims are not adopted as facts.
- [Schim Schimmel's official biography](https://schimmelsmith.com/about-2__trashed/bio/) links wildlife, Earth and cosmic imagery, describing his move to full-time painting in 1987 and a Tokyo exhibition in 1991. [His licensing representative](https://www.creativerightsinc.com/schim-schimmel) calls the work Environmental Visionary Surrealism and describes ecological interdependence. Neither source establishes an artist's religious beliefs or a historical movement called New Age Dolphin Fantasy.
- [Jim Warren's official biography](https://jimwarren.com/about/) describes a 1990s environmental focus. His [Seven Dolphins](https://jimwarren.com/portfolio/seven-dolphins/) record identifies an oil painting; [Dolphin Whisperer](https://jimwarren.com/portfolio/dolphin-whisperer/) supplies a contrasting rainbow-body treatment. The inspected Splash Dance is excluded as a non-marine boundary.
- [Novaspace's Passage of the Dolphin publication](https://novaspace.com/shop/space-art/schim-schimmel/passage-of-the-dolphin/) explicitly links dolphins, outer space and the natural world in its account of Schimmel's work. [Gallery Direct Art's Our Home Too II record](https://www.gallerydirectart.com/ga-1024.html) identifies the distinct dolphin/Earth composition; edition/authenticity claims were not independently tested.
- [Seven Dolphins' creator biographies](https://dolphins-unlimited.com/pages/artist-bios) and the individual product records below provide attributed decorative objects and reflective print presentations. These are bounded material/merchandising examples, not evidence that every dolphin collectible was made for spiritual practice. Retailer prose with an incorrect dolphin count and unsupported process-history claims is not promoted into the visual analysis.

## Analysis

New Age Dolphin Fantasy is a descriptive visual label for luminous, idealized cetacean worlds where underwater clarity, cosmic imagery, dreamlike illumination or surreal transformation exceeds ordinary marine depiction. It draws on visionary/environmental fantasy and decorative circulation; it is not presented as a historically self-named movement or a synonym for all marine art. Dolphin-led imagery is central, while selected whale/orca works establish a closely related cetacean variant.

The strongest shared relationship is smooth, brightly modeled organic forms suspended within unusually legible depth. Light beams, white caustic webs, bubble chains and small star-like accents direct attention toward an open luminous center. A dark reef or cosmic surround contains dense miniature detail around that center. Above/below-water splits, planets at incompatible scales, impossible simultaneous horizons and rainbow body transformations make the fantasy relationship explicit. Not every work needs a planet or rainbow; those are variants, not universal requirements.

DO03–06/30 show why a cosmic tiger, horse, leopard or wolf alone remains a neighboring environmental-fantasy image. DO10/15 show that moonlit whale tails can remain broad romantic marine art. DO38/39 show that realistic dolphins in beautiful blue water also fall short of the stronger fantasy relationship. DO27 has rainbow liquid color but lacks a marine identity. These exclusions constrain the scope instead of using creator names or subject tags as automatic qualifications.

The corpus has a substantial Lassen concentration and relatively fewer Warren works. It therefore supports shared color/light/composition relationships more strongly than universal culture, typography or historical motion. Schimmel's planetary-scale juxtapositions and Warren's hidden/transformed dolphin forms preserve differences rather than collapsing all three artists into one signature style. DO32/33 are adjacent collectible material references; they help translate reflective contours and compact faceted framing, but do not define spiritual use or prove an artist's intent.

**Proposed frontend translation:** build an original ambient listening interface called Pelagic Radio. Its player shell can have a deep curved cobalt body, a bright translucent inner well, a thin wave-like seek track, a small dolphin-shaped playhead, pearly crescent volume controls and a low row of named station/scene selectors. Put concise titles and elapsed time in quiet dark reading wells. The player, station list, selected state, progress rail and loading/status treatment should embody the smooth/light/depth relationship; a regular rectangular card with a marine painting attached is insufficient.

The composition can put one large central playback well inside a luminous irregular border, with miniature star/bubble markers at its periphery. Use original abstract dolphin/wave SVG contours and independently created light textures. Do not trace any source work, reuse a signature animal pose/composition, or ask a generator to imitate any named living artist. No portraits or people are needed. Where audio is included, use user-provided or original/licensed audio and an actual working play/pause/seek/volume flow; clearly identify any visual-only demonstration.

Proposed roles, not measured painting constants: surround `#071735`, deep body `#153A77`, inner cyan `#56C6DF`, pearl `#EDF9F6`, violet `#7060C6`, warm accent `#E9BB78` and quiet text well `#10294C`. Layer radial/linear gradients and a restrained original caustic line mask around functional surfaces. Small glossy highlights can define borders and selected controls. Functional labels need opaque high-contrast planes; glowing text and busy caustics behind every word would undermine the light hierarchy.

A readable humanist/system sans or restrained serif can carry track titles and metadata; a lightly letterspaced display title is a modern choice. No source supports calling a particular font historically canonical. Use real range inputs, buttons and lists with accessible names. Preserve keyboard operation and visible focus, expose selected station in text/shape as well as color, label time/volume, and announce loaded/playing/paused state without noisy continuous time announcements. Touch targets must not inherit the narrow physical fin of an ornamental icon.

All source motion remains unknown. The modern player may be intentionally still except for truthful playback progress; optional slow decorative caustics must stop under reduced motion and never be necessary for understanding status. Avoid autoplay audio, strobing sparkle, parallax that distorts text and fake loading delays. On phones, stack the listening well and station selector, retain a generous seek target and keep the curved body/material framing; do not shrink a desktop control panel.

**Cultural and rights context:** ecological and cosmic imagery can suggest wonder and interconnectedness without making medical, spiritual-healing or dolphin-therapy claims. Do not infer an artist's religion from a painted Earth or star field. Keep source attribution and artwork rights in research, and publish original interface assets. A royalty-free-looking retailer image is not a reuse license. The artwork's fantasy animals are not a basis for claims about actual dolphin behavior or conservation status. Metallic process stories and edition authentication are outside this analysis.

# Vectordelia

Classification: **separately useful canonical visual system**, related to the neighbors below. A retrospective label for exuberant commercial vector-like composition built from circles, rays, arrows, ribbons, stars, halftone fields and urban/cultural cutouts. Frutiger Metro is a common alternate search name; it does not imply a Frutiger typeface or a direct relationship to Microsoft’s Metro interface. Connected botanical growth remains separately described by Vectorbloom.

## Analysis

A retrospective label for exuberant commercial vector-like composition built from circles, rays, arrows, ribbons, stars, halftone fields and urban/cultural cutouts. Frutiger Metro is a common alternate search name; it does not imply a Frutiger typeface or a direct relationship to Microsoft’s Metro interface. Connected botanical growth remains separately described by Vectorbloom.

Vectorbloom uses connected botanical/hybrid growth as its organizing principle. Vectordelia covers broader non-botanical geometric ornament. Frutiger Aero centers glossy nature/technology imagery and interface polish. Vectorheart is more industrial/diagrammatic and usually less florid.

The non-negotiable formal traits are: Crisp layered vector-like motifs; rhythmic circles/rays/ribbons or related geometric system; deliberate directional density; a clear information hierarchy. They are qualitative conclusions from the inspected corpus, not a universal frequency claim. Palette descriptions are estimated by visual inspection; exact hex colors below are authored application choices. Physical images cannot establish software timing, responsive breakpoints or interaction states.

Application proposal: Build a poster-layout tool with motif layers, ribbon/orbit choices and undo. Selected layers change the visible composition; use plain controls, circular active markers and striped caption rails. Export must reflect the actual selected state.

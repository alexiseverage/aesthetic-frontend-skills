### Visual evidence summary
BuzzFeed News is the best origin source: Lex Nicoleta coins/propels the term around Nancy Meyers heroines, especially Meryl Streep in `It's Complicated`, with neutral flowing tops, self-care domesticity, hosting, fresh flowers, magazines, kitchens, and button-down shirts ([1]). Vogue compresses the street-style translation into breezy oversized button-ups, cool hats, cozy knits, and ecru-toned summer layering ([2]). Wirecutter turns the object vocabulary into retail specifics: linen shirt, cashmere cardigan, linen robe, leather sandals/huaraches, white sneakers, and "easy luxury" geared toward life's simple pleasures ([3]).

### Seven-dimension synthesis
- Palette: Ecru, cream, white, oatmeal, sand, pale khaki, sea-glass neutrals, faded chambray, and occasional soft navy. Contrast is low to medium, sun-softened rather than stark ([1], [2], [3]).
- Type: Clean, readable serif or tasteful humanist sans with editorial/home-lifestyle overtones. Type should feel mature, calm, and magazine-friendly rather than trend-chasing.
- Texture: Linen, light cashmere, cotton poplin, wicker/rattan-adjacent home textures, ceramic/table linen, breezy drape, and lightly rumpled natural fibers are core ([1], [3]).
- Shape: Oversized button-downs, easy cardigans, relaxed trousers, roomy shirting, sun hats, open robes, low-stress sandals, and table/homeware silhouettes. Nothing body-con or aggressively tailored ([1], [2], [3]).
- Motion: Slow, domestic, breezy, and unhurried. Motion should evoke doors opening to sea air, fabric drift, gentle page turns, or hosting rituals rather than kinetic trendiness.
- Spatial: Airy rooms, kitchen-is-center domestic scenes, porch/patio/beach-house layouts, uncluttered but lived-in surfaces, and comfortable hosting zones are important ([1]).
- Cultural markers: Nancy Meyers films, Diane Keaton/Meryl Streep references, hosting, fresh flowers, magazines, deli sandwiches, iced tea, piano, Williams Sonoma, linen shirting, cardigans, and tasteful beach-house leisure ([1], [2], [3]).

### Related-aesthetic boundaries
- Versus `warm-minimalism`: both like neutrals and restraint, but coastal grandmother is softer, more domestic, and more coastal/home-hosting coded; warm minimalism is cleaner, pricier, and more design-system polished.
- Versus `cottagecore`: cottagecore is rural, handmade, floral, and whimsical; coastal grandmother is affluent-adult, seaside, and Nancy Meyers domestic rather than pastoral fantasy.
- Versus `quiet-luxury`: coastal grandmother overlaps with "easy luxury" materials, but it is less stealth-wealth formal and more approachable, hosting-oriented, and film-character specific ([1], [3]).
- Versus `scandinavian-modern`: both can be light and airy, but coastal grandmother needs East/West Coast leisure cues, linens, and homemaking signals.

### Analysis and boundaries

Coastal Grandmother is a 2022 internet lifestyle label for relaxed domestic hospitality, natural fibres and an affluent coastal-leisure image associated with Nancy Meyers films. Its useful design translation is lived-in hosting and material ease, not generic nautical decoration or a demographic prescription.

Use recipe and hosting notebooks, home goods, gathering plans or calm domestic editorial. A beach gradient, anchor icon or expensive minimalist room alone is insufficient. People, age and beachfront ownership need not carry the visual grammar.

### Proposed frontend application

Use menu choices, ingredient quantities, preparation lists and editable notes. Original still-life objects and subtle linen rules can communicate the material system without portraits. Keep practical text large enough; quiet design does not require faint controls.

Proposed palette: linen #f4efdf, ink #3d5143, sage #8d9c7b, pale blue #adc0c7 and clay #b17658. Thin woven rules, matte off-white cards and a serif/sans pairing are contemporary choices. Use open spacing that collapses to one column; no looping material motion is required.

Pair a warm readable serif such as Georgia with a straightforward sans for quantities and controls. Avoid fine low-contrast luxury type and faux handwritten recipe text that cannot be read.

## Connections

Compared with UPA graphic modernism, the category foregrounds elastic bodies/props and physical impossibility across several earlier production traditions, not one postwar studio’s selective-plane grammar. Clowncore is a contemporary persona/dress system, not an animation technique; many rubber-hose subjects are animals, objects or human performers rather than clowns. Surrealism overlaps impossible transformation without requiring cartoon contour or theatrical pose logic.

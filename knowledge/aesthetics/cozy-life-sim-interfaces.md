# Cozy Life-Sim Interfaces

## Scope and classification

Distinct descriptive game-interface language, related to Cottagecore and Skeuomorphism but organized around possessions, recipes, letters and neighborly tasks. Farm/RPG, town, boat and tavern-management works provide explicitly adjacent variants. The term is not an assertion of a single named historical movement or a promise that every sampled game is universally relaxing. Screens from the same authored game system are grouped as one work; multiple pages from one manual also count once.

## Source / Evidence Links

- [Russell Vaccaro, Fae Farm UI art](https://foxrocket.com/faefarm.html): primary lead-artist account describes a reusable screen template, cream/wood-derived comforting colors and balancing icons against the world. Derek Valerio is credited for UX; supporting artists are named on the page. The inventory and house-storage originals were inspected. No attribution of every icon to Vaccaro alone.
- [Glumberland, April 2019 devlog](https://ooblets.com/2019/04/april-2019-devlog/): primary pre-release context describes simplified purchasing, management/almanac overhaul, scrollbars, expandable quest HUD and scheduled letters. Two complete originals were inspected as one game system. These 2019 screens are not asserted to be the final current interface.
- [DigiPen, Molly Jameson on Cozy Grove](https://www.digipen.edu/showcase/news/digipen-grad-molly-jameson-engineers-snazzy-clothes-cozy-grove): institutional interview describes low-key daily play and UI/localization engineering. Its gameplay cadence does not determine an interface animation duration or establish every player's emotional response.
- [Cozy Grove Camp Spirit control guide](https://games-netflix.helpshift.com/hc/en/4-cozy-grove-camp-spirit/faq/255-what-are-the-controls/?p=android): primary support context lists backpack, quick slots, currency, badges, quest notebook and touch controls. This text does not count as inspected game media or a separate visual work.
- [Interface In Game, Animal Crossing New Horizons](https://interfaceingame.com/games/animal-crossing-new-horizons/): full 1280×720 recipe and dialogue captures. Same game system, one qualifying work. The gallery's own site loader is excluded from game-motion analysis.
- [Nintendo DS official manuals](https://www.nintendo.com/en-za/Support/Legacy-system/Nintendo-DS-games-manuals-1006833.html) and [Nintendo 3DS official manuals](https://www.nintendo.com/en-gb/Support/Legacy-system/Nintendo-3DS-games-electronic-manuals-850636.html): complete Wild World and New Leaf PDFs, selected full pages/diagrams directly inspected. Small embedded screen images support broad layout/color; their tiny text is not a basis for exact type measurements.
- Official Steam developer/publisher galleries are individually linked below. Full original images, not store thumbnail cards, support observations. Promotional worlds often hide the interface; those discovery files were excluded. Store screenshots may be older/pre-release or captioned and do not establish current release behavior.

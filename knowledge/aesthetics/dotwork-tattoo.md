# Dotwork Tattoo

Classification: **separately useful canonical visual system**, related to the neighbors below. A craft grammar that constructs tone, volume or patterned fields with discrete points and their changing density. It includes figurative, botanical, geometric and ornamental work; it is not every blackwork tattoo, every dotted outline or a universal sacred-symbol system.

## Analysis

A craft grammar that constructs tone, volume or patterned fields with discrete points and their changing density. It includes figurative, botanical, geometric and ornamental work; it is not every blackwork tattoo, every dotted outline or a universal sacred-symbol system.

Fine Line emphasizes contour delicacy and may use stipple selectively. Op Art pursues optical relationships across varied media; Guilloché uses continuous repeated lines. A barbed-wire outline, dense solid black fill or generic sigil without point tone was excluded.

The non-negotiable formal traits are: Tonal or structural work done by discrete points; intentional density/negative-space relationships; coherent bounded geometry or illustrative form; recognizable readability at the delivered scale. They are qualitative conclusions from the inspected corpus, not a universal frequency claim. Palette descriptions are estimated by visual inspection; exact hex colors below are authored application choices. Physical images cannot establish software timing, responsive breakpoints or interaction states.

Application proposal: Build an abstract pattern configurator with density, radial count and reserved-channel controls, plus saved samples. Sliders must visibly change the point construction. Decorative dots are hidden from assistive technology; controls use solid labels and focus rings.

### Sampling limits and contradictory examples

Corpus includes concentrated authorship and formal overlap. Dates, needles, session process and healed appearance are generally unknown. Whole compositions count once; the number of dots, panels, photographs or motifs is never the artifact count.

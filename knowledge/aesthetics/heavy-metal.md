### Coverage and stopping decision

The working target is met. Early Maiden images establish dramatic story, spatial depth and recurring identity. Matthews expands landscape and organic fantasy; Szaybo supplies a constructed-object photograph; Wilkinson supplies credited mechanical/portrait painting and a firsthand colour counterexample. Late batches reinforce bright graphic, photo and text-led variants. Additional stills would not resolve motion or every subgenre boundary.

The result is substantial for this scoped classic-record application, with explicit UK/European/US-market concentration. It does not claim all extreme metal, fashion, stage/video or worldwide independent traditions. Classification confidence is moderate for the broad genre name and high for the useful painted-world branch; historical motion and universal exact fonts remain unknown.

## Analysis

A developer using only blackletter, darkness and skulls would miss decisive evidence. The useful commonality is an authored band/record identity and deliberate dramatic hierarchy, not one mandatory subject. Painted fantasy is a strong chosen branch, while British Steel, Master of Reality, Vol.4 and Denim and Leather keep object, photographic and graphic alternatives visible.

Direct observations concern delivered image/type composition, surfaces, silhouette, relative scale and colour relationships. Source-supported claims concern creator testimony, named commissions and dates only where uncontradicted. Proposed/inferred values include exact application colours, chapter annotation geometry, native track-note disclosures, reading progress, responsive panels and feedback timing. Unknowns include every historical font, pigment/process, current-preview authorship and motion.

The showcase uses original fictional imagery and prose, no lyrics or borrowed mascots. An album companion is grounded in inspected band-site metadata/track disclosures, while interactive sleeve landmarks and reading-progress mechanics are a contemporary interpretation. A track duration does not turn a note into an audio player.

### Cultural and reuse review

Do not assign one masculine, heterosexual, white or national identity to all fans. Horror/confinement, religious quotation, political war imagery and heroic violence in individual sources are narrative choices, not required application ingredients or evidence of real beliefs. Original fictional worlds can preserve scale and theatrical light without copying protected characters or stigmatizing people.

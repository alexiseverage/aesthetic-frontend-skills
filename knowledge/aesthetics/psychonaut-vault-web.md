## Analysis

Psychonaut Vault Web is a descriptive interface-culture label for documentation-oriented psychedelic/consciousness reference networks, their personal gateways and graphic periphery. It is narrower in purpose than generic Early Internet and structurally different from a psychedelic poster: the information architecture, attribution and useful links remain the center. It is not a historical movement name, a medical endorsement or a claim that all referenced sites share one visual skin.

The most defensible non-negotiables are a navigable archive/taxonomy, explicit document identity or provenance, and a deliberate relationship between practical reading surfaces and an authored exploratory/visionary frame. Underlined links, dense indexes, compact native controls, grouped headings, update notices and author/source lines provide the documentary layer. Star fields, cosmic or botanical emblems, recursive contours and luminous framing provide an optional expressive layer. A generic page of neon swirls lacks this archival relationship. A purely generic encyclopedia without any authored exploratory identity is a neighboring reference system, not sufficient by itself.

PV01–15 establish interface relationships. Current wiki, bookstore, modern reference and botanical-publication variants do not prove that an early Erowid page had their typography or carousel. PV16–31 establish a broader community graphic vocabulary: reflective gold ribs, warm biological symmetry, dark purple portals, high-chroma branches, pale micro-worlds and repeated radial petals. These images are not UI templates. PV26 is excluded because generic whimsical suspended figures add little defining visual evidence. The corpus therefore supports a family of bounded translations, not a recipe requiring every motif on every surface.

**Proposed frontend translation:** build an original fictional Oneiric Field Archive for dreams, ambient observations and imagined places. Use a compact masthead with an abstract original orbital/branching emblem, a topic index rail, underlined text navigation, a small labeled query form and an archive table with title, observation type, date and editorial status. Selecting a row opens a real readable entry with provenance metadata and a linked contents list. A native-looking submission form can save a local fictional record; it must clearly expose validation and save state. None of the example records should borrow real experience reports, drug names, doses, medical claims or private stories.

A dark proposed route might use surround `#100D18`, reading plane `#211C2B`, ink `#F1EBDD`, pale violet link `#C9B1EF`, muted gold `#D6BC72` and rule `#776483`. These are modern design choices. The pale-route equivalent can use warm paper, dark ink and underlined plum links. Give functional text a stable opaque plane and test composited contrast; the poor blue-on-black source text in PV08 is an anti-pattern to correct. Use a readable serif for document titles/body and a compact sans for metadata/control labels. Reserve any pictorial or extruded treatment for the emblem or a small archive heading.

Express the aesthetic through the table rules, document tabs, index grouping, inset fields, provenance badges, selected rows and original ornamental divider shapes. An unrelated image above modern cards is insufficient. A small CSS/SVG recursive line emblem may be original, with no tracing of credited artworks, brand marks, sacred scripts or specific cultural symbols. On phones place a compact topic select above one reading column, reflow metadata and maintain real table/list semantics rather than shrinking a desktop scan. Keep navigation and form targets comfortably touchable.

The showcase can be intentionally still: immediate row selection, plain local-save feedback and a readable loading label preserve the archive grammar. If a short transition is added, its duration is proposed and removed under reduced motion. No strobing fractal field, compulsory animated wallpaper, autoplay audio, distorted functional text or attention-trapping progress sequence is justified by this corpus.

**Rights and context:** Erowid’s report-data restrictions are a concrete exclusion, not a prompt to scrape through mirrors. The inspected preserved topical/author indexes contain links to anecdotes and potentially unsafe claims; the aesthetic analysis does not test, endorse or republish those claims. Artist gallery license notices are not permission to copy/modify protected imagery into the website. Cultural motifs in community art cannot be treated as a grab bag of Indigenous or religious symbols. Use original abstract geometry and fictional observations, retain attribution in research, and avoid presenting community information as clinical authority. The source site’s intention, an author’s recollection, directly observed pixels and new frontend proposals remain separate evidence classes.

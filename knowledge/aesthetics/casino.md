## Analysis

Casino Neon Tableplay is application-ready because its strongest signal combines environmental spectacle with table-game objects. The load-bearing pattern is: neon-sign spectacle + table-game object system + felt/glow/gloss texture + layered venue spatial logic. Frontend translation should begin with either a sign/facade scene or a table/felt composition, then layer chips, cards, roulette geometry, marquee type, and controlled glow.

The category is not lotto. Casino may share gambling-domain language with lottery products, but it is room/table/signage-first and hospitality-oriented. Avoid scratch panels, barcode corners, serial blocks, latex/foil cover layers, state-lottery legal/product language, and ticket-like flat reveal grids; those belong to the sister `lotto` entry.

Recommended token direction: use dark stage grounds, felt green, red/black/gold gaming objects, neon magenta/cyan accents, theatrical display type, chip/card/roulette shapes, and subtle sign or table motion. Accessibility risk is mainly dark grounds plus glow effects; keep essential copy on solid felt plaques, cards, or panels and avoid mandatory blinking.

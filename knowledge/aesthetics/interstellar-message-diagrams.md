## Analysis

Comparing the Voyager cover and Pioneer plaque establishes that warm metal and fine radial registration are one branch of the grammar. The Arecibo sequence, Cosmic Call primer and Oliver pictogram provide different ways to turn discrete marks into a framed pattern. The latter two use repetition or corners to communicate layout. Publisher images of A Beacon in the Galaxy and the Last Arecibo Message use color to make section boundaries visible to human readers. The Altair explainer adds familiar signs to help explain less familiar notation. Heller and de Paulis deliberately test different assumptions about inference. The breadth of this corpus is constrained by the small number of independently published, legible visual messages. Searches for the Teen Age Message, Sónar Calling and Lone Signal yielded documentation, audio or partial fragments without a comparably attributable complete visual payload. Multiple pages or recolorings of one known transmission are grouped together.

# Makeup Face-Chart Studio

## Scope and classification

A useful descriptive branch of beauty working graphics: a printed/drawn face becomes a plan for pigment placement, accompanied by product recipes, swatches, zone names or a repeatable look record. It spans sparse practical worksheets and richly rendered chart art. It is distinct from a model comp card, clinical anatomy and a finished portrait with no chart-making context. The studio worksheet is the proposed interface translation, not a claim that every artwork contains form fields.

## Source / Evidence Links

- [Allure commissioned charts](https://www.allure.com/story/face-charts-by-makeup-artists): ten artists interpreted a custom Samantha Hahn face template; the feature documents backstage communication. One chart per commissioned look, not one per crop or product.
- [Juniper’s backstage report](https://www.junipersjournal.com/mac-face-charts-for-mifw/): photographed MAC working sheets from Malaysia International Fashion Week, published in 2012. Four distinct sheets, not the repeated overview photograph.
- [Liza Kondrevich: uses and workflow](https://facechart.art/blog/what-are-face-chart-used-for/): primary practitioner describes predesigning and recording looks for shoots, client discussion and education. Her promotional claims about origin and universal mastery are not adopted as established history.
- [Kondrevich: metallic chart experiment](https://facechart.art/blog/metallic-eyeliner-face-charts/): material contrast of pigment and foil; this is visual design evidence, not a safety recommendation for applying crafting adhesives to skin.
- [École Lancôme workbook](https://lancometraining.com/files/Ecole%20Workbook%20Makeup.pdf): primary brand training, selected printed pages 46–47 directly inspected.
- [NYX The EPIC template set](https://www.nyxcosmetics.com/on/demandware.static/-/Sites-nyxcosmetics-us-Library/en_US/pdf/NYX-MPU-FaceCharts-TheEPIC.pdf): two-page primary template PDF with visible Kondrevich credit, inspected in full and counted once.
- [Alicia Goveas beauty illustration portfolio](https://aliciagoveas.com/expertise/beauty-illustrations/): useful boundary comparisons; portrait/event illustrations were excluded from the chart corpus.

## Analysis

The high-confidence grammar is relational: face/feature contour, localized pigment, paper workspace and a mechanism for communicating the intended look. Backstage sheets and the Luca Luca recipe establish the practical form; Lancôme supports the educational template/workbook relationship; NYX supports varied face angles and hair. Allure’s commissioned works show that a shared base can accommodate sharply different artist choices without a single mandatory palette. The Halloween sheets add a structured recipe format with numbered modules and detail views. These are direct visual observations, with practical planning context supported by the artist and brand publications.

Richly rendered chart art complicates a rule that every face must be a flat black outline. Kondrevich’s drawn gloss, hair and skin can approach portrait illustration; retaining an explicit chart-making or planning context matters at that boundary. Conversely, the sparse MAC red-lip chart does not need photorealistic skin. The interface should retain blank space and meaningful product/zone information, not simulate a physical face’s response to cosmetics. None of these templates establishes a universally appropriate face shape, gender, skin tone or ethnicity.

A web look planner with selectable eye/cheek/lip zones, named pigment swatches, finish choices, editable look notes and recipe disclosures is a contemporary application inference. Static sources do not establish live blending physics, timing, breakpoints or saved-state behavior. Proposed colors, CSS strokes and measured interface contrast belong to the application brief. Intentional stillness suits reading and comparison; no flashing glitter or automatic repaint is warranted. Any cosmetic performance, ingredient suitability or skin-safety claim would require separate evidence and is outside this visual entry.

Self-review checked chart-versus-portrait boundaries, the ten artist/template credits, repeated crops, small-original limitations, practical/artistic exceptions and rights. No independent review is claimed. History of invention remains unknown; an uncorroborated practitioner 1980s claim is not promoted into a canonical origin date.

## Connections

Model Comp Cards share portrait/metadata organization but document a person’s casting information. Anatomical Flap Atlases share diagram zones but communicate anatomical structures rather than cosmetic intent. Photographic Contact Sheet shares labeled working-document repetition; face-chart studio uses one dominant plan and recipe instead of a grid of captured moments.

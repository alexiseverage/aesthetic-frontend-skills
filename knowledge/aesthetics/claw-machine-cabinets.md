# Claw Machine Cabinets

## Scope and classification

Physical prize-crane cabinets whose large transparent playable chamber sits between an identifying marquee and a projecting control deck, with payment and retrieval lower down. The grouping includes dark illuminated frames, restrained white cabinets, small candy machines and large multi-player configurations. Neon or a claw illustration alone does not establish the style.

## Source / Evidence Links

[ELAUT cranes](https://www.elaut-group.com/); [Smart Industries](https://www.smartind.com/); [Coast to Coast equipment](https://coasttocoastcranes.com/equipment/); [Sega UFO Catcher9](https://am-show.sega.jp/jaepo15/lineup_ufo9.html); [Taito Capriccio Box flyer](https://www.taito.co.jp/Content/Media/pdf/am14_CAPRICCO_BOX_202004_A4.pdf).

## Dimension Synthesis

**Palette**: Black housings with colored illuminated rails and pale white/cyan/pink cabinets are both well represented. Candy and carnival variants add purple, yellow and red. Illumination outlines functional regions rather than requiring an all-over rainbow.

**Type**: Bold sans, rounded display and theme-specific script marquees sit above plain instructional or numeric displays. Control and retrieval labels remain subordinate but legible. No single cabinet font is established.

**Texture**: Glossy painted or molded housing, transparent glazing, metallic fittings and luminous strips. Use modest reflections and edge highlights. Source studio reflections and promotional backgrounds do not belong to the hardware itself.

**Shape**: Rectangular clear chamber, hanging claw and rail, separate projecting joystick/button pod, and low retrieval opening. Round, oval and teardrop hatches coexist with square doors; some double and four-player housings repeat compartments.

**Motion**: This corpus inspects still manufacturer imagery and one flyer. It establishes visible mechanisms, not operating timing, probability or claw strength. User-controlled browser position feedback and a deterministic demonstration are contemporary proposals; no perpetual flashing is required.

**Spatial**: A dominant central play window, a top title and a lower control/retrieval plinth. Large multi-player machines repeat this relationship. A low table-like Capriccio Box is a documented boundary variant with a reduced marquee.

**Cultural markers**: Commercial amusement hardware from ELAUT, Smart, Coast to Coast, Sega and Taito/Alta records. ELAUT and Coast share corporate ownership, so brand count is not independent ownership count. Taito exhibition previews are development imagery, not evidence of installed operation.

Confidence is high for chamber/control/retrieval relationships and moderate for palette/material generalization. Exact fonts, emitted colors, dimensions, operational timing and mechanical chance are unknown. No historical numeric token is inferred.

## Direct Media Audit

42 complete media versions were individually inspected; 33 unique qualifying cabinet artifacts remain after eight conservatively grouped family duplicates and one low-detail boundary exclusion. Different widths, graphics and alternate angles do not inflate cabinet-family counts. Multi-player installations count once. The 10 floor and 30 target are met. ELAUT/Coast ownership concentration and North American product-marketing source bias remain explicit; Sega and Taito add Japanese cabinet alternatives. Images labeled 2026 development previews are not observed operating installations. The Capriccio Box PDF is one flyer/cabinet, not a separate artifact for each illustration.

Reference media are private analytical copies; publication uses observations and links only. Studio reflections, promotional backdrops and flyer ornament are distinguished from hardware. Still evidence does not establish light cycles, claw motion or win rates.

## Analysis

A deterministic object picker can use a clear display chamber, named target choices, a range or directional control and a large action button. Show the selected object in a lower collection hatch after confirmation. Keep reset and keyboard controls visible. Status, credit/demo counters and instructions belong in the control deck, not floating over the objects.

Use a framed grid with marquee, large central chamber, beveled projecting deck and solid lower plinth. Original SVG gantry/claw and collectible shapes can supply the mechanism. Proposed tokens: dark frame #171e31, cyan #53dce4, pink #ff70af, light label #f6f7ef, thin edge glow and a 12px cabinet radius. These are design choices, not measured cabinet colors. Disable decorative transforms and glow pulses under reduced motion.

Use original objects, a fictional cabinet name and a transparent deterministic demo. Do not imply real prize value, measured win probability or authentic operating physics from still images. Preserve accessible direct selection alongside directional controls.

The restrained Sega cabinet and low Capriccio table contradict a universal black-RGB tower. Candy House and oversized carnival housings expand silhouette without displacing the control relationship. Repeated XL and Deluxe families are grouped despite altered themes. Generic LED gaming dashboards without a chamber are outside scope.

## Connections

Capsule walls repeat compact vending cells. Cute Arcade Puzzle Boards organize a flat tile playfield, while Vector Arcade Graphics centers luminous line rendering. Skeuomorphism describes the broader translation of physical controls, not this particular chamber/deck/hatch system.

# Lava Flow Cartography

## Dimension Synthesis

| Dimension | Observed system and variation | Confidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Palette | Warm observed extents recur alongside gray relief or pale land. Dated red ramps, a flat red overlay and translucent multi-color zones all occur. Some models use a cool-to-warm sequence with separate values. | High for relative contrast |
| Type | Sheet titles, coordinates, dates, flow and vent names, scale and categories are distinct. Some field labels use white backing. | High |
| Texture | Contours, relief and point stipple establish context. Flow overlap can have a hatched treatment. Satellite plates retain photographic terrain. | High |
| Shape | Extent polygons branch into narrow lobes. The vent can be a point or a line, with thinner outlines for infrastructure and faults. A few maps group whole coverage regions. | High |
| Motion | The audited maps are static, including timed snapshots and multiyear comparisons. Map state change is a digital translation. | High for the evidence boundary |
| Spatial | Locator insets, white legend blocks and separate scale placement provide orientation. Both full-sheet and regional crops appear, sometimes as coordinated small multiples. | High |
| Cultural markers | Chronology, credits, place names, survey times and observational versus modeled terms are substantive. | High in sampled institutional sources |

## Direct Media Audit

This corpus contains 31 qualifying maps and independently composed figures. The 10 USGS atlas sheets are separate authored views with distinct titles, region coverage and legends, including one islandwide index. The Réunion series contributes separately composed figures representing distinct visual arguments. Subpanels and revisions of the same cumulative Icelandic sheet were grouped. The La Palma release is one dated sheet from a longer series. Eleven qualifying PDF sheets were rendered temporarily for inspection. A fetched USGS Kīlauea summary and a small Copernicus vulnerability inset were outside the qualifying map sample. None of the source media is embedded in the showcase.

## Analysis

The stable distinction is between the visual background and the changing geometry assigned to lava. Basemap contours, coastline and roads provide consistent location. Flow extents vary from bright red single-layer silhouettes to softly translucent region maps and timestamped fringes. Legend terms and labeled samples distinguish coverage from classified probabilities, which call for their own semantics. An islandwide inset often prevents a zoomed local map from losing its anchor. Selection can keep the basemap stable while changing the visible depth of the dated record.

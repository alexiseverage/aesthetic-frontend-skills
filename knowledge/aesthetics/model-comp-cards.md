# Model Comp Cards

## Source / Evidence Links

- [Basic Models, What Is a Comp Card](https://basicmodels.com.sg/model-comp-card/) — Agency co-founder Alex Lee explains the portrait, selected range, data, representation and updating function in 2019. Its 4–6 photo recommendation is a local practice, not a universal rule.
- [HK Talents, Digital Comp Cards](https://www.hktalents-models.com/compcards) — Agency explains professional use and supplies the complete Cherry.Y card. Roster portraits and unfinished category pages are not additional comp cards.
- [NEVS, FW23 Show Pack](https://www.nevsmodels.co.uk/pdfs/NEVS%20FW23%20SHOW%20PACK.pdf) — Primary agency publication contains individually named two-photo cards. Thirteen selected works are inspected separately; the pack cover and uninspected pages add no count.
- [Stage Models, Juna Bauer and agency PDF cards](https://stage-models.com/juna-bauer/) — Individual agency pages link complete named one-page PDFs; twelve distinct composed cards are inspected. Embedded magazine images do not establish permission to reuse their content.
- [Bella Agency, commercial/lifestyle print cards](https://www.bellaagency.com/ny/lifestyle/men/) — Three published print-card PDFs broaden portrait/lifestyle and commercial presentation. Temporary signed portfolio-image URLs are neither required for the count nor published.
- [ModelManagement, Model Academy chapter 2](https://www.modelmanagement.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/Capitulo-2-Model-Academy_compressed.pdf) — Practitioner chapter describes concise photographic selection and casting use. Text was accessible through the web index; shell retrieval was blocked, so its example is not a qualifying visual artifact.

## Dimension Synthesis

The following separates direct visual observations from proposed web translation. Color descriptions and relative geometry are estimated from visual inspection; no corpus-wide numerical sampling or measured animation is claimed.

| Dimension | Synthesis / scope | Confidence and limitation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Palette | White, black and pale neutral framing commonly let photographs supply color. Entirely monochrome cards, black-backed supporting montages and pale identity bands are substantial variants. Bright studio backgrounds or clothing can dominate individual pictures. A restrained frame is common; desaturating every photograph or imposing a luxury beige wash is unnecessary. | High for the inspected visual sample |
| Type | The professional name anchors the card, with smaller labeled statistics and representation/contact details. Plain sans-serif names, larger height numbers, widely spaced agency lettering and marker-like names all occur. Some magazine mastheads are embedded in portfolio images rather than used as card headings. A few PDFs expose actual font metadata, but no one typeface defines the format. | High for observed hierarchy and treatment; selected PDF families verified through metadata, raster lettering unverified |
| Texture | Photographic light, grain, skin, fabric and location detail carry most material interest. Quiet flat backing and clean gutters keep those differences legible. Some show-pack identity bands imitate handwriting or translucent overlays; these are variants. Artificial paper distress, cut-film sprockets or UI noise should not compete with the work. | High for the inspected visual sample |
| Shape | A large portrait or main image commonly opposes several smaller rectangles. Two-picture spreads, asymmetric grids, wide supporting images and narrow information rails offer alternatives. Compact name/stat blocks and agency marks are compositional anchors. Preserve meaningful picture crops; the face, pose or garment should not be accidentally truncated to fill identical rounded cards. | High for the inspected visual sample |
| Motion | The evidence is still composed cards and PDF pages. Editorial poses imply no card animation. A modern card viewer may switch model, side or selected photograph immediately; intentional stillness supports comparison. An automatic slideshow, spinning card or moving statistics is not inherent to the format. | Unknown historical behavior; still composition directly observed |
| Spatial | Main portrait, supporting range and identity/data form distinct but related tiers. Information can sit in a side rail, lower white band, black montage panel or carefully chosen image space. Large/small photo contrast matters more than a fixed image count. Supporting pictures can repeat a main photograph within one card; they do not become independent cards. | High for the inspected visual sample |
| Cultural markers | Agency representation, casting, a current professional name, selected work, styling range and practical garment-fit data locate the format. Commercial/lifestyle and fashion-editorial cards differ. Measurements are reported casting information, not a definition of attractiveness, health or human worth. Agency-specific sizing and field conventions should remain explicit. | Medium; supporting context and variable examples |

### Translation and remaining uncertainty

The defining palette/material, shape and spatial relationships have strong direct support within this bounded sample. Type and motion must retain their stated limitations. Proposed colors, web spacing, breakpoints, animation duration and controls in the canonical entry are design decisions, not measurements recovered from static imagery. No font family is identified without metadata. Cultural narratives beyond the linked source descriptions remain outside the claim scope.

Review method: single-agent comparison of individual originals, repeated-card and underlying-work grouping, source-caption checks and mechanical ledger audit. This is not independent review or an exhaustive historical survey.

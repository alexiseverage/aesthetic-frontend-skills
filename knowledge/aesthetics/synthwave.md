## Analysis

Synthwave is a revival aesthetic built from 1980s sonic and cinematic memory rather than a direct historical 1980s design movement. The accessible sources consistently frame it around retrofuturist nostalgia, outrun iconography, and a sincere rather than ironic attachment to neon action-poster cool.

Representative visual evidence is horizon-driven: low sun, grid road, angular chrome type, and night-driving motion. The style depends on cinematic dark-mode energy; without depth, speed, and high-contrast neon, the result drifts into generic retro color grading.

Ethical and usage note: synthwave can be overgeneralized as 'all 80s neon.' Keep its revival status and its adjacency to music, game, and poster culture visible instead of treating it as a catch-all for any magenta/cyan palette.

## Connections

- `vaporwave` — both are retro-digital, but synthwave is earnest, active, and cinematic where vaporwave is ironic, static, and consumer-archaeological.
- `city-pop` — city-pop shares 1980s nostalgia but stays daylight, leisurely, and Japanese-leisure-specific.
- `cyberpunk` — both can be neon, but cyberpunk adds grime, surveillance, and social coercion instead of sleek retro romance.

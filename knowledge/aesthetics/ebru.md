# Ebru — research profile

## Classification and Context

**Decision:** separately useful material/craft aesthetic: the living Turkish marbling tradition, not a synonym for all marbled paper or generic digital fluid graphics. Its multiple named branches share pigment arrangement on a prepared water surface and transfer, but have different spatial and formal organization. The selected corpus supports a practical pattern-and-paper translation while retaining cultural specificity.

**Source-supported:** [UNESCO's 2014 inscription decision](https://ich.unesco.org/en/decisions/9.COM/10.44) identifies Ebru as a Turkish tradition, emphasizes practical transmission and social relationships, and describes pattern transfer from a prepared liquid surface. This establishes cultural context rather than a universal screen palette or motion specification.

**Source-supported:** [Türkiye's culture portal](https://kulturportali.gov.tr/portal/ebrusanati) describes book, calligraphic and independent-art uses and acknowledges uncertainty about early chronology. Its media records credit specific makers and name techniques for contemporary competition works. A title such as “Ethem Efendi Battalı” is a technique/reference name in these records, not the identity of the contemporary maker.

**Source-supported:** [Galatart](https://galatart.com/ebrular_english.html) supplies studio variants including sandy marbling, flowers and illustrative hybrids. [Ebru Koçak](https://www.ebruwithebru.com/history-of-ebru) credits historical reproductions and publications. Some early chronology on that practitioner page is insufficiently corroborated and is not adopted as fact. [The Met](https://store.metmuseum.org/blog/marbled-paper-makes-a-comeback) supplies book-art context and distinguishes Suminagashi and other marbling traditions. No single-origin story is inferred from this set.

## Direct Media Audit

- 33 media versions individually opened and visually inspected at their full served dimensions; 30 qualifying unique still works/artifacts.
- Two studio-wall overview photographs, EB01 and EB20, are excluded as contextual surveys and are not split into all the works hanging on the wall. Glare, occlusion and small detail limit their evidentiary role.
- EB05 is excluded as an unresolved hybrid: the dense circular marks and mosque silhouette cannot establish an Ebru technique without further attribution.
- EB22 contains three photographed views of one forming pattern/event and counts once. EB32 combines multiple ornamental sections and one calligraphic panel and counts once. No views, motifs, crops or panels are inflated into additional works.
- Qualifying collection distribution: culture portal 17; Galatart 9; Ebru Koçak's credited historical reproductions 4. These are three useful collections, with a majority from one contemporary state competition and a substantial studio-selection bias. Four historic reproductions are not four independent archives.
- Discovery included the studio's 12 gallery slots, the ministry's 21-result competition metadata search plus a separately linked floral work, and the practitioner's four historical references. Related thumbnails and metadata-only works were not counted. Additional pages at Ebristan, KB and decorated-paper sources supplied leads rather than qualifying media.
- Working target 30 and absolute floor 10 met. Source images were linked and viewed; no reference pixels downloaded, generated or republished. Custom application graphics are separate and count zero.
- Source access: the UNESCO element page returned an anti-bot form; the accessible inscription decision supplied context without solving the challenge. Web image fetching timed out for ministry originals, but the same linked originals rendered through the research browser. One large ministry image completed after navigation timeout and was inspected only after a fresh state/screenshot confirmed it. Wix's small transformed previews were replaced by the original media resource identified in the same supplied URLs. KB and decorated-paper routes did not yield useful analyzed originals in this run.
- Full served image dimensions are recorded below. Some works are photographed obliquely or through mounts; source reproduction size, color balance and age are limitations. EB18 is monochrome and does not support original hue claims; EB26 is sufficient for motif/repeat but not fine calibrated texture.
- Stop reason: later batches confirmed separated pigment and technique-specific organization while broadening palette, scale, all-over versus focal composition, historical versus contemporary practice, sandy texture, meaningful calligraphy and illustrative hybrids. Textile/ceramic applications, full workshop behavior and exact chemistry remain gaps. The still-artifact scope is not inherently motion-dependent, and no motion timing is claimed.

### EB01 — Studio display photograph

**Excluded**. Creator/credit: Galatart studio collection; individual maker and date not stated. [Source](https://galatart.com/ebrular_english.html); [inspected media](https://galatart.com/images/ebrular/Picture1.jpg). Inspected 1125 x 633; full-size.

### EB06 — Illustrated skyline on ochre marbled ground

**Qualifying**. Creator/credit: Galatart studio collection; individual maker and date not securely established. [Source](https://galatart.com/ebrular_english.html); [inspected media](https://galatart.com/images/ebrular/Picture24.jpg). Inspected 1006 x 648; full-size.

### EB07 — Siyah Beyaz Karanfil, 2015

**Qualifying**. Creator/credit: Elif Aydın. [Source](https://kulturportali.gov.tr/medya/fotograf/fotodokuman/2494/siyah-beyaz-karanfil); [inspected media](https://kulturportali.gov.tr/repoKulturPortali/Dokumanlar/aydnelifcopy_20151231092626303.jpg). Inspected 790 x 1008; full-size.

The border contains denser red, blue-gray, cream and green cellular patterns, contrasting with the airy floral center. ### EB08 — Ebru — Şal Ebrusu, 2015

### EB09 — Ebru — Taraklı Ebru, 2015

**Qualifying**. Creator/credit: Nuray Metin. [Source](https://kulturportali.gov.tr/medya/fotograf/fotodokuman/2574/ebru); [inspected media](https://kulturportali.gov.tr/repoKulturPortali/Dokumanlar/metinnuray2_20151230103409306.jpg). Inspected 620 x 936; full-size.

Narrow navy, sky-blue, magenta and cream lines repeat in comb-like order, while broader waves gently distort the columns. The pattern retains sharp boundaries between pigment bands rather than merging them into a smooth gradient; a regular tool rhythm coexists with local irregularity.

### EB10 — Gelenekli Türk Ebrusu — Ethem Efendi Battalı, 2015

**Qualifying**. Creator/credit: Hayrunisa Ocak. [Source](https://kulturportali.gov.tr/medya/fotograf/fotodokuman/2575/gelenekli-turk-ebrusu); [inspected media](https://kulturportali.gov.tr/repoKulturPortali/Dokumanlar/ocakhayrnisacopy_20151230103638159.jpg). Inspected 934 x 644; full-size.

### EB11 — Barış, 2015

**Qualifying**. Creator/credit: Asuman Samur. [Source](https://kulturportali.gov.tr/medya/fotograf/fotodokuman/2576/baris); [inspected media](https://kulturportali.gov.tr/repoKulturPortali/Dokumanlar/samurasumancopy_20151230103849671.jpg). Inspected 784 x 588; full-size.

### EB12 — Su Altındaki Gizler — Gelgit Üzeri Neft Serpme, 2015

**Qualifying**. Creator/credit: Vuslat Tunç Kurt. [Source](https://kulturportali.gov.tr/medya/fotograf/fotodokuman/2573/su-altindaki-gizler); [inspected media](https://kulturportali.gov.tr/repoKulturPortali/Dokumanlar/kurttunvuslatcopy_20151230103133352.jpg). Inspected 806 x 1198; full-size.

### EB13 — Mavi Huzur — Tarz-ı Kadim Battal Ebrusu, 2015

### EB15 — Ethem Efendi Battalı, 2015

### EB17 — Bridge illustration on a marbled field

The composition is dense and flat, with high color separation and no soft gradient; it demonstrates the named Şal branch at a broader gesture scale than EB08.

## Analysis

**Observed:** cells preserve visible boundaries, fine droplets and local density differences; drawn-out bands and combed arcs preserve distinct tool-like organization. Floral and Hatip forms can sit against quiet or fully marbled grounds. Some contemporary pieces add outlined illustrations, and meaningful calligraphic work can combine marbled letters, dense borders and a calmer central ground. These are branches of a broader paper practice, not instructions to combine every motif.

**Source-supported:** named techniques, maker credits, contemporary dates and cultural context come from the linked records. Photographs alone do not prove exact recipes, the order of every gesture, or the physical media of later illustrative additions. Historical-image attribution is retained as source credit rather than independently verified dating.

**Inference for application:** a contemporary workbench can foreground pattern organization, show an explicitly digital interpretation of pigment placement, and let a visitor retain a still sheet. Controls, precise colors, type scale, responsive layout, gesture feedback and any motion are proposed design values. A deterministic procedural graphic is an application asset, never historic evidence or proof of chemical simulation accuracy.

**Boundary:** soft gradient blobs alone omit pigment separation and craft context; Psychedelic shares curves without sharing the making tradition. Cyanotype and Risograph are different sheet-production systems. Suminagashi and other regional marbling practices should not be renamed Ebru merely because ink or pigment floats. No unsupported claim that one tradition invented all marbling is made.

**Limitations:** source selection is paper- and exhibition-focused, with 17 works from one state competition, nine studio examples and four historical reproductions. Workshop motion, textile and ceramic applications, exact chemistry, calibrated color and extensive maker interviews remain outside this visual corpus. EB05 remains excluded; its ambiguity does not justify redefining all circular decorative work as Ebru.

## Connections

- `cyanotype`: shared process-to-sheet premise, different physical image formation.
- `risograph`: shared material awareness, different ink/stencil production.
- `organic-digital`: fluid appearance can overlap, but soft-tech gradients do not carry this craft grammar.
- `psychedelic`: saturated curves and repeated optical forms overlap without defining the same cultural or material system.
- `art-nouveau`: botanical tapering can look adjacent, but Ebru is not a generic lithographic floral-ornament style.

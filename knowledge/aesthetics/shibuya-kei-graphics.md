# Shibuya-kei Graphics

Classification: **separately useful canonical visual system**, related to the neighbors below. A scene-related graphic lens around selected Shibuya-kei record design: editorial sampling of mod, lounge, modernist, pop and retro-futurist references, recomposed through precise typography and packaging. The musical genre is broader than one visual style; neither every Japanese sleeve nor every release by a relevant musician qualifies.

# Puffy Sticker Albums

The material and arrangement language of raised sticker pieces on sheets and reusable album pages: padded silhouettes, separate carrier surfaces, themed collections and peel/place logic. This is a focused material format, not a synonym for all cute illustration, flat sticker collage or sports trading albums.

## Source / Evidence Links

- [Pipsticks puffy sheets](https://www.pipsticks.com/collections/puffy-stickers): Complete manufacturer-supplied product images; individual sheet designs counted once.
- [Melissa & Doug Pet Place](https://www.melissaanddoug.com/products/puffy-sticker-activity-book-pet-place): Reusable puffy pieces, scene pages, spiral binding and carrying format.
- [Gordy Puffy Stickers and Album Set](https://thundercats.org/toys/gordy-puffy-stickers/): Documented 1985 package photograph and coated-page collector context; not proof of invention.

## Analysis

**Palette**: Broadly varied: pastel turquoise, pink and lilac, saturated rainbow bands, warm animals, black/red historical packaging. White or pale substrate rims are common. All UI palette values are visual estimates.

**Type**: Sheet headers and album tabs organize a collection; sticker text may be part of an individual motif. Use rounded readable lettering for controls and ordinary text for metadata. A maker logo is not a generic font recipe.

**Texture**: Raised padded edges, small highlights and shallow edge shadows distinguish pieces from their carrier. Glitter and gradients occur as variants. Some manufacturer pictures are renderings and cannot measure actual thickness.

**Shape**: Independent die-cut silhouettes, often with a narrow pale rim; rectangular character-backed pieces also occur. Internal artwork may be angular, outlined, minimally outlined or softly shaded.

**Motion**: No peeling animation or physical speed measured. Proposed UI uses a brief 140 ms press/placement response with a static reduced-motion alternative; avoid perpetual wobbling.

**Spatial**: A sheet groups related objects with tiny fillers and larger anchors; an album separates background scenes, stored pieces, page divisions and a binding edge. Decorative crowding must leave readable interactive zones.

**Cultural markers**: Collecting, swapping and reusable scene making across historical licensed products and current stationery/toys. Current samples lean toward US commercial collections; no single inventor or universal 1980s origin is asserted.

The useful distinction is between a carrier and independent padded pieces. A generic rounded rectangle with an illustration misses that relationship. The sampled current sheets often use cute motifs, but angular crystals, mechanical objects, a signing hand and historical character-backed rectangles broaden the boundary. Manufacturer reusability claims support proposed album interactions; still images do not establish friction, animation or actual reuse durability.

## Connections

Kidcore can overlap in bright collecting culture but is broader than this material format. Cute character drawing may overlap with kawaii traditions without requiring a raised adhesive substrate. Puffy Sticker Albums is a distinct material-format translation.

# Groovival

Classification: **separately useful canonical visual system**, related to the neighbors below. A retrospective term for the commercial return of 1960s/1970s flower-power signs in late-1980s through early-2000s music, fashion, retail, packaging and digital products. Revival context matters: it often edits the older vocabulary into cheerful, accessible merchandising rather than a dense original psychedelic poster.

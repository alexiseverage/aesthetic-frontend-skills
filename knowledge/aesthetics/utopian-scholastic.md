# Utopian Scholastic

Classification: **separately useful canonical visual system**, related to the neighbors below. A retrospective cluster of idealized encyclopedic collage: carefully isolated objects, natural-history illustrations and technological specimens are organized by classical typography, labels and editorial rules. Scholastic here means a learned reference atmosphere, not the publisher’s book fairs or every children’s science package.

## Analysis

The Newgrange interpretive panel pairs a cutaway and annotated raised view. The separately sourced Stout and Ray figures prioritize sectional and survey geometry. Prendergast’s azimuth and altitude plot explicitly separates an ancient model from the orthorectified modern floor image. For Maeshowe the Reijs map locates horizon marks around Ward Hill while Scott’s figure illustrates the implications of alternate passage geometry. The Historic Environment Scotland statement describes modern midwinter observations and the care needed to read the monument’s history. Fajada field photos and simulations share dates and framing. Darvill’s seasonal calendar diagram is a worked interpretation of Stonehenge geometry. Drawings, orthophotos and simulations contribute different evidence. Copies of the OPW panel crop and time-series samples are grouped. Accessible references are concentrated around a small number of well-documented sites and several accounts repeat the same underlying survey or interpretive plan.

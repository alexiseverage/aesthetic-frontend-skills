## Analysis

Apple-core Tech is application-ready because its signal is distributed across the seven-dimension framework rather than resting on palette alone. The most important implementation guardrail is to preserve the combined palette, material, shape, motion, spatial, and cultural-marker system; if only one cue is present, the result will read as generic speculative technology.

For frontend translation, begin with the spatial and shape grammar, then layer palette, typography, texture, and motion. Keep critical content on solid readable surfaces when glow, grain, scanlines, particle fields, or dense machine texture would reduce accessibility.

## Connections

Related to warm-minimalism through restraint, but cooler and more embodied; related to Bauhaus clarity, but less exposed/geometric.

### Application decision

Retain identity and relationships, replace filler and false material/motion universals. Contemporary fictional product quotation: original light dial, restrained off-white/graphite enclosure, quiet hierarchy, intentional status color and low-clutter room control. Read/Focus/Unwind change the lamp state, brightness drives the drawn dial, and the pausable focus timer performs a real local workflow. All fonts, hex values,270-degree dial, timing and15/25/45-minute choices are proposed application values. No robot/person photograph, Apple logo, camera/microphone access or claim of actual home-device control is needed. Source-motion and30-work research targets remain open.

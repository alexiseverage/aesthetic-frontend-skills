## Analysis

Skate video VHS / fisheye title cards is a capture-and-title aesthetic defined by its camera, lens, and tape materiality. The Sony VX1000 MiniDV camera paired with the Century Optics MK1 fisheye lens is skateboarding's defining capture system, producing the signature wide-angle barrel distortion and close-up framing. VHS tape adds grain, noise, and tracking artifacts, and the title-card tradition adds hand-drawn or animated display lettering between clips.

The aesthetic is a coherent motion-and-title system: fisheye action footage, VHS materiality, and bold title-card typography. The motion character — in-your-face editing, quick cuts, fisheye tracking shots — is central to the aesthetic but is inferred from static/frame evidence; actual timing must be inspected from motion artifacts before canonization.

For frontend translation, the useful rules are: VHS color cast and grain, fisheye distortion, bold hand-drawn title typography, and a title-card framing convention. Motion claims should be treated as design recommendations until motion artifacts are inspected.

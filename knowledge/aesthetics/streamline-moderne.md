## Historical / Origin Notes

Streamline Moderne emerged in the 1930s as a late Art Deco style inspired by aerodynamic design, emphasizing curving forms, long horizontal lines, and nautical or transport references across architecture and industrial design.

## Notable practitioners, artifacts, regions, or institutions

- Ocean liner imagery, rail and bus design, domestic appliances, and commercial architecture are the core references.

## Analysis

Streamline Moderne turns modernity into smooth motion: curving shells, horizontal speed cues, and polished machine optimism make function look aerodynamic and approachable.

Non-negotiables for implementation: aerodynamic contour; horizontal speed lines; polished machine surfaces; transport-era optimism.

Cultural sensitivity assessment: Low identity-risk but historically specific. The style belongs to 1930s industrial optimism and consumer modernization, not timeless luxury futurism.

## Connections

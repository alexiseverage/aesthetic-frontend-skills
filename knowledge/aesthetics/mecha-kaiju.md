## Analysis

Mecha / Kaiju is application-ready because its signal is distributed across the seven-dimension framework rather than resting on palette alone. The most important implementation guardrail is to preserve the combined palette, material, shape, motion, spatial, and cultural-marker system; if only one cue is present, the result will read as generic speculative technology.

For frontend translation, begin with the spatial and shape grammar, then layer palette, typography, texture, and motion. Keep critical content on solid readable surfaces when glow, grain, scanlines, particle fields, or dense machine texture would reduce accessibility.

## Connections

Related to anime-mecha-realism, but monster-scale and spectacle-first; related to brutalism through mass but requires kaiju/mecha semantics.

### 2026-11-12 — Direct-media correction and application study

Inspected 33 distinct artifacts: 30 qualifying (27 stills and three played trailers), with three exclusions. Official TOHO film publicity supplies 17 qualifying posters, official SUNRISE/Bandai Namco series publicity supplies ten key visuals, and three separately developed games supply motion evidence. This is a deliberately concentrated Japanese franchise/publicity sample, not a statistically representative survey of all mecha, kaiju, countries, independent creators or production eras. Poster reproduction dates are often unspecified: film release dates identify the associated film, not a proven date of the current printed/reproduced sheet. No protected characters, logos or source media enter the proposed interface.

Primary context: [official Godzilla film catalog](https://godzilla.com/blogs/movies), [Criterion's Godzilla record](https://www.criterion.com/films/27755-godzilla), [official Gundam series introduction](https://en.gundam-official.com/what-is-gundam), and the publisher/developer Steam pages linked below. Godzilla's 1954 Japanese nuclear-anxiety context and Gundam's 1979 beginning are source-supported anchors, not evidence that the two genre histories begin simultaneously. Mecha centers constructed mechanisms; kaiju centers giant strange creatures. Their overlaps include mechanical monsters, but neither term is a synonym for the other.

# Postal Savings Stamp Cards

## Dimension Synthesis

| Dimension | Observed system and variation | Confidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Palette | Single blue, green or black inks on buff are common. Prominent reds and blues appear together in government and privately printed war savings issues. Some paper colors differentiate denominations. | High |
| Type | Stacked formal capitals, program and face values, compact instructions, and plainly set numbered slots establish the hierarchy. Engraved or drawn script accentuates titles and slogans. | High |
| Texture | Fine borders, screen marks, perforation and stamped ink document both printing and use. Plain form card stock also occurs. | High |
| Shape | Repeated square or rectangular stamp fields are framed by folds, margins, tabs and cancellation areas. Spacer fields, slogans and applications can interrupt the grid. | High |
| Motion | Historical paper forms are static. Accumulation is visible across blank and completed states. | High |
| Spatial | A front title and terms can pair with a wide inner grid, or a front grid can share the printed face with a cancellation and ownership area. | High |
| Cultural markers | Dates, issue marks, prices, issuer names, and cancellation stamps preserve the program context. The sampled wartime issues use distinct and sometimes forceful campaign imagery. | High for sampled issues |

## Direct Media Audit

Thirty distinct program layouts qualify. Twenty-three were inspected in readable facsimiles from Charles Blount's symposium paper. Seven came from Canadian, Australian, Malayan and U.S. collection sources. Reprints with the same underlying grid and cover system, denomination and color variants, cancellation states, and additional sides were grouped. The 1941 Postal Museum scan corroborates the PS 333 series entry. A University of North Texas cover and grid also corroborate the vertical Eagle with Flag series. Incomplete or low-resolution objects and non-stamp savings forms were excluded. Images and PDFs were temporarily retrieved for inspection and remain link-only references.

## Analysis

The same structural device serves many schemes. A fixed stamp footprint turns progress into a count the reader can see and touch. The institution places other information around it, including denomination, redemption, cancellation, and ownership. Unfilled cells can be numbered, labeled with cumulative amounts, or filled with pictures or slogans that disappear beneath the stamp. Across all sampled issuers, title art and official identity sit alongside rules that make the repeated action unambiguous.

## Earlier Analysis (2026-07-07)

Cyberpunk is application-ready because its signal is distributed across the seven-dimension framework rather than resting on palette alone. The most important implementation guardrail is to preserve the combined palette, material, shape, motion, spatial, and cultural-marker system; if only one cue is present, the result will read as generic speculative technology.

For frontend translation, begin with the spatial and shape grammar, then layer palette, typography, texture, and motion. Keep critical content on solid readable surfaces when glow, grain, scanlines, particle fields, or dense machine texture would reduce accessibility.

## Connections

Related to synthwave/vaporwave through neon, but dirtier, denser, and more socially coercive. Related to techno-noir; distinct from cassette-futurism's tactile analog machinery.

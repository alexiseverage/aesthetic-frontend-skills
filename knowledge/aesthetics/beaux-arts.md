## Historical / Origin Notes

Developed from the French École des Beaux-Arts academic system and spread internationally through civic, cultural, and infrastructural commissions in the nineteenth and early twentieth centuries.

## Analysis

Academic monumental classicism: axial plans, ceremonial hierarchy, sculptural ornament, masonry mass, and civic grandeur organized through rigorous composition rather than free historical collage.

Non-negotiables for implementation: axial ceremonial composition; masonry/relief materiality; classical ornament integrated with structure; institutional scale and hierarchy.

Cultural sensitivity assessment: Beaux-Arts is low identity-risk but tied to academic gatekeeping, class-coded institutions, and colonial-era civic monumentality. Preserve the architectural training and civic context rather than treating power symbols as neutral decoration.

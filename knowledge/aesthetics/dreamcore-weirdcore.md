### Visual evidence summary

- Dreamcore centers on dream-memory imagery: empty schools, malls, quiet playgrounds at night, foggy surreal landscapes, and blurred childhood references [DW1].
- Weirdcore leans harsher and stranger: distorted low-resolution pictures, no-context image juxtaposition, MS Paint / Photoshop edits, compression artifacts, and disorienting nostalgia [DW4].
- Both aesthetics borrow liminal spaces and familiar-but-off locations, but dreamcore is softer and wistful while weirdcore is more startling and disassociative [DW1][DW4].
- Contemporary circulation is heavily platform-driven: Tumblr image blogs, TikTok edits, Discord/YouTube communities, and derivative horror or AI-content ecosystems [DW1][DW2][DW3][DW4].

### Seven-dimension synthesis

- Palette: Dreamcore favors washed pastel pinks, baby blues, lavender, sunset peach, foggy greens, and faded childhood primaries; Weirdcore favors harsher digital hues, compression-muddied reds and cyans, dim greens, overblown flash color, and low-bit posterization [DW1][DW4].
- Type: Dreamcore text, when present, is sparse and dreamy; Weirdcore often uses cryptic captions, blunt lowercase phrases, or meme-like image text that heightens confusion [DW1][DW4].
- Texture: VHS noise, blur, jpeg artifacts, camera-phone grain, fog, glow, scanlines, primitive digital graphics, MS Paint roughness, and amateur compositing are core to both, with Weirdcore generally rougher [DW1][DW3][DW4].
- Shape: suburban roads, play structures, mall geometry, impossible horizon lines, cutout objects, crude symbols, and familiar architecture flattened into uncanny collage [DW1][DW4].
- Motion: slow float, looping drift, awkward zoom, sudden cut, or disjointed montage; movement should feel remembered or sleepwalking rather than designed for UI slickness [DW1][DW3].
- Spatial: liminal, depopulated, and strangely open; Dreamcore reads as a remembered dream-space, while Weirdcore often collapses normal context entirely and destabilizes orientation [DW1][DW4].
- Cultural markers: childhood nostalgia, anemoia, early-internet image culture, Tumblr/TikTok circulation, liminal-space hashtags, dream-logic, VHS melancholy, and surreal dread [DW1][DW2][DW3][DW4].

### Related-aesthetic boundaries

- Distinct from `liminal-space-backrooms`: dreamcore/weirdcore can use liminal environments, but they are not confined to plausible transition architecture and often prioritize emotional or surreal edit logic over architectural realism [DW1][DW4].
- Distinct from `glitch`: glitch foregrounds corruption as technical failure; weirdcore may include compression or distortion, but its goal is disorientation and familiarity-loss, not signal-corruption aesthetics alone [DW3][DW4].
- Distinct from `vaporwave`: both are nostalgic and internet-native, but vaporwave is more graphic, curated, and commercial-media-referential; dreamcore/weirdcore are more memory-fragmented and psychologically eerie [DW1][DW2].

## Analysis

### Branches and boundaries

Dreamcore foregrounds remembered, softened or emotionally displaced familiarity. YY Wang's miniature bedroom and impossible pool demonstrate a physical dream-memory branch. Weirdcore more often breaks context through crude juxtaposition, selective focus, uncanny substitutions and disconnected language; Yunbomu's mixed photo/graphic layers and Weirduuh's impossible object/sign collages demonstrate that grammar. These are useful internal contrasts, not a rigid pastel-versus-black split. The joint canonical identity is preserved.

### Claim-to-artifact map

- Mixed rendering and visible collage: YB-14, YB-11, YB-8, YB-3 and WD-96405561.
- Ordinary object made impossible: YY-CoolPool, WD-86860110, WD-86395212 and WD-78183768.
- Childhood-memory environment: YY-Yesterday and YY-Exhibit.
- Ambiguous caption/instruction and repetition: WD-96405569, WD-83519441 and WD-78385038.
- Contradiction to universal lo-fi digital medium: all three YY works; contradiction to universal pastel color: YB-4 and WD-95915597.
- Timing, easing, responsive behavior and UI interaction remain unknown from still sources.

### Context and attribution

[It's Nice That's Yunbomu interview](https://www.itsnicethat.com/articles/yunbomu-digital-art-discover-270426) documents the artist's Photoshop/Illustrator practice and mixing organic and inorganic imagery. Artist statements about healing or dream-sharing describe personal intent or experience; they do not establish medical benefit or general beliefs for a whole culture. The visual artist named Weirdcore is not automatically evidence for the internet genre with the same name. Generated sample images were not used as historical evidence.

### Interface translation

Use deterministic low-fi texture and mismatched image registers. Keep file selection, recovery and reset clear and keyboard accessible. Static artwork is a valid choice: stillness permits inspection of the contradiction without inventing a motion history. Any proposed exact colors, timing, font choices or breakpoints must be labeled implementation decisions rather than measured source values.

### Source bibliography / URLs

- [DW1] provides a concise internet-history framing of dreamcore: Tumblr roots, liminal-space inheritance, TikTok amplification, and contrast with weirdcore.
- [DW2] documents the wider internet taxonomy boom that made labels like weirdcore and pastel goth legible at scale.
- [DW3] is useful less for formal history than for contemporary adjacency: AI horror creators explicitly borrow weirdcore, dreamcore, liminal spaces, VHS, and nostalgic dread.
- [DW4] offers a fuller weirdcore description: low-quality early-internet visuals, amateur edits, anemoia, surreal context collapse, and distinction from dreamcore.

### Visual evidence summary

- Dreamcore centers on dream-memory imagery: empty schools, malls, quiet playgrounds at night, foggy surreal landscapes, and blurred childhood references [DW1].
- Weirdcore leans harsher and stranger: distorted low-resolution pictures, no-context image juxtaposition, MS Paint / Photoshop edits, compression artifacts, and disorienting nostalgia [DW4].
- Both aesthetics borrow liminal spaces and familiar-but-off locations, but dreamcore is softer and wistful while weirdcore is more startling and disassociative [DW1][DW4].
- Contemporary circulation is heavily platform-driven: Tumblr image blogs, TikTok edits, Discord/YouTube communities, and derivative horror or AI-content ecosystems [DW1][DW2][DW3][DW4].

### Seven-dimension synthesis

- Palette: Dreamcore favors washed pastel pinks, baby blues, lavender, sunset peach, foggy greens, and faded childhood primaries; Weirdcore favors harsher digital hues, compression-muddied reds and cyans, dim greens, overblown flash color, and low-bit posterization [DW1][DW4].
- Type: Dreamcore text, when present, is sparse and dreamy; Weirdcore often uses cryptic captions, blunt lowercase phrases, or meme-like image text that heightens confusion [DW1][DW4].
- Texture: VHS noise, blur, jpeg artifacts, camera-phone grain, fog, glow, scanlines, primitive digital graphics, MS Paint roughness, and amateur compositing are core to both, with Weirdcore generally rougher [DW1][DW3][DW4].
- Shape: suburban roads, play structures, mall geometry, impossible horizon lines, cutout objects, crude symbols, and familiar architecture flattened into uncanny collage [DW1][DW4].
- Motion: slow float, looping drift, awkward zoom, sudden cut, or disjointed montage; movement should feel remembered or sleepwalking rather than designed for UI slickness [DW1][DW3].
- Spatial: liminal, depopulated, and strangely open; Dreamcore reads as a remembered dream-space, while Weirdcore often collapses normal context entirely and destabilizes orientation [DW1][DW4].

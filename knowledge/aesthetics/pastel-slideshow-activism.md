# Pastel Slideshow Activism

## Scope and classification

A descriptive subset of social-media educational graphics: softly colored, mostly text-led cards turn an issue into a legible sequence of definitions, examples, actions and resources. The strongest cluster here is the 2020 Instagram guide. Some single-panel explainers use the same visual language; they are identified as variants rather than falsely described as carousels. A pastel quote, cosmetics advertisement or generic presentation template is insufficient. Activist graphics also include dark, saturated, photographic and monochrome systems outside this narrower entry.

The corpus supports a useful visual system, not one style shared by an entire movement. It is concentrated in English-language US/UK 2020 references and a Traditional Chinese community translation archive. The selected label is a catalog description, not a documented historical self-name or claim of invention.

## Source / Evidence Links

- [Eye on Design, Jennifer Rittner, July 2020](https://eyeondesign.aiga.org/black-lives-matter-movement-guides-are-changing-the-shape-and-longevity-of-protest-graphics/): creator interviews place explanatory carousels in a specific organizing/design context; editorial montages were leads, not full-original counts.
- [Vox, Terry Nguyen, August 2020](https://www.vox.com/the-goods/21359098/social-justice-slideshows-instagram-activism): contemporaneous reporting supplies original guide links and discusses the format’s persuasive and reductive possibilities. Its visual examples span more than pastel styling.
- [W Magazine, Maxine Wally, July 2020](https://www.wmagazine.com/story/the-graphic-designers-defining-the-aesthetics-of-black-lives-matter): interviews describe different designers’ approaches and resist a single movement-wide aesthetic. Original posts, including two monochrome Dom Roberts boundaries, were separately inspected.
- [Dumitrița Dumitrica and Hannah Hockin-Boyers](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/1369118X.2022.2155487): a primary scholarly case study discusses explanatory structure, sourcing and political subject formation. Its account-specific findings do not establish every carousel’s purpose or quality.
- [TaiwaneseAmerican.org bilingual resources, June 2020](https://www.taiwaneseamerican.org/2020/06/chinese-english-understanding-black-lives-matter-chinese-english-resources/): primary community publication credits original content and translators; six qualifying guides and six boundary guides were individually reviewed.
- [British Vogue’s authorized Harper reproduction](https://www.vogue.co.uk/arts-and-lifestyle/article/non-optical-ally-guide): complete selected 1080px slides, not the article’s small preview.
- [SURJ Historic Triangle resource archive](https://surj-ht.wixsite.com/historic-triangle/get-started/anti-racism-slides): preserves independently authored charts and multi-slide terminology resources; archive attribution remains distinct from independent authentication.
- [McGill course project](https://www.mcgill.ca/ahcs/files/ahcs/meet_at_the_why_-_a_curated_guide_to_digital_activism_for_mcgill_students.pdf): an authored November 2020 practice project documents its educational Instagram purpose. The whole PDF counts once, despite containing many posts.

## Analysis

Observed identity depends on explanatory hierarchy and reading sequence, not a serif-only font rule. Harper and Tembe show that the same peach ground can carry very different text structures: numbered explanation versus a two-column bibliography. Annika’s gradient guide combines expressive serif cover typography and an orderly directory. The Julia resource changes flat page colors while retaining a number/term/body system. The bilingual archive demonstrates that paired scripts, heavier term labels and small running credits can preserve the system while changing density considerably. Sophie provides a tall-condensed sans variant. Single-panel Danielle, Ewing and Utendahl explainers are related visual variants, not evidence that every artifact is a multi-page deck.

Soft visual treatment does not establish a message’s credibility or emotional effect. The observed Ewing PSA makes sourcing itself the subject; the scholarly study supplies context for how evidence and calls to action are structured in its cases. No engagement counts establish effectiveness here. The pastel subset should not stand in for the breadth of Black Lives Matter graphics: reviewed black/pink, white/red, navy/photo, saturated green and yellow works make that boundary explicit. Its 2020 prominence in the sources is not its proven invention date. The references do not prove that a named typeface, Canva template or one designer originated the language.

Sampled raster ground pixels at 3%/3% of six complete images were Harper/Tembe #ffe1c5, Carrie #f4dcdc, Sophie #f4b2be, bilingual guiding-principles #ced3ee and action guide #afc4c2. These are exact samples from specific compressed/adapted files, not universal tokens or original production swatches. Display sizes, margins and rhythm elsewhere are estimated visually. Proposed application grounds #f7dac9, #e5e3b5, #d6d3ec, #d5e6de and #fff9ef contrast with #252b29 at approximately 10.87:1, 10.98:1, 9.88:1, 11.13:1 and 13.77:1 respectively by sRGB WCAG calculation; those pairs alone do not certify the complete interface.

A manual guide reader with actual selectable text, visible numbered controls, a text-reading alternative, source disclosures and a local checklist is a contemporary UI translation. It should keep expressive title cards and small source strips while letting text reflow instead of shrinking a raster square. Proposed breakpoints and immediate page updates are implementation choices. A seed-sharing concept offers an original community subject without fabricating endorsements or republishing another community’s tragedy as decoration. Its gardening content must be separately sourced and its local demonstration state clearly described.

Self-review checked guide-versus-poster boundaries, single-panel variants, creator/translator attribution, underlying-work grouping, selected-page limits, source concentration and the unmet target. No independent reviewer is claimed. Medium-confidence history and cultural coverage remain caveated; no unresolved claim about timing or origin is promoted.

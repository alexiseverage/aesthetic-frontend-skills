## Dimension Synthesis

The following separates direct visual observations from proposed web translation. Color descriptions and relative geometry are estimated from visual inspection; no corpus-wide numerical sampling or historical hardware timing is claimed.

| Dimension | Synthesis / scope | Confidence and limitation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Palette | A bright, pastel, pearlescent or translucent shell surrounds a much quieter gray/green monochrome display. Black stepped characters coexist with colored printed backings and care-icon bands; color in a product photo does not prove a color LCD. Yellow/red/blue contrasts, glitter, white cases and dark outlines all occur. A single pink-and-mint palette is not mandatory. | High for the inspected visual sample |
| Type | Shell identities use playful lettering, scripts or compact functional legends; LCD text uses sparse bitmap capitals, numerals and pictograms. Screen type and package cartoons belong to different layers. Exact historical font families are unverified. Contemporary labels can use readable system sans-serif and tabular monospace without making all body copy tiny bitmap lettering. | Moderate for observed shell/screen treatment; exact font identities unverified |
| Texture | Molded glossy plastic, recessed glass, small seams, rim highlights, worn surfaces and printed motifs contrast with hard-edged display pixels. Pearl, metallic and glitter variants exist alongside opaque solids. Printed landscape/color backing must remain visually distinct from active dark pixels. A screen photo or manufacturer rendering cannot prove that a displayed character is powered rather than a sticker or insert. | High for the inspected visual sample |
| Shape | Egg and droplet silhouettes are common, with protruding round buttons and a keyring or loop. Rectangular brick housings, circular companions, molded wings, antennas and expanded pictorial controls contradict a mandatory egg or three-button rule. Pixel creatures need a clear stepped silhouette at a deliberate grid scale; avoid tracing branded characters or cases. | High for the inspected visual sample |
| Motion | Three actually played manufacturer Connection GIF sequences show discrete poses, feeding, displacement/wipes and a result caption. Their exported file cadence is not a measurement of original LCD refresh, sound or care schedules. Motion coverage is one program family; other device behavior remains unknown. A web translation may use immediate user-triggered pose changes or an optional pausable stepped idle loop. Reduced motion keeps a readable static pose and every care/status change; never blink critical information. | Moderate for one played manufacturer animation family; historical hardware timing and other programs remain unknown |
| Spatial | Shell perimeter, inset bezel, small character field and peripheral menu/status bands organize the interface. A few controls act on a selected operation rather than exposing a full dashboard. The character remains visually primary while status occupies compact named zones. Larger variants and four-button controls are valid exceptions. Preserve touch targets and readable status by scaling the object, not shrinking text to simulated hardware dimensions. | High for the inspected visual sample |
| Cultural markers | Pocket companionship, collectible shells, routine care, playful dependency, school-age toy culture and linked-device exchange are contextual references. Seasonal, monster, insect, human and licensed robot companions broaden the subject beyond domestic animals. Brand mascots, exact sounds, character silhouettes and proprietary care icons are not reusable generic decoration. | Medium; supporting context and variable examples |

## Direct Media Audit

**34 qualifying unique device/program artifacts: 33 still images and one animated program family**, individually inspected at accessible original/full-size resolution. Three native GIF sequences were actually played and grouped as one qualifying animated program; zero interactive websites; zero generated images count as historical evidence. The 10-artifact floor and 30-artifact working target are met. Exact source, attribution, observed resolution, content hash and dimension observations are in the [linked media ledger](../media-audits/lcd-virtual-pet-toys.json). Only analytical links and observations are published, not the reference files.

The broader discovery pool contained 1139 image leads, including repeated views, logos, thumbnails and unresolved candidates. These are not 1139 analyzed works. 42 records received full-media inspection; 5 were explicitly marked duplicate views, and 67 files were retrieved for analysis. Retrieval itself contributes zero to the qualifying count.

Sampling unit is a distinct device/program design, with shell colorways, states, angles and coupled devices grouped. Related Connection/Entama/Uratama generations remain close variants, identified separately by housing/antenna and program identity, not independent aesthetic origins. Thirty-three still artifacts and one animated Connection program family qualify. Twenty stills come from one collector compendium, eight manufacturer packages from GigaPets, four manufacturer product images from Bandai and one Nintendo diagram. Several originals are intentionally small; notes limit them to legible broad layout. Blank displays and possible printed inserts establish material/control organization but not powered pixels. Three played GIF sequences belong to one program, not three independent motion artifacts. Other motion routes were attempted: GigaPets native YouTube embeds returned error153 or no decoded data; its public video API returned no files; its only public GIF was a142×80 homepage intro, inadequate for LCD motion analysis; Bandai Original/Digimon pages yielded stills, and old R2-D2 microsite returned404. No bypass was attempted. Thus motion confidence is moderate for discrete illustrated poses in that program and low for historical timing, sounds or other hardware. Collection stops after target and broad silhouette/control counterexamples; it is not a comprehensive toy chronology.

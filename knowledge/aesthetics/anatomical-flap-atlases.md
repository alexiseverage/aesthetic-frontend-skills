# Anatomical Flap Atlases

A descriptive category for printed anatomical diagrams whose superimposed paper parts disclose related structures or alternate states through physical flaps. It includes single-sheet fugitive prints and bound teaching atlases. The distinguishing grammar is a registered diagram, a local hinge and a meaningful reveal; an antique anatomical image alone is insufficient. This is a material publishing tradition, not a single historical art movement or an assertion that its anatomy remains clinically authoritative.

## Sources and Context

- [Duke, Animated Anatomies: early flap anatomies](https://exhibits.library.duke.edu/exhibits/show/anatomy/intro/early) places early anatomical fugitive sheets in print history and stresses uncertainty about their audience and use. The [Duke research guide](https://guides.library.duke.edu/anatomicalflapbooks) broadens formats and periods. Do not assume every sheet was a medical student's textbook.
- [Jefferson Archives, anatomical flap books](https://library.jefferson.edu/archives/exhibits/flapbooks/) provides actual filmed handling from six source volumes: [Remmelin](https://library.jefferson.edu/archives/exhibits/flapbooks/cat.cfm), [Hellwig](https://library.jefferson.edu/archives/exhibits/flapbooks/nosce.cfm), [Witkowski](https://library.jefferson.edu/archives/exhibits/flapbooks/witkowski.cfm), [Spratt](https://library.jefferson.edu/archives/exhibits/flapbooks/obstet.cfm), [Cottage Physician](https://library.jefferson.edu/archives/exhibits/flapbooks/cottage.cfm) and [Dr Minder](https://library.jefferson.edu/archives/exhibits/flapbooks/dr_minder.cfm). Source catalogue dates and credits are retained, including Minder's uncertain 190- date. A named author/editor is not automatically the illustrator.
- [Wellcome, Ruelle sheets](https://wellcomecollection.org/works/u9kes8da) describes two sheets with mounted flaps and Latin explanatory text, catalogued [1539], with the lowest male flap missing. Its fourteen selected images are states of two designs, not fourteen independent works. [Rabaud's 1900 second edition](https://wellcomecollection.org/works/bfcw29ad) identifies four laminated plates; eight separately catalogued scans were reconciled into four artifacts. German diagram labels in a French volume do not establish Rabaud as their original artist.
- [Royal College of Physicians, More than words](https://history.rcp.ac.uk/exhibitions/body-knowledge/more-than-words) supplies an animated sequence and oblique photograph of Bartisch's 1583 eye diagram. Its modern English labels are explanatory overlays, not original sixteenth-century typography.
- [Duke's 2015 digitisation account](https://blogs.library.duke.edu/bitstreams/2015/04/16/a-look-under-the-hood-and-the-flaps-of-the-anatomical-fugitive-sheets-collection/) documents a particular digital reconstruction. Its interface transitions are not evidence for historical paper handling speeds, and the current interactive collection could not be inspected through the repository's verification screen.

These sources support provenance and publication context. The media observations below support appearance and local movement. Exact print processes beyond catalog descriptions, anonymous illustrators, fine text in small native video, and universal historical usage remain unknown.

## Direct Media Audit

34 full-media records represent 30 qualifying unique artifacts: 23 timed video demonstrations, six still plate/sheet artifacts and one animated/full-artifact diagram. Four additional Rabaud scan records are duplicate states. Related photographs and layer states remain grouped with their plate. No thumbnails contribute to the count; no reference media were downloaded or republished, and no generated assets are research evidence. Three independently useful collections are represented: Jefferson 23, Wellcome six and RCP one. The six Jefferson volumes supply broad mechanism evidence but create strong source and Western publishing concentration; thirty plate designs are not thirty unrelated historical traditions.

Each video was played through meaningful recorded intervals; timestamp ranges below describe actual inspected portions, not a claim to have watched every second. Complete native portrait files are 380×740 and landscape files 740×380, often with black bars. They reveal paper handling and composition, but do not support tiny-text transcription or fine print-process attribution. Wellcome originals reach 4878×6248. The native RCP GIF is 400×600 and was paired with its complete 1800×1200 oblique photograph. Its cycle duration was not measured.

Distinct numbered designs may count separately; repeated flap states, reframings and editions of the same design do not. Several Witkowski filmed segments contain more than one page and are conservatively one artifact each. Ruelle's two independently composed sheets are two artifacts, with all selected layers grouped. Hellwig's compositions belong to the Remmelin tradition and are not claimed as independent inventions. Their stripped irregular foldout presentations differ from the framed sheets. Spratt plates VI, VIII, IX and X have related section grammar but separately drawn positions, instruments and overlay relationships.

Working target 30 and floor 10 are met. Further available Spratt plates mostly repeat the established mechanism; the last batch added the important exception of alternate-state comparison. Collection stops with resolved defining traits rather than counting every layer. Duke interactive media were inaccessible at verification; Sabio's Wellcome pair had original-image connection failures after discovery and is excluded from the count. Uninspected Cincinnati/Columbia video leads are not evidence. No verification challenge was completed or circumvented.

## Analysis

### Claim map and boundaries

A local change within a stable field defines the category. AJ-Wit-0 and AW-R1 retain torso registration while different-sized components open; AR-Bartisch-eye and AJ-Wit-2 concentrate the same grammar within an eye. AJ-Cat-1/2 first demonstrate smaller peripheral reveals while leaving the large engraved figure still. The relationship is semantic rather than simply a decorative page-turn effect.

The flap may reveal depth, replace a state or compare a position. AJ-Spratt-X makes the third case explicit: the underlying figure changes while its enclosing frame remains aligned. AJ-Spratt-IIB/IVB and VII show that a page can combine interactive and static subfigures. A whole-page carousel that discards context loses this distinguishing relationship. A lift-up tab with unrelated promotional content is only a material analogy.

Monochrome woodcut/engraving, colored muscle diagrams, specialist cross-sections and household manikins form variants. AW-Ruelle-M/F establish dense text beside a seated figure; Catoptrum adds emblematic frames and many vignettes; Nosce isolates large standing foldouts; Spratt uses airier pages. The visual system is broader than sepia paper plus red organs. A copied stain, missing flap or curled corner is not a prerequisite.

Recorded motion is produced by a human handler. Flaps may turn up, down or sideways with blank paper backs, small shadows and selective occlusion; there is no universal duration or easing. The fixed print remains still. Modern video crossfades and the RCP still-sequence labels must not be passed off as properties of the original object. Closed/open still pairs establish layering and registration but do not independently measure a movement path.

These are historical educational artifacts. Anonymous artists, reused figure traditions and uncertain dates stay explicit. Original sexed poses and anatomical models reflect their publishing context; an interface should avoid converting them into claims about all contemporary bodies. The corpus supports a publishing/material aesthetic, not diagnosis, procedural instruction or medical efficacy.

### Frontend application (inferred)

A layered technical article or exhibit panel can pair an original simplified organ diagram with a numbered accordion. Opening a section reveals a registered layer and corresponding explanation, preserves the outline and offers reset. Use purposeful callouts, plate numbers and caption hierarchy; readable body text replaces miniature historical labels. A layer list is a useful information architecture even when no animation runs.

Design values are proposed: cream paper, dark blue-black rules, restrained coral/bone/blue accents, contemporary serif reading text and compact numeric labels. Draw original schematic material and identify it as an illustrative study rather than a clinical atlas. Do not trace historical medical diagrams while claiming new scientific authority. Include a textual account of every revealed structure.

Buttons/summary controls need programmatic expanded state, keyboard operation, clear focus and persistent text. No hover-only layers, drag-only mechanism or color-only legend. Mobile should stack the diagram and explanation while keeping the figure complete; long captions must wrap. A user may close or reset any layer. Reduced motion should switch layers immediately without perspective rotation; reading order and labels should remain stable. An optional short hinge transition is a contemporary design interpretation, not a historical measurement.

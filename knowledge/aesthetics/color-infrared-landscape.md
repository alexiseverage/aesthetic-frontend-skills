# Color-Infrared Landscape

## Scope and Source Context

A process-based photographic language: reflected near-infrared information produces selective false color while recognizable landscape geometry, texture and light remain intact. Analog color-infrared film, modified cameras and near-infrared sensor composites are related branches with different purposes and palettes. All false-color imagery, pink painting and thermal imaging do not belong indiscriminately.

[USGS](https://www.usgs.gov/faqs/what-do-different-colors-a-color-infrared-aerial-photograph-represent) explains conventional red-vegetation/dark-water color-infrared aerial interpretation. [NASA](https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/why-is-the-amazon-forest-red-83207/) explains a specific near-infrared-to-red Earth view. These scientific accounts do not make an artistic palette a plant-health measurement.

[Pettigiani](https://www.paolopettigiani.com/infraland) dates his converted-camera project from 2015; the New York and Dolomites series are dated 2016 and 2017. [Borowick](https://www.matthewborowick.com/infrared) describes an Aerochrome-inspired full-spectrum/filter practice. [Breindel](https://www.adosphotography.com/process/) discusses earlier film and later digital processing/channel remapping. These are first-person practice accounts, not calibration reports. Simplified optical explanations must not be repeated as universal physics; exact filters and film stocks cannot be assigned to individual images without metadata.

## Analysis

### Identity and Variants

The distinctive operation is selective spectral transformation within believable photographic space. Red forests and cyan lakes are prominent; documented yellow, blue and low-chroma variants prevent a compulsory magenta-only definition. Retain material-linked color separation with realistic texture and spatial continuity.

Film, modified-camera and satellite branches are related, not interchangeable methods. CSS hue rotation cannot reconstruct measured near-infrared data. Black-and-white infrared is adjacent; IR21/IR27 retain restrained chromatic separation. Thermal body maps, radar composites and arbitrary rainbow effects remain outside.

Psychedelic poster design has a separate lettering/ornament grammar. Solarpunk's regenerative future/social narrative is not established by spectral scenery. Cyanotype concerns blue contact-print chemistry/material, not near-infrared color separation. Those neighbors do not already own this independently usable treatment.

### Claim-to-Artifact Support

- Selective foliage contrast with retained neutral detail: IR01/IR02/IR06/IR07/IR12/IR13/IR17/IR24/IR28; process context from USGS/NASA and IR30 labels.
- Quiet water/sky/snow against fine foliage: IR03/IR05/IR07/IR09/IR10/IR13/IR28.
- Credible geometry/depth: IR02/IR06/IR08/IR14/IR16/IR18/IR20/IR22–IR26.
- Red is common, not universal: yellow IR14/IR22/IR25; blue/silver IR21/IR27/IR29. These exceptions changed the initial palette brief.
- Static medium: all counted works. Star/light trails IR12/IR15/IR18/IR20 show exposure effects, not animation.
- Process/cultural context comes from creator and scientific accounts, not colors alone.

### Proposed Values and Web Application

Visually estimated application palette, not calibrated wavelength samples: paper #F4F3EB, ink #153E44, coral #F1A39E, crimson #A92D48, pale cyan #D2EBE7, teal #0E626A. Use paper/ink for reading. Vivid imagery and small accents carry spectral color; validation must not rely on red/cyan alone.

A normal photo-essay/article card can pair heading/byline, wide photograph, prose/pull quote and a portrait detail. A save toggle and reading disclosure provide honest states. On mobile stack the composition, preserve useful focal regions and 44px controls. Neutral grotesk plus an italic serif is a proposed editorial pairing, not an infrared font.

Stillness suits the evidence. Optional 150ms button color changes are proposed UI behavior, removed under reduced motion. Generated fictional scenery must be identified; it is neither real sensor data nor an actual location. No unrelated before/after pair or fabricated measurement is justified.

### Confidence, Stop Decision and Gaps

The target is met with three independent photographic collections, scientific comparisons, different locations/seasons/orientations and meaningful non-red variants. Later batches reinforced retained material/space while refining the palette boundary. Additional images would not establish a universal font or animation system.

Artistic work dominates; historic film sheets and calibrated aerial surveys are underrepresented. Dates/filters remain incomplete. Colors are screen estimates, not calibrated samples. ESA detail is soft. These limits constrain technical/history claims without undermining the visible operation. No exhaustive history, universal ecological interpretation or independent review is claimed.

# Spirit Photography Seance

## Dimension Synthesis

These findings describe a bounded photographic corpus, not every spiritualist practice. Interface values are proposals; no exact historical colors, fonts or animation timings were measured.

| Dimension | Direct observation / source support | Variation and confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Palette | Pale yellow-brown fields, dense brown objects/clothing and light mounts recur in the Getty album. | Moderate across the wider aesthetic: 31 of 32 qualifying works come from one album. Hope's print has deeper shadows and a broader pale mount. Sepia alone is insufficient. |
| Type | Inspected fronts mostly contain images and mount rules rather than display typography. Hope's print includes a handwritten signature. | Low confidence for a historical type system. Serif imprints, archive numbers and plain labels are proposed translations, not identified source fonts. |
| Texture | Uneven fading, scuffed/chipped mounts, mottled fields and softened secondary exposures are visible. | Moderate. Aging, scan behavior and original process cannot be separated from pixels alone. Avoid uniform distressed-parchment overlays. |
| Shape | Rectangular mounts, softly rounded outer corners, fine rules, upright small photographs and dark tables organize many works. | Moderate beyond the album. Cabinet cards are not the only format. A small APS oval image remains an uncounted discovery lead. |
| Motion | All qualifying media are still photographs. | Historical timing, easing and process sequence unknown. Manual A/B states or registration controls are contemporary explanatory proposals. Stillness is appropriate. |
| Spatial | Dark solid foregrounds contrast with paler overlapping figures, behind, beside or crossing the foreground. Several works use a small portrait on a table instead of a living sitter. | High within the album, moderate across the category. No compulsory centered portrait or fixed image/text ratio is established. |
| Cultural markers | Institutional texts situate these works in spiritualism, bereavement, commercial portraiture and contested photographic evidence. | Preserve belief, manipulation and attribution uncertainty as distinct topics. Neither supernatural authentication nor generic Halloween decoration is warranted. |

## Direct Media Audit

42 reference versions inspected: 40 full-composition views and two small display-image leads. **32 qualifying unique still photographs**: Getty 31; Science Museum Group 1. Eight full-media boundaries and two small display copies are excluded. No confirmed duplicate versions count; one APS identity remains unresolved. No video, measured motion or live photographic process was inspected.

Getty, Science Museum Group, American Philosophical Society and Met records were consulted. The Met loan is a Getty work and adds no independent count. Eastman's collection route rejected access; Science Museum's metadata API rejected direct retrieval, although the complete image was accessible. Reference files are not included. Getty's images are CC0; other rights are retained or unresolved and do not authorize production reuse.

The 30-work target is met, but the corpus is strongly weighted toward one American album and attribution to Mumler, sometimes alternatively to Helen F. Stuart. British, French and later séance practices remain underrepresented. This concentration limits broader claims even though the count passes.

Historical type confidence is low; historical motion and precise exposure parameters remain unknown.

### Frontend translation

Mounts, negative sleeves, plate IDs, exposure comparisons and an observation register can make the medium legible. An object-only reconstruction can explain layering without historical sitters, but must be labeled a contemporary fictional study. Do not present an empty-room drawing as an authentic historical spirit photograph or redefine the portrait tradition to fit an interface constraint.

Keep text on quiet opaque paper. Serif/monospace pairings, numeric registration offsets and immediate view changes are proposed interface choices. Still sources establish no mandatory dissolve duration. Comparison can remain still; drifting mist and supernatural verification claims are unnecessary.

## Connections

Gothic photography can share old materials without an exposure/evidence structure. Hauntology concerns mediated memory and unrealized futures; this category concerns contested photographic records. Halloween collage does not become spirit photography through sepia or fog. Object-exposure studies are contemporary explanatory applications, not replacements for the historical portrait tradition.

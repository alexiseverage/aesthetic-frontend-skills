# Snow Crystal Photomicrography

## Scope and classification

A descriptive photographic/specimen visual tradition, with separate historical monochrome and modern illuminated branches. Distinct from the existing Polarized Crystal Photomicrography entry: ice morphology, isolated specimens and typological observation are central, whereas polarized fields may have many materials and non-radial crystalline regions. Libbrecht’s gallery uses colored illumination; visual rainbow edges alone establish no polarization method. The sampled specimens include plates, lobed stars, dendrites, capped columns and a multi-crystal field; not every snow crystal is a perfect six-armed star.

## Source / Evidence Links

- [Libbrecht’s natural-crystal gallery](https://www.snowcrystals.com/photos/photos.html): primary photographer’s original linked images, explicitly described as naturally fallen crystals. The page attributes the color to colored lighting. Sixteen complete published originals were inspected, including two deliberately close compositions; no thumbnails count.
- [Libbrecht’s snow science](https://www.snowcrystals.com/science/science.html): primary scientific explanation distinguishes crystals/aggregates, relates sixfold organization to ice structure and cautions that irregular crystals are common. Selected photogenic specimens do not prove a population distribution, universal perfect symmetry or an absolute uniqueness claim.
- [Smithsonian, 15 Bentley images](https://www.smithsonianmag.com/smithsonian-institution/these-15-stunning-microscopic-snowflake-images-helped-change-the-way-we-see-the-world-180987978/): fourteen individually identified original scans were inspected from the publisher’s source URLs embedded in its image links; the 1072px transformed display copies were not counted separately. The circa1890 attribution is the publisher’s, not a newly authenticated capture date.
- [Met286768](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/286768): institutional context describes Bentley’s typological photographic practice and a ca1910 gelatin-silver print. Current API metadata exposes no primary image and reports isPublicDomain:false; the page restricts enlargement/download. That object is not counted and no restriction was bypassed.
- [Met2015.152 / DP351880 on Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:-Snow_Crystal-_MET_DP351880.jpg): a separate complete original scan supplies a warm-toned plate/print comparison. The Commons life-plus notice does not by itself settle every reuse condition; no reference file is republished here.
- [Linda Hall Library, Bentley and the 1931 book](https://www.lindahall.org/about/news/scientist-of-the-day/wilson-alwyn-bentley/): institutional publication context. The viewed title page and plate110 display copies support modest serif hierarchy, fine rules, typological array and bracketed numbering; they contribute zero qualifying specimens. A grid of twelve tiny pictures was not promoted into twelve inspections.

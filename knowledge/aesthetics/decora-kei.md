# Decora Kei

> **Origin**: Harajuku street-fashion subculture translated into interface language through accessory stacking, toy-plastic tactility, and joyful decorated density around readable content islands.

## Analysis

_Analyzed: 2026-06-16 | Images reviewed: 0 | Analyst: implementation brief synthesis_

Decora Kei's differentiator is accessory stacking as identity performance. Subculture writing and fashion scholarship describe Harajuku decora as layered, colorful, DIY/self-styling expression; the frontend translation must preserve stacking and material toy/accessory cues.

### Color

Candy-bright color is necessary but not sufficient. A neutral or white utility anchor keeps high-density decoration usable.

### Typography

Rounded display/caption type should feel like stickers or charms, with cleaner rounded sans for functional copy.

### Texture

Glossy plastic, foam, beads, puffy vinyl, and felt cues distinguish decora-kei from generic cute themes.

### Motion

Bounces, pops, confetti, and attach motions fit the accessory metaphor; reduced-motion should keep static layered decoration.

### Layout

Layer decoration at edges and around modules, while preserving a readable center of gravity for navigation and core content.

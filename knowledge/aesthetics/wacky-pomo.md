# Wacky Pomo

Classification: **separately useful canonical visual system**, related to the neighbors below. A retrospective commercial cluster of warped signs, cartoon apparatus and exuberant dimensional lettering across children’s television, packaging, retail and early software. The transferable system is playful construction: uneven contours, exaggerated depth and clear activity zones. It is more specific than bright colors or a Memphis squiggle.

## Analysis

Cassette Futurism is application-ready because its signal is distributed across the seven-dimension framework rather than resting on palette alone. The most important implementation guardrail is to preserve the combined palette, material, shape, motion, spatial, and cultural-marker system; if only one cue is present, the result will read as generic speculative technology.

For frontend translation, begin with the spatial and shape grammar, then layer palette, typography, texture, and motion. Keep critical content on solid readable surfaces when glow, grain, scanlines, particle fields, or dense machine texture would reduce accessibility.

## Connections

Adjacent to atomic-age but less optimistic; adjacent to cyberpunk but less urban/body-mod centered; distinct from high-performance HMI because it is diegetic retrofiction.

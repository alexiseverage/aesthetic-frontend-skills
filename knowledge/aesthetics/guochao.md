# Guochao

> **Origin**: Contemporary Chinese heritage-branding and China-chic commerce movement. The frontend translation is present-tense cultural confidence: premium product clarity integrated with script-aware typography and selective heritage ornament.

## Analysis

_Analyzed: 2026-06-16 | Images reviewed: 0 | Analyst: implementation brief synthesis_

Guochao is documented in marketing and cultural press as a contemporary Chinese heritage-branding movement. The sources position it as modern cultural confidence in commerce and product design, not generic historic decoration.

### Color

Use red, gold, jade, porcelain, and ink as a disciplined premium system. Avoid flooding the UI with red/gold without hierarchy.

### Typography

Script-aware typography is a non-negotiable. Calligraphic and seal-script accents should be structural and restrained.

### Texture

Foil, lacquer, paper, fabric, and ink-wash references should support product ceremony rather than become all-over ornament.

### Motion

Use ceremonial reveals, stamp impacts, and scroll-like procession. Hyperactive motion reads as the wrong subculture.

### Layout

Center-weighted commerce heroes, framed story panels, product shrines, and disciplined grids maintain premium clarity.

## Connections

- **`maximalism`** — Guochao uses structured ceremonial ornament, not abundance for its own sake.
- **`art-deco`** — both can signal luxury, but Guochao's grammar is Chinese heritage and contemporary commerce.
- **`city-pop`** — city-pop is retro Japanese leisure nostalgia; Guochao is current Chinese identity branding.
